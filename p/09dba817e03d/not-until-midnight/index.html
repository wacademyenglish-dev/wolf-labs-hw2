Not Until Midnight · C1 Unit 4 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on inversion after negative expressions of time and place: Not until midnight did they arrive; Not until I saw it did I believe it; Not once did we stop; Not since 1990 has it snowed; Nowhere have I seen such stars.

The story: A hiker (invented) tells the story of a night lost in the fog on a mountain, and the rescue at midnight.

About 48 min: I · The Night on the Mountain (10) · II · Fact or Story? (9) · III · Make It Dramatic (8) · IV · On the Mountain (7) · V · The Story in Order (7) · VI · Your Story (7).

Open with: “Have you ever been lost? What did you feel when you finally got home?”

Inversion feels artificial to students. Present it as a storyteller’s tool: it makes the listener wait. Read the story with drama. The key trap: with Not until + a clause, the inversion goes in the SECOND part. Tell the student it is natural in stories, speeches and writing, and that “It wasn’t until… that…” is the everyday alternative.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Not until + time: Not until midnight did… · Not until + clause: Not until I saw it did I… · Not once did… · Not since … has / had… · Nowhere have I… · the everyday option: It wasn’t until… that…

THE NIGHT ON THE MOUNTAIN

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

FACT OR STORY?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

MAKE IT DRAMATIC

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

THE STORY IN ORDER

The events are mixed. The student taps them in order; wrong order shows in red. When it’s right, the student tells the whole story with first, then, after that, finally.

YOUR STORY

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Not until midnight they arrived, Not until did I see it I understood, Not once we stopped, Nowhere I have seen, Not since 2010 it has rained, Not until midnight arrived they. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

ON THE MOUNTAIN (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the transformations, your student is on the mountain and makes three decisions: the fog at 4 p.m., the phone with 10% battery, and a night hike a month later. There is no right answer: let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Push for one inversion per answer (Not until I’m safe will I…, Never would I…). Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · The Night on the Mountain.
  • Focus: II · Fact or Story? and III · Make It Dramatic.
  • Out loud: IV · On the Mountain, V · The Story in Order and VI · Your Story.
