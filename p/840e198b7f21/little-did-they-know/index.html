Little Did They Know · C1 Unit 4 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on inversion after negative expressions of manner and rule: Little did they know…, At no time did…, Never before had…, In no way was…, Under no circumstances should…, On no account must…

The story: The audio guide of a lighthouse museum (invented): in 1911 the keeper Thomas Bell saved six fishermen in a storm. At the end, the museum’s safety sign.

About 42 min: I · The Audio Guide (10) · II · Story or Sign? (9) · III · The Loneliest Jobs (8) · IV · Find the Mistakes (7) · V · The Hot Seat (8).

Open with: “What do you imagine the life of a lighthouse keeper was like, a hundred years ago?”

Inversion lives in two places: stories with a twist and very strict rules. The audio guide gives both in one text. Read it as a museum guide. The sign with mistakes makes the student fix the order; the hot seat lets the student hear YOU use it about your own life.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Little did they know… · At no time did… · Never before had… · In no way was… · Under no circumstances should… · On no account must…

THE AUDIO GUIDE

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

STORY OR SIGN?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Little they knew, Under no circumstances you should, At no time he said, Never before I had seen, On no account visitors must, In no way it was easy. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE LONELIEST JOBS (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the rule, your student ranks five lonely jobs, from the one they would never do to the one they might enjoy, and explains the top and the last with inversion (Under no circumstances would I…, In no way could I…). There is no right order. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it). Tap Show my teacher’s order and agree on one top 3 together. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · The Audio Guide.
  • Focus: II · Story or Sign? and IV · Find the Mistakes.
  • Out loud: III · The Loneliest Jobs and V · The Hot Seat.
