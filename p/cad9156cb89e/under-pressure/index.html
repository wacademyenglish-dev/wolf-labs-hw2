Under Pressure · The Attention Target

The student already knows what happened to them under pressure. What they can’t do is explain it, because the three links of an explanation get mixed up: because / because of (cause), leads to / results in (effect), to / in order to / so that (purpose). On screen, an attention target with three rings: the center is the body, the middle ring is the task, the outer ring is the room. A point of light shows where attention is. And a chain of three links: cause, effect, and the purpose that opens the valve.

DON’T CORRECT SCENE I

The student picks one arena and keeps it all hour. The first answers will contain because of I was nervous and for not fail. Write them down; they come back at the end.

THE STUDY

Scene II: students with the best memory for numbers lost the most under pressure. The recordings describe real findings; they don’t quote anybody. Most students guess the opposite: let them guess wrong out loud.

THE PROFILE IS A MOMENT

Fourteen questions (scene III) give two numbers (scene VI): the Worrier (attention leaves for the room) and the Watcher (attention goes into the body). The remedies are opposite. If the student uses it as a label for who they are, bring it back to the arena.

THE RECORDINGS

Four recordings with pause and 0.75×: the lab (II), the golfer and the speaker (VIII, they are meant to sound alike), the coach (XI, only after the six markers).

THE CHAIN

Scene IX: the parts come shuffled; the student taps cause, effect, purpose in order, and says the whole sentence before the last link. If you’re behind, cut chains from IX and rounds from VII. Never cut I, V, IX or XII.

YOU’RE THE COACH (IX) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the two cases, your student becomes the coach and decides what to do in three situations: a friend the night before an interview, a penalty in a final, a presentation in English. There is no right answer. Let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast quietly while they talk (because of + a thing, leads to -ing, in order not to); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Your Arena, III Fourteen Questions, VI The Profile, IX You’re the Coach, X Your Own Chain.
  • Listen: II The Lab, VIII Two Cases, XI The Report.
  • Focus: IV Because, or Because Of?, V The Three Links, VII To, For, or So That?
  • Write: XII, their arena in one chain. It goes to their My notes.
