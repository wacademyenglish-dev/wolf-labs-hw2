The Lost and Found · A1 Unit 2 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 2 (Things and people). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student works at the lost and found desk of a train station tonight. You are the three owners.

About 46 min: I · Bingo (8) · II · The Lost and Found (15) · III · Your Lost Thing (5) · IV · Find the Mistakes (7) · V · Would You Rather? (7) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Pick a door.” There is no order. One minute per door, no notes.

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

a / an by the sound · size + color + thing (a small black bag) · but × and · the plural (keys, boxes, children, people) · this / that / these / those · describing people

BINGO

A 3×3 card of quick tasks. The student picks a square and does it out loud; tap Done. Three in a line (across, down or diagonal) is a BINGO. Keep going for a second line if there is time.

THE LOST AND FOUND

You are each owner. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the ticket fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. After three owners, each one comes back: the student gives the things back with Is this your…? / Are these your…?. Listen for the three slips on your screen (a umbrella, a umbrella black, this keys) and tap the one you heard.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

WOULD YOU RATHER?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

a umbrella, a bag black, this keys, childrens, peoples, more cheap. A1: one correction per answer is enough.

YOUR LOST THING (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the owners get their things back, the student talks about THEIR things: choose one of three (the worst thing to lose; a thank-you gift), with an almost ready sentence (a small black…, these…). It is a different format from the Would You Rather later. Answer first yourself, as a model. Then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and ask the PUSH line (only you see it): a yes / no question. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves. About 5 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. There are three; use one when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Bingo and IV · Find the Mistakes and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Lost and Found, III · Your Lost Thing and V · Would You Rather?.
