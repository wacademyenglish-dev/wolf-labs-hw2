Three Out of Three · The Spotlight

The passive puts the thing the action happens to at the front: be + third form. By … only when the doer matters. Some verbs never take it: happen, come, go, arrive, die. Students say was stole, it was did, it was happened. The story: a woman with no amygdala felt no fear of snakes or horror movies, but in 2013, one breath of CO₂ gave her the first panic attack of her life. On screen, a dark stage: in the passive, the object card steps into the spotlight at the front, the verb becomes a lamp (was + third form), and the doer goes to the wings, with a BY tag, or disappears.

NOT ABOUT THE STUDENT’S FEAR

Every question is about the woman, the study or the recordings. If the student shares something personal, listen, then go back. Scene IX is a finding about other people, not advice.

THREE PATIENTS

Say the sample size out loud: three patients and twelve healthy people. The finding moved a conclusion; it didn’t replace it with a certainty.

THE SPOTLIGHT

Scene III: two taps. First the verb (be + third form); then KEEP or DROP the doer. Sentence 1 accepts both: drop it if the cause is known, keep it if the cause is the news.

THE RECORDINGS

Three recordings, each with pause and 0.75×; questions and transcripts open after listening: the neurologist (V), the 2013 researcher (VII), and the “I am excited” study (IX). In V, line 2 is the trap: came.

WHAT WOULD YOU DO? (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Scene VIII is now three light dilemmas about courage (a TV night in a “haunted” house, a talk on stage, a skydive that has already been paid) and a last word: if their fear could be switched off for a year, like the woman in the lesson, would they do it? There is no right answer. Let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast mistakes quietly (“was stole” → “was stolen”); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
