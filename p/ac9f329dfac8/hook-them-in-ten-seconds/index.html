Hook Them in Ten Seconds · C1 Unit 10 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on openings: the rhetorical question (Have you ever wondered why…?), the surprising fact (Did you know that…?), Imagine…, the mini-story (Last year, I…), Raise your hand if…, and the promise (By the end of this talk, you’ll…). Plus what not to do: name first, apologizing, “I’m nervous”.

The story: A speaking contest (invented): three speakers open the same talk about sleep in three very different ways.

About 44 min: I · Three Openings (10) · II · Which One Hooks You? (9) · III · Fix the Opening (8) · IV · The Tough Room (8) · V · Your First Ten Seconds (9).

Open with: “Think of a talk or a class you loved. How did it start?”

This is the first lesson of the unit that ends with the student’s own talk. Today is only the first ten seconds. Read the three openings like a speaker; Speaker 1 should sound flat on purpose. Grammar watch: “wondered why do we” (indirect question) and Imagine + present. The writing card is the opening of the student’s real talk: keep it for the final lesson.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Have you ever wondered why…? (no do) · Did you know that…? · Imagine… (present) · Raise your hand if… · Last year, I… Here’s what changed. · By the end of this talk, you’ll…

THREE OPENINGS

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

WHICH ONE HOOKS YOU?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

FIX THE OPENING

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

BINGO

A 3×3 card of quick tasks. The student picks a square and does it out loud; tap Done. Three in a line (across, down or diagonal) is a BINGO. Keep going for a second line if there is time.

YOUR FIRST TEN SECONDS

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

my name is… as the first sentence, sorry for my English, have you ever wondered why do we, imagine that you will be, today I will talk about… (flat), I’m very nervous (don’t say it). C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE TOUGH ROOM (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

This scene replaces the Bingo. Your student’s talk on sleep is at 9 AM, for two hundred tired engineers: they put five hooks from best to worst for this audience, then defend their first and their last. There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence while they order, then use the PUSH line (only you see it): the audience changes, does the order change? Then tap Show my teacher’s order and agree on one top 3 together. Recast quietly while they talk (watch for why do we after “Have you ever wondered”); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · Three Openings.
  • Focus: II · Which One Hooks You? and III · Fix the Opening.
  • Out loud: IV · The Tough Room.
  • Write: V · Your First Ten Seconds.
