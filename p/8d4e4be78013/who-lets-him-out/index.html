Who Lets Him Out? · Jujutsu Kaisen · The Domain

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: A strong boy, a cursed finger, and a death sentence that waits.

The grammar: Make, Let, Be Made To: They made him eat it. They let him live. He was made to fight.

The class (about 56 min): I Warm-up and The Sorcerer Rules · II The Trailer · III Scene by Scene · IV The Arena · V Make, Let, Be Made To · VI Domain Expansion · VII Your Rules · VIII The Hot Seat. No extra scenes: every scene is part of the class.

Open with: “You know Yuji now. In the trailer, what do the sorcerers make him do? Guess.”

Jujutsu Kaisen (2020) is the Japanese anime from Gege Akutami’s manga. In this world, curses are born from people’s fears and anger. Yuji Itadori, a strong high school student, swallows a cursed finger of Sukuna, the King of Curses, to save his friends; now Sukuna lives inside him. The sorcerers want to execute him, but his teacher Gojo lets him live and makes him a student at Jujutsu High. The grammar: make / let + person + base verb (they made him eat it, Gojo let him live), the passive be made to (he was made to train: the to comes back), and be allowed to (let has no passive). The Portuguese trap: deixar = let, not leave.

A note for the teacher: the trailer has Japanese audio with English subtitles in the picture; it has fights and monsters, but nothing graphic is on the lesson screens. The lines were read from the picture, frame by frame (26/09), and each moment is told in our own words. This lesson shares its grammar with the WOLFSTATION B1 Onimusha lesson (a cross-line reinforcement); no scene is repeated.

Warm-up and The Sorcerer Rules (I): two parts. 1 · Warm-up: three questions about the student’s life, one at a time (big on the screen, with “Ask more” under each and one short line about the anime under the photo). Let them talk; don’t correct the grammar yet. 2 · The Sorcerer Rules: four rules of this world appear one at a time (curses come from fear, only sorcerers can see them, the old sorcerers make the rules, Yuji is made to eat the fingers). For each, the student taps Yes / No way / Only if… and says why, with make, let or be allowed to.

The ending (VIII, The Hot Seat): the student is Yuji at Jujutsu High; the teacher asks five questions with make, let, was made to and allowed to. Then swap. Speaking only.

ON SCREEN

The cover is a lonely shrine gate under a violet vortex. I: Warm-up and The Sorcerer Rules. II: the trailer in three steps. Before: a 30-second clock: the student talks about who made the rules at home. Watch: five sentences with a missing word, each with a ▶ time that jumps to that line of the trailer; the student taps the word that fits. After: match who makes and who lets (Fear makes curses grow, Gojo lets Yuji stay…), then say the sentence. III: five frames on the film strip. V, Make, Let, Be Made To: five sentences, each with a wrong part (made him to study, was made fight, was let to, leave me go, feeling). The student says the fixes, taps Check, and the rule board appears; it stays in the corner on the next pages. VI, Domain Expansion (Carlos’s idea, 25/09): the student is trapped inside a domain, a ring of six barrier plates around the center. For each situation the student taps the meaning (forced or allowed, was made to or weren’t allowed to…), the plate cracks with lines of light, and the student says the sentence. With six, the barrier falls and the center turns to sky. VII, Your Rules: six cards (your parents, your school, your boss, your friends, your city, you). Open one, tell the teacher, and tap each word (made me, let me, was made to, wasn’t allowed to, makes me feel) when you use it. VIII: the hot seat.

THE COMMON ERRORS

made him to eat, made him ate, let me to go, leave me go (let me go), was made train (was made to train), was let to live (was allowed to), allowed leave, makes me feeling, let you using, make him changes.

THE ARENA (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the trailer moments, your student takes a side: Gojo lets his students take big risks. Is that a good teacher? There is no right answer. Let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then start the one-minute clock; when they finish, tap Swap sides and they defend the other side. Use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Recast make / let + verb quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Warm-up and The Sorcerer Rules, IV The Arena, VI Domain Expansion, VII Your Rules and VIII The Hot Seat.
  • Listen: II The Trailer and III Scene by Scene.
  • Focus: V Make, Let, Be Made To.
  • Write: the homework (the lesson ends speaking).
