We’re Going to Start in March · Selling Services · Lesson 13

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 13 (going to: plans). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Leo sends the project plan to Tom Reed, project manager of Brava Foods (fictional), and then explains it on a call: kick-off, process map, TOTVS integration, test payrolls, go-live. The student puts a plan in order and writes a four-step plan for a real client.

About 58 min: I · Your Week (6) · II · The Plan E-mail (7) · III · “So, What’s the Plan?” (9) · IV · Going To, Not “Go To” (10) · V · Put the Plan in Order (8) · VI · You Choose (6) · VII · Your 4-Step Plan (9) · VIII · I Can (3).

Open with: “What are you going to do this week? Are you going to travel this month?”

Brazilians translate “vamos começar” word by word: “we go to start”. Other classic errors: no “to” (“we are going start”) and the past after going to (“going to started”). Three checks: be? going TO? simple verb? In the plan, ask for real months and dates, and make the student say it out loud like a presentation: First… Then… In April… The go-live is going to be on…

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

We’re going to start in … · First, … Then, … In April, … · We aren’t going to … · Are you going to …? Is she going to …?

YOUR WEEK

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

THE PLAN E-MAIL

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

“SO, WHAT’S THE PLAN?”

A short audio dialogue (American voices). BEFORE: one question. LISTEN: play it once without stopping (0.75× is fine for A1), then the questions; Show answer after the student tries. The transcript opens only after the audio ends: read it together and point to the highlighted words.

GOING TO, NOT “GO TO”

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

PUT THE PLAN IN ORDER

The events are mixed. The student taps them in order; wrong order shows in red. When it’s right, the student tells the whole story with first, then, after that, finally.

YOU CHOOSE

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

YOUR 4-STEP PLAN

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. If the student has a real project, use his four steps in the real proposal (the timeline page).

THE TECH WORDS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno vende serviços B2B. Você não precisa ser especialista; precisa saber do que ele está falando. Não ensine estes termos: deixe que ele explique o trabalho dele (é conversa real).

  • BPO (Business Process Outsourcing): Terceirização de processos: a empresa contratada EXECUTA uma rotina do cliente (folha de pagamento, contabilidade, fiscal, contas a pagar). O cliente para de fazer aquilo dentro de casa.
  • BTO (Business Transformation Outsourcing): Terceirização com transformação: além de executar, a contratada MUDA o processo (tecnologia, automação, integração de sistemas). Vende-se como “melhorar”, não só “fazer por você”.
  • ERP: Sistema de gestão que integra finanças, compras, estoque, RH etc. Exemplos (marcas): SAP, Oracle, TOTVS. Um BPO/BTO não vende o ERP: trabalha COM ele ou o integra.
  • Kick-off (meeting): Reunião de abertura do projeto: apresenta as equipes, o escopo e o cronograma.
  • Go-live: O dia em que o novo processo/sistema “entra no ar” e passa a valer de verdade (ex.: a primeira folha rodada pelo BPO).
  • Implementation plan · timeline: Plano de implantação · cronograma: as etapas e datas entre a assinatura e o go-live.
  • Process mapping (map the process): Mapeamento do processo: desenhar passo a passo como o cliente faz hoje (quem faz o quê, com qual sistema).
  • Parallel run (test payroll): Rodada paralela/teste: rodar a folha no sistema novo e no antigo ao mesmo tempo e comparar, antes do go-live.
  • Project manager (PM): Gerente de projeto: coordena prazos e pessoas. Do lado do cliente, costuma ser o contato do dia a dia.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

We go to start in March (We’re going to start), We are going start (We are going to start), It’s going to started (It’s going to start), You going to come? (Are you going to come?). A1+: one correction per answer is enough.
