Such a Small Room · The Night Blueprint

So + adjective: The room is so small. Such a + adjective + noun: It’s such a small room. No A with plural or uncountable nouns: such loud music, such noisy people. And that opens the result: such a long night that we saw the sun come up. The choice isn’t about meaning; it’s about what comes after. Students say so small room and such small. The story: Room 34, a tiny hotel room next to a party. The screen is the room’s floor plan at night; each correct complaint lights up a problem in its place.

THE ONE QUESTION

Is there a noun after the adjective? Yes: SUCH (A). No: SO. Let your student find it in scene III (the noun comes back at step three), then use it.

THE RECORDINGS

Twenty-two short recordings: two versions of each fact (II), four versions of the same room (V), and six sentences with THAT (VII). One guest says “queue”: British for line. Each plays with pause and 0.75×; the words appear only after listening.

YOU ARE THE FRONT DESK

Scene VIII: tap the complaint you heard, answer as the night manager, offer something small, and let your student push. Then swap roles.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

so small room, such small, such a loud music, such a noisy people, such a ugly view, such a long night so…

THE HOT SEAT (IX) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Scene IX is now a hot seat, and your student switches sides: in VIII they complained as Room 34; now they threw the party in Room 35. You are the hotel manager with a 200-dollar noise fine (your secret: you forget the fine if the apology is really good). Ask the four questions; they answer in the role with so, such or that. Wait five seconds in silence before you accept anything, and use the PUSH line (only you see it). Recast mistakes quietly (“so small room” → “such a small room”); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I A Place You Stayed, III One Step Up, VI Room 34, VIII Call the Front Desk, IX The Hot Seat.
  • Listen: II Whose Version Is Bigger?, V Four Versions, VII That Is Why.
  • Focus: IV What Comes After?
  • Write: X, their own review. It goes to their My notes.
