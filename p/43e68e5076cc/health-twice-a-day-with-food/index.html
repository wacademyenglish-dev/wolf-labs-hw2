Twice a Day With Food · English for Healthcare · Lesson 7 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: medication counseling: questions before (allergies, conditions, other medicines, supplements), then dose, frequency (once / twice / three times a day, every 8 hours, at bedtime, as needed, up to), food (with food, on an empty stomach), what to avoid, side effects, when to get help (should, shouldn’t, must not), and teach-back.

The story: A PharmAchieve practice station (Canadian pharmacy exam style): a pharmacist counsels a patient with a new prescription for pulmonary hypertension. Then labels turned into plain English and five instructions to interpret.

About 34 min: I · A New Prescription (11) · II · Label to Plain English (10) · III · Read the Instructions (9) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “How do you say ‘de 8 em 8 horas’ in English?” (every eight hours / three times a day).

The video shows 0:23–5:00. It mentions that the active ingredient is also used for erectile dysfunction: a real counseling moment (the patient asks if it is OK for women). The labels in the exercises are generic examples, not medical advice.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

twice a day / every 8 hours · with food / on an empty stomach · as needed, up to… · You should avoid… · You must not take more than… · Can you tell me how you’re going to take it?

A NEW PRESCRIPTION

PharmAchieve, “PEBC OSCE Practice Station - Patient picking up new prescription”, only 0:23–5:00 (captions checked on 15/10/2026).

LABEL TO PLAIN ENGLISH

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

READ THE INSTRUCTIONS

Listen or read, then choose one option. The colors show right and wrong, and one line says why. Ask the student WHY before you move on.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to write the counseling script for the medicine they explain most.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

two times for day (twice a day), take with the stomach empty (on an empty stomach), remedy (medicine / medication), it can give sleep (it may make you sleepy). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: II · Label to Plain English.
  • Focus: III · Read the Instructions and IV · I Can.
