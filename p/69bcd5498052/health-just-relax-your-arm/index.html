Just Relax Your Arm · English for Healthcare · Lesson 5 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: gentle instructions during an examination or procedure: Could you…? · for me · just · gently · a little · Try to keep still · This might feel cold · Let me know if…, and I’ll stop. Examples from dentistry, medicine, nursing, physiotherapy and pharmacy.

The story: No story: a practice lesson. Pairs of bossy vs gentle instructions, eight instructions by profession, and a 60-second speed round.

About 33 min: I · Bossy or Gentle? (10) · II · By Profession (12) · III · Speed Round (7) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: Say “Open your mouth!” in a hard voice, then “Could you open wide for me?” softly. Ask: “Which dentist do you want?”

Imperatives are not rude in themselves, but in clinical English the softened forms are the norm and patients notice the difference. In the speed round, adapt the prompts to the student’s profession.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Could you… for me? · I’m just going to… · gently / a little · Try to keep still. · This might feel a bit cold. · Let me know if…, and I’ll stop.

BOSSY OR GENTLE?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

BY PROFESSION

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

SPEED ROUND

A 60-second speed round. Tap Start the clock, then Right or Pass for each item; the answer is on your screen. At the end of class, play again and beat the score.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to list the 10 instructions they give most often at work, in the gentle form.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Breathe! Don’t move! (bare orders), Make a big open (open wide), Stay quiet (keep still), touching without warning. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Bossy or Gentle? and IV · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · By Profession and III · Speed Round.
