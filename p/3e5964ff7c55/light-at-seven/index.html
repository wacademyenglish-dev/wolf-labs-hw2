The Light at Seven · Windows at Night

The student just moved in. From the kitchen window they see a building across the courtyard, and one window that turns on at seven every night. The screen is that building: every time the student says a neighbor’s routine with the -s, a window lights up. On Friday the window stays dark; on Saturday there are two cups. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

Present simple, third person: he works, she reads, it opens. One S only: a plural front already has it (the dogs bark), so they takes none. Four endings: works · watches · studies · has (and goes, does). The negative moves the S into does: he doesn’t wake up. The mistakes: she work / he go and they works / the dogs barks.

THE AUDIO

Pause and 0.75× on every line; the transcript opens after listening; all were checked against the audio. Scene VIII is the whole story read aloud (about two minutes): listen twice, then the questions, then the text.

OK OR NOT OK? (V) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the eight windows light up, three neighbor rules, green or red: Tom plays the piano at midnight, Ana’s dog barks at six, and you watch the man’s window every night (the story of the lesson). There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it). The starters keep he / she …s in their mouth. Recast quietly while they talk (he play → he plays); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: II Every Night, VII Friday, Saturday, VIII The Whole Story.
  • Out loud: I Someone in Your House, V OK or Not OK? (green or red, then he / she …s), IX Curious Neighbors.
  • Focus: III One S Only (the front or the verb, never both), IV The Building (eight neighbors light eight windows), VI Four Endings.
  • Write: X, their own building, with the red wavy line for she work, they works, watchs / studys and doesn’t wakes. It goes to their My notes.
