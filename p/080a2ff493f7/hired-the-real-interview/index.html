The Real Interview · WOLF HIRED · Lesson 12 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the full interview: small talk, the pitch, the CV walkthrough, why you’re leaving, a STAR story, a weakness, why us, salary, questions for them, closing; then three recorded final questions (why hire you, a mistake, five years).

The story: A real mock interview with a coach’s feedback, a full interview with the teacher, then three recorded questions from a new interviewer.

About 53 min: I · A Mock Interview With Feedback (8) · II · The Full Interview (20) · III · Three Final Questions (14) · IV · The Offer Call (7) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “How do you feel about interviewing in English now, compared with the start of the course?”

Record the full interview with the platform’s recording button if the student agrees, so they can watch it later. Don’t correct during the interview; take notes. Feedback at the end on three things only: one strength, one phrase to change, one thing to practice before the real interview.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

I’m a … with … years of experience… · Last year, I was working… · I tend to…, so I’ve started… · What drew me to… is… · Could you tell me what… is? · Thank you so much for your time.

A MOCK INTERVIEW WITH FEEDBACK

Curry College, mock interview with feedback (0:03–2:55).

THE FULL INTERVIEW

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

THREE FINAL QUESTIONS

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Celebrate the end of the course. Ask the student to book their real interview, send the thank-you email after it, and tell you how it went.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I have experience in customers (with customers), In five years I see me (I see myself), I would like to be leading (correct!), reading the CV in order (tell your story). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

THE OFFER CALL (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the interview, the phone rings: the student got the job. Now a real back-and-forth about the offer. You are Laura from HR at a fictional company; you offer 4,000 a month, a start in two weeks and the office every day, but your secret limit (only you see it) is softer: up to 4,400, a start in four weeks, one home day. Ask the four questions in order, insist a little, wait five seconds in silence after each, and don’t give your limit first. Then close the three points on screen and use the PUSH line (another candidate said yes to 4,000). Recast quietly while they talk (based on the market, I’d be looking for …; I was wondering if …); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
