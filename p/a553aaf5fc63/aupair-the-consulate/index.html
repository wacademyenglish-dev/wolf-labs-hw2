The Consulate · Au Pair · Lesson 11 of 24

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the J-1 visa interview: what the exchange program is, short and true answers about purpose, sponsor, host family, plans, and having the documents ready (passport, DS-2019, and what the agency and the consulate ask for).

The story: An official video from the U.S. Department of State about the J-1 program. Then eight answers to classify, and five questions from a recorded officer.

About 36 min: I · What the J-1 Is (8) · II · Good or Risky? (10) · III · The Officer Asks (14) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “What is the one sentence that explains your trip?” Help the student find it.

IMPORTANT: we never teach the student to hide information or to lie. The J-1 is an exchange program; if her real plans do not fit it, the conversation is with the agency BEFORE the interview. The list of documents and the steps (forms, fees, appointment) come from the agency and the consulate’s official website: always check them there. The interview may be in Portuguese or in English.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

the purpose of my trip · sponsor organization (agency) · I’ll live with… in… · I’ll take care of… · Have you ever…? — Yes, I have / No, I haven’t · I plan to…

WHAT THE J-1 IS

U.S. Department of State, Bureau of Educational and Cultural Affairs, “J-1 Visa Exchange Visitor Program” (2 min, official captions checked on 15/10/2026).

GOOD OR RISKY?

One situation at a time. The student says which side it goes on and WHY, then taps. The color confirms and one line explains. The two columns build a board the student can photograph at the end.

THE OFFICER ASKS

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to write her five true answers and read them out loud once a day before the interview, without memorizing word by word.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

memorized speeches, very long answers, not knowing the family or the city, inventing anything. A2+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · What the J-1 Is.
  • Focus: II · Good or Risky? and IV · I Can.
  • Out loud: III · The Officer Asks.
