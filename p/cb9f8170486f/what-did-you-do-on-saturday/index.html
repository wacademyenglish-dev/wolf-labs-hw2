What Did You Do on Saturday? · The Monday Mic

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: It’s Monday morning, and the student is the new street reporter for The Monday Morning.

The grammar: The Rule: In a past question, did carries the past.

Short class (about 43 min): I You and the Weekend · III Four Weekends · V Maple Street Plaza · VI The Rule · VII The Mystery Guest · XI The Monday Page. Skip if you’re short on time: VIII, IX, X (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 63 min.

Open with: “What do you usually do on Saturdays?”

It’s Monday morning, and the student is the new street reporter for The Monday Morning. The column always asks one thing: What did you do on Saturday? First, four real people answer on video. Then the student takes the microphone to a plaza and asks the questions. After the rule, a mystery guest: the student can only ask Did you…?, and reports each answer to Nora, the editor, with a short answer. The front page, printed on dark paper, fills with YES and NO stamps. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

did carries the past, so the verb goes back to its base: Did you go? Where did you go? The order: question word + did + you + verb. Short answers: Yes, I did. No, she didn’t. The mistakes: Did you went?, Where you went?, You went? (only the voice), Yes, I went as a short answer, No, she don’t.

THE VIDEO

A real video, 0:06 to 2:18, in four chapters (Tim, Buli, Sian, Georgie), sealed until you start. Every question in it has did. The speakers are British: they say at the weekend; in American English, on the weekend.

The words (II): weekend, hiking, beach, cake and swimming, before the video, in match the columns and the missing word. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (IV, extra): every line of the video; tap one to hear it again, and the lines with yellow words open the explanation.

THE REPORTER’S CHOICE (IX) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

The student is still the Monday reporter: first they choose the question for a tired friend (you answer it, with Yes, I did / No, I didn’t), then which weekend from the video goes on page one. Answer first, then let them choose; wait five seconds in silence and let them say the whole sentence. The PUSH line (only you see it) is a quick yes or no. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one light personal question. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I You and the Weekend, V Maple Street Plaza, VII The Mystery Guest, VIII Monday, 8 AM, IX The Reporter’s Choice and X Your Weekend.
  • Listen: II The Words, III Four Weekends and IV Line by Line.
  • Focus: VI The Rule.
  • Write: XI The Monday Page.

No audio in this lesson: your voice is the guest’s and Nora’s.
