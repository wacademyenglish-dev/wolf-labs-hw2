Better Speaking · Episode 3 · In My Opinion · B2

Episode 3 of Better Speaking. Czech footballer Václav Němeček, one of the first Europeans to play in China, sounds confident even with simple English. Richard builds an opinion scale (I reckon, I believe, I strongly believe, I’m absolutely certain, I’m not so sure, but) and a response scale (Exactly · That’s true, but · I agree up to a point, but · Oh, come on!). The sister lesson for B1 is “I Think You Should…”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — What do you reckon, React like Callum. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — Your opinion scale, Match the reaction, Fix the traps, Choose the phrase.
  • Write — Your strong opinion. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

“Eu concordo” becomes I am agree. Double opinion openers: In my opinion, I think…. And “acho que não” becomes I think that no instead of I don’t think so.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, matches and writes. “Only you see this” is yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. The bar on top shows the season: eight episodes, today’s one lit. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
