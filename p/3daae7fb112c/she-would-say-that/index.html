She Would Say That · C1 Unit 2 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on will and would for character: typical past actions (Grandpa would sit here), typical present behaviour (cats will sleep anywhere), annoying habits (he WILL leave his cups), predictable reactions (she would say that, wouldn’t she?) and always + -ing.

The story: Two siblings, Lia and Tom (invented), clear out their grandmother’s house on a Sunday and talk about the family.

About 47 min: I · Who Does That? (6) · II · Two Siblings (10) · III · Future or Character? (9) · IV · Say It With Character (7) · V · Do People Change? (7) · VI · The Hot Seat (8).

Open with: “Annoying or charming? Let’s choose.”

The student knows will = future and would = conditional. Today they discover a third use: character. Read the dialogue together (you can take one part). In the comparison, the student must explain the meaning, not the form. In the hot seat, it’s the student who asks about YOUR family: answer with real stories.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

would for typical past actions · used to for past states · will for typical behaviour · WILL (stressed) for annoying habits · she would say that, wouldn’t she? · always + -ing for complaints

WHO DOES THAT?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

TWO SIBLINGS

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

FUTURE OR CHARACTER?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

SAY IT WITH CHARACTER

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

we would live in Recife (state: used to), she was going to say that (for typical: would say that), he always will leave, would have a dog (state), you’re always lose, he will always forgets. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE ARENA (V) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the transformations, your student takes a side: can people really change their habits? They pick a side and defend it for one minute with will / would for character; then they tap Swap sides and defend the other one. Wait five seconds in silence before they start, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it personal. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
