Dear Host Family · Au Pair · Lesson 2 of 24

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the letter to the host family: skip the obvious introduction, add one unique detail, talk about experience with children and why the USA. Grammar: joining ideas with and, because and so.

The story: Hannah, in a Cultural Care video, gives tips for the letter. Then Beatriz’s letter (invented) in pieces, and the student writes her own.

About 40 min: I · What Families Want (8) · II · Join Your Ideas (9) · III · The Story in Order (7) · IV · Your Letter (12) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “What is one thing about you that nobody forgets?” Write it down: it may open the letter.

The planned video (Cultural Care, profile tips) is no longer online; we use another video from the same series about the letter. Keep the letter TRUE: invented experience is a risk in interviews and in the house.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Dear Host Family, · something unique · and (adding) · because (reason) · so (result) · I can’t wait to meet you!

WHAT FAMILIES WANT

Cultural Care Au Pair, “Profile tips 3: Your host family letter” (1 min, captions checked on 15/10/2026). Short and fast: play it twice.

JOIN YOUR IDEAS

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

THE STORY IN ORDER

The events are mixed. The student taps them in order; wrong order shows in red. When it’s right, the student tells the whole story with first, then, after that, finally.

YOUR LETTER

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Save the letter: it is part of the student’s real profile. Next lesson: the video.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

starting with name, age and city again, because at the start of every sentence, so = then (wrong meaning), very long sentences. A2+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · What Families Want.
  • Focus: II · Join Your Ideas and V · I Can.
  • Out loud: III · The Story in Order.
  • Write: IV · Your Letter.
