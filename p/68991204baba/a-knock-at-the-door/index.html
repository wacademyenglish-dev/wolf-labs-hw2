A Knock at the Door · A2 Unit 4 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new A2 lesson on articles: a / an the first time, the when we know which one or there is only one, no article for things in general (I love music), and a / an with jobs.

The story: At 10 p.m. somebody knocked at the student’s door (Flat 9, invented) and left a box with an old key and a note: “Thank you for the music. The key is for the roof.” The student plays the piano every evening; the neighbor is a musician.

About 40 min: I · The Box (9) · II · A or The? (9) · III · Green, Yellow, Red (6) · IV · The Message to the Neighbor (7) · V · The Hot Seat (9).

Open with: “Somebody knocks at your door at 10 p.m. What do you do?”

Portuguese uses the article a lot more (“eu amo a música”), so watch for “I love the music” in general meaning, and for missing a with jobs (“she is teacher”). The story makes the a → the change visible: first a box, then the box.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

a / an: the first time · the: we know which one · the: only one (the sun, the roof) · an before a vowel sound · a / an with jobs · no article for things in general

THE BOX

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

A OR THE?

One situation at a time. The student says which side it goes on and WHY, then taps. The color confirms and one line explains. The two columns build a board the student can photograph at the end.

THE MESSAGE TO THE NEIGHBOR

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I love the music (in general: I love music), she is teacher (a teacher), a old key (an old key), the box (first time: a box), a sun (the sun). A2: one correction per answer is enough.

GREEN, YELLOW, RED (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

A traffic light in the middle of the lesson, between the two practice screens: now it’s your student’s door. Three choices: open the box? go up to the roof tonight? say yes to the musician’s invitation? Green, yellow or red, then why. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast articles quietly while they talk (a box… the box, a musician); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 6 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · The Box.
  • Focus: II · A or The? and IV · The Message to the Neighbor.
  • Out loud: III · Green, Yellow, Red and V · The Hot Seat.
