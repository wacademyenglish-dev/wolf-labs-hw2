Salary Talk · WOLF HIRED · Lesson 7 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: salary conversations: gratitude first, clarifying questions (Could you tell me how… works?), the ask (Based on…, I’m targeting… Is it possible to make up the difference?), ranges (I’d be looking for something between… and…), conditionals (If we could…, I’d…) and not negotiating against yourself.

The story: An HR leader’s example conversation, lines to sort into strong and weak, then three recorded recruiter calls (early question, offer, counteroffer).

About 39 min: I · The Example Conversation (8) · II · Strong or Weak? (9) · III · Three Calls From Recruiters (18) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “Have you ever negotiated a salary? In Portuguese or English? How did it go?”

Numbers in the lesson are monthly examples; adapt them to the student’s market and currency. The hardest part for most students is the silence after the ask: practice it, count three seconds.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Thank you so much for the offer. · Could you tell me how… works? · Based on…, I’m targeting… · Is it possible to make up the difference? · I’d be looking for something between… and… · If we could…, I’d…

THE EXAMPLE CONVERSATION

Self Made Millennial, salary negotiation conversation example (2:27–5:00).

STRONG OR WEAK?

One situation at a time. The student says which side it goes on and WHY, then taps. The color confirms and one line explains. The two columns build a board the student can photograph at the end.

THREE CALLS FROM RECRUITERS

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to research the real market range for their target job and write their ask sentence.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I need more because of my rent (base it on value), If we could get 9,000, I will sign (I’d sign), What is the salary? (Could you share the range you’ve budgeted?), but if not, it’s okay (stop after the ask). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.
