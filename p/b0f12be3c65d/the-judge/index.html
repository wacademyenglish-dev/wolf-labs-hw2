The Judge · Four Ribbons

Saturday at the Riverside fair, and the student is the judge. Every prize is a superlative, and a prize ribbon (drawn on screen) only sticks when the form is right: -est for short words, the most for long words, best / worst for the two that break. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

Clap the word. One clap: the tallest, the biggest (and the spellings: double the letter, y → i, drop the e). Two or more claps: the most beautiful. Never both: the most biggest. good → the best, bad → the worst. The mistakes: the most big, the more…, the goodest. And a polite judge says the second best.

THE VIDEO

Two pieces of the same video: 0:05–0:57 (who is the tallest?) and 1:32–3:26 (the cake contest). Before: a question. During: the student taps six words. After: one question and a model answer.

YOUR PICK (VI) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

In the middle of the fair, your student decides as the judge: the best prize for Mrs. Alves, the best food at the fair, and their own contest next year. Three options each, sentence almost ready, no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it a little harder. The starters keep the -est / the best in their mouth. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Before the Fair, II The Podium, V The Height Check (one guess: 272 cm), VI Your Pick (you are the judge), VII The Angry Baker (you are the baker), X Judge Your Life.
  • Listen: IV and VIII, the video.
  • Focus: III Pin the Ribbon (clap, then choose; a wrong one falls off), IX The Newspaper (the third paragraph hides the most fair).
  • Write: XI, their own prizes, with the red wavy line for the most tall, the most biggest, the goodest, the beautifulest, bigest and taller in the class. It goes to their My notes.
