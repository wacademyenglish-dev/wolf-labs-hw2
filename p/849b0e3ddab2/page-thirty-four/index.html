Page Thirty-Four · The Night Shelf

Would rather takes a plain verb and than: I’d rather read than scroll. Prefer takes to: I prefer reading to scrolling, or I prefer to read. Students say I’d rather to read and I prefer read. The story: six books on a shelf at night, five with a bookmark ribbon stuck near the start. Five were chosen for their readers; the sixth, the only one without a bookmark, the reader chose himself, and finished.

I PREFER TO READ IS RIGHT

Never mark it wrong. Scene IV is about the PAIR: prefer… TO, rather… THAN.

THE RECORDINGS

Five readers, one short recording each, explaining why they stopped (scene III), and the same recordings again to find who chose each book (scene V). Each plays with pause and 0.75×; the transcript opens only after listening.

THE TWIST

Scene V: nobody chose their own book. Ask “What do the five have in common?” and wait; let your student find it. Scene VI: the sixth book has no ribbon.

YOUR BOOK CLUB

Scene VIII: your student asks; you answer in full, then ask back. They answer with rather, then with prefer.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I’d rather to read, I prefer read, I prefer reading than…, I’d rather walk to drive, Would you rather to…?

UNPOPULAR OPINIONS (IX) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Scene IX is now a scale of unpopular opinions about reading: four statements, and your student taps from 1 (totally disagree) to 5 (totally agree), then defends the number with RATHER or PREFER. There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence, then take the opposite number and use the PUSH line (only you see it). Recast mistakes quietly while they talk (“I’d rather to …” → “I’d rather …”); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. The last statement brings back the sixth book.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Six Spines, II Page Thirty-Four, VII Not Tonight, VIII The Book Club, IX Unpopular Opinions.
  • Listen: III Instead, V Who Chose It (five recordings).
  • Focus: IV To or Than, VI The Sixth Book.
  • Write: X, their own shelf. It goes to their My notes.
