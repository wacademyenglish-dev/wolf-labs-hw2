My Job · Global Payments English · Lesson 1

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the first lesson of a course for people who work with INTERNATIONAL PAYMENTS (FX, SWIFT, settlement). Level A1 that feels like A0: tiny steps, a lot of repetition, everything out loud.

The story: Ana, a payments analyst in São Paulo, meets a colleague from the New York office on a video call. The student meets the Exchange simulator (a fictional payments queue) and writes three sentences about the real job.

About 52 min: I · Morning Check (3) · II · Good Morning (4) · III · Screen Words (8) · IV · I’m, This Is, It’s (8) · V · The Simulator (12) · VI · A Video Call (7) · VII · My Job (7) · VIII · I Can (3).

Open with: “Good morning! How are you today? Are you at the office?”

This course is for analysts who use payment systems in English every day. The student knows the JOB very well; the English is the problem. So: never explain the banking (the glossary below is for you), and never rush. One small sentence, said three times, is a win. Use the PT button when the student is lost: it shows a hint in Portuguese.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

I’m … / I’m a payments analyst. · This is … (the queue, the system) · It’s … (a payment, busy) · Sorry, can you repeat that? · One moment, please. I’m checking.

MORNING CHECK

The fixed ritual: the same kind of screen every lesson, only the data changes. The student reads the screen out loud, one question at a time; then the whole sentence three times (with you, together, alone). The Core 10 list shows the phrases that come back in every lesson; the gold one is new today.

GOOD MORNING

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

SCREEN WORDS

Eight screen words. Tap a word: play the audio, show where it appears on the screen, and the student says it three times (tap I said it each time). The pronunciation tip is only on your screen. The PT button shows the Portuguese if the student is lost.

I’M, THIS IS, IT’S

The explanation in blocks: English on top, Portuguese under each block. Read the card WITH the student, block by block, and play the sentence. Then the student builds the sentence by tapping the blocks, and says it out loud.

THE SIMULATOR

The simulator: a fictional copy of a payments system (Exchange queue, SWIFT Monitor, Unit settlement). Each mission is a task on the screen: the student does it (the mission turns green by itself) and then SAYS the sentence. If the mission is only spoken, tap Mark as done. The student is the expert in the real job: you take care of the English.

A VIDEO CALL

A short audio call (American voices, a British voice in some lessons). BEFORE: one question. LISTEN: play it once without stopping (0.75× is fine), then the questions; Show answer after the student tries. The transcript opens only after the audio: read it together and point to the highlighted words.

MY JOB

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. The open lines go to the homework and to the Morning Check of Lesson 2.

AS SIGLAS E OS TERMOS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno trabalha com pagamentos internacionais. Você não precisa ser especialista: precisa saber do que ele está falando. Cada cena mostra as siglas dela no alto (só você vê) e no painel “Preparar em 1 minuto”. Se ele perguntar algo técnico, devolva: “Can you explain it to me?” (é conversa real).

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Am analyst (I’m an analyst), Is a payment (It’s a payment), I’m a analyst (I’m an analyst), Dis is the queue (This: tongue on the teeth). A1: one correction per answer is enough.

  • Focus: IV · I’m, This Is, It’s and V · The Simulator and VIII · I Can.
