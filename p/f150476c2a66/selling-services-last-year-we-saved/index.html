Last Year We Saved 20% · Selling Services · Lesson 11

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 11 (past simple: was, were, -ed, had, made, sent, began). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: A one-page Bluepeak case study (Atlas Motors, fictional), then Leo tells the same story to Olivia, the new CFO of Northway Chemicals (fictional). The student writes and tells a mini case of a real project.

About 57 min: I · Your Week (6) · II · The Case Study (8) · III · A Story for the CFO (8) · IV · Saved, Not “Save” (10) · V · Tell It in the Past (7) · VI · You Choose (6) · VII · Your Mini Case (9) · VIII · I Can (3).

Open with: “How was your weekend? Did you have meetings yesterday?”

Brazilians often mark the past only with the time word (“last year we save”) and put the past twice (“we didn’t saved”). Two questions fix almost everything: “Is it the past? Then change the verb.” and “Is there didn’t? Then the verb is simple.” Teach only four irregular verbs today (had, made, sent, began). In the mini case, ask for ONE number: it is what the client remembers.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Last year, … was / were … · We + verb-ed: saved, connected, worked · We had · made · sent · began · They didn’t need … · It wasn’t …

YOUR WEEK

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

THE CASE STUDY

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

A STORY FOR THE CFO

A short audio dialogue (American voices). BEFORE: one question. LISTEN: play it once without stopping (0.75× is fine for A1), then the questions; Show answer after the student tries. The transcript opens only after the audio ends: read it together and point to the highlighted words.

SAVED, NOT “SAVE”

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

TELL IT IN THE PAST

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

YOU CHOOSE

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

YOUR MINI CASE

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to bring one real number from a past project (approximate is fine) for the next proposal lessons.

THE TECH WORDS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno vende serviços B2B. Você não precisa ser especialista; precisa saber do que ele está falando. Não ensine estes termos: deixe que ele explique o trabalho dele (é conversa real).

  • BPO (Business Process Outsourcing): Terceirização de processos: a empresa contratada EXECUTA uma rotina do cliente (folha de pagamento, contabilidade, fiscal, contas a pagar). O cliente para de fazer aquilo dentro de casa.
  • BTO (Business Transformation Outsourcing): Terceirização com transformação: além de executar, a contratada MUDA o processo (tecnologia, automação, integração de sistemas). Vende-se como “melhorar”, não só “fazer por você”.
  • ERP: Sistema de gestão que integra finanças, compras, estoque, RH etc. Exemplos (marcas): SAP, Oracle, TOTVS. Um BPO/BTO não vende o ERP: trabalha COM ele ou o integra.
  • Case study · success story: Estudo de caso: o relato de um cliente real (problema → o que fizemos → resultado com número). É a principal “prova” na venda de serviços.
  • Savings (cost savings): Economia de custos. “They saved 20% on payroll costs” = economizaram 20% no custo da folha.
  • Reference client: Cliente-referência: um cliente satisfeito que aceita ser citado ou atender uma ligação de um prospect.
  • ROI (return on investment): Retorno sobre o investimento: quanto o cliente ganha/economiza comparado ao que pagou. O CFO pergunta muito.
  • Payroll costs: Custo da folha: não só os salários, mas também o custo de processar a folha (pessoas, sistema, retrabalho).

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Last year we save 20% (we saved), We didn’t saved money (we didn’t save), It was don’t easy (It wasn’t easy), We maked a plan (We made a plan). A1+: one correction per answer is enough.
