Tricky Questions · Au Pair · Lesson 10 of 24

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the tricky interview questions: What would you do if…? Grammar: the second conditional (If + past, I would + base verb) and If I were you, I would… The safe answer shape: safety first, stay calm, talk to the parents.

The story: Three real host moms (Au Pair in America video) say what they look for. Then a board with twelve situations: crying, a fall, homework, snow, an allergy, homesickness, a stranger, a mistake, a task that is not the au pair’s job.

About 44 min: I · What Host Moms Look For (10) · II · If… I Would… (8) · III · Twelve Situations (16) · IV · If I Were a Host Mom (6) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “What would you do if you missed your flight?” Let the student answer, then show the grammar she used (or should use).

There is rarely one right answer. Praise answers that protect the child, show calm and include the parents. For medical emergencies, the answer always includes calling 911 and the parents.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

What would you do if + past? · If + past, I would + verb · If I were you, I would… · safety first · stay calm · talk to the parents / call 911

WHAT HOST MOMS LOOK FOR

Au Pair in America, “Host Moms Give Advice To Future Au Pairs About Matching Process” (3 min, captions checked on 15/10/2026). Three voices, natural speed.

IF… I WOULD…

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

TWELVE SITUATIONS

A board game around the table. Tap Roll the die: the token moves and the square shows a task that mixes the grammar of the unit. The student does it out loud; tap Done and roll again until the end of the board.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to prepare answers for three more situations she is afraid of.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I would called, If she would cry, If I was you, will in an imaginary answer. B1: one correction per answer is enough.

IF I WERE A HOST MOM (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the twelve situations, no new situation: the student becomes the host mom and ranks five things from the video (family fit, experience with kids, safe driving, fast answers to emails, relaxed photos), then explains the top one and the last one. Open your order and agree on one top 3 together; your order follows the video (family fit first), but it is only one opinion. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it). Recast quietly while they talk (If I were…, I would…, I wouldn’t choose…); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
