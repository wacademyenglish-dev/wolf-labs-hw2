Where Is It? · The Dollhouse

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: A wooden figure, a chair and a box show seven places.

The grammar: Move It: The ghost shows where each thing goes.

Short class (about 47 min): I Look Around · II The Wooden Figure · IV Watch · VI Move It · VII Fix the Labels · VIII Your Pick · XI Close the House. Skip if you’re short on time: IX, X (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 61 min.

Open with: “What is in front of you right now?”

A wooden figure, a chair and a box show seven places. Then a dollhouse living room, cut open: a table, a sofa, a shelf. Every time the student says where something is, it slides there. The hour ends with two rooms that are almost the same, and only talking finds the five differences. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

next to, in front of, behind, under, above, between … and …, on. Where is the cat? It’s under the table. The traps: in the table (on), between of / above of (no of), in front the (in front of), next the (next to), between the window. (both halves), Where the cat is?

THE AUDIO AND THE VIDEO

Seven short recordings for the figure (pause and 0.75×; the words appear after listening; checked against the audio). The real video (0:16–1:14): a person moves around a box and a chair.

The words (III): box, chair, stuck, airport and home, before the video, in match the columns and mixed letters. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (V, extra): every line of the video; tap one to hear it again, and the lines with yellow words open the explanation.

YOUR PICK (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Three light choices, two options each, all about a new house: where the desk goes, where the cat sleeps, where you stand at a party. There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it a little harder. The starters keep next to / in front of / under / between … and … in their mouth. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Look Around, VIII Your Pick, IX Five Differences and X Your Room.
  • Listen: II The Wooden Figure, III The Words, IV Watch and V Line by Line.
  • Focus: VI Move It and VII Fix the Labels.
  • Write: XI Close the House.
