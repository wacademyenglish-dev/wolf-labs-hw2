Know Your Rights · Au Pair · Lesson 13 of 24

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the program rules and the au pair’s rights: up to 10 hours a day and 45 a week; one and a half days off per week; one full weekend off per month; two weeks of paid vacation; a private bedroom; up to $500 for classes; the passport stays with the au pair; the right to report abuse; the State Department help line.

The story: A host family (video) learns the five numbers and plans a real week. Then the student’s rights card and six real weeks to count.

About 37 min: I · The Five Numbers (12) · II · Your Rights Card (9) · III · Count the Hours (12) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “Do you know how many hours an au pair can work per week?” Most students guess more than 45.

Sources checked on 15/10/2026: the federal regulation (22 CFR 62.31) and the official site j1visa.state.gov. The 2023 proposal to change hours and pay has NOT been approved (status in October 2026); check j1visa.state.gov before each new group. We don’t teach the weekly stipend amount: it is set by the program and can change; the agency confirms it. The video is from a scheduling app company: we show only 0:44–6:06, without the product part.

THE FIVE NUMBERS

AupairSync, “45 hours a week: scheduling your au pair without breaking the J-1 rules”, only 0:44–6:06 (captions checked on 15/10/2026). It is made for host families: perfect to show the student the rules from THEIR side.

YOUR RIGHTS CARD

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

COUNT THE HOURS

Listen or read, then choose one option. The colors show right and wrong, and one line says why. Ask the student WHY before you move on.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to save the help line in her phone today, under “State Department help line”.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

the hours of last week carry over, extra pay allows extra hours, sleeping kids don’t count, a family trip is vacation. B1: one correction per answer is enough.
