The Other Track · The Railway Switch

The third conditional is for a past that didn’t happen: If I had known, I would have told you. Both halves move back: if + had + third form, would have + third form. Students say If I would have known. In speech, both HAD and WOULD become ’d. On screen, a track map: the main track is what happened; when both halves are right, the switch flips and the train runs down the other track to a place that never existed.

SCENE I IS NOT CORRECTED

Write down the student’s third answer word for word. It will probably come out as If I would have. It comes back in scene X.

OPEN OR SHUT

Scene II: the tense follows the door, not the feeling. A door is shut when the moment is gone. In scene IV, item 5 is an open door on purpose.

THE RECORDINGS

Scene V: four short lines with two ’d each, with pause and 0.75×. The student taps what each ’d stands for: the one next to IF is HAD. Line 2 has HAD HAD, and that’s correct English. Then you both say each line at normal speed.

THE CHAIN

Scene VIII: after each choice, ask “And then what would have happened?” and tap ONE MORE STOP for each link; the train goes further down the other track. The chain drifts into the simple past: pull it back to would have.

KEEP IT KIND

Regret can be heavy. If a choice is painful, let the student pick a lighter one.

THE BIGGEST SWITCH (IX) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

The five stories from Flip the Switch come back. Your student ranks them from the biggest change to the smallest and explains the top one and the last one with if + had + participle, would have + participle. Then open your order and agree on one top three together. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk (watch for “If I would have”); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I A Choice That Changed Things, III Flip the Switch, VII No IF Needed, VIII Three of Your Choices, IX The Biggest Switch.
  • Listen: V Two ’d, Two Words.
  • Focus: II Open or Shut?, IV Both Halves Move Back, VI If I Would Have.
  • Write: X, their choice on the other track. It goes to their My notes.
