The Book You Keep · The Card Catalog

A second-hand bookshop keeps a card for every book, every person and every place. The student learns to describe without naming: a person who…, a thing that…, a place where…. The screen is a card catalog with three drawers, and a card only goes into the drawer of the right word.

NO WHAT · WHERE NEVER GOES

the book what I keep is the error of the hour. WHO and THAT can disappear before a new person (the book you keep); WHERE stays.

Scene IX is the heart: the student defines, you guess. Guess slowly.

THE SHOP’S NEW IDEAS (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

A traffic light: the owner has three new ideas (books in brown paper, a free reading corner, notes inside that cost more), and your student gives each one green, yellow or red. There is no right answer: let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it personal. Recast quietly while they talk (a book that, never a book what; a place where); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
