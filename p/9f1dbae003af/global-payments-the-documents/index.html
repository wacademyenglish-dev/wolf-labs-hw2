The Documents · Global Payments English · Lesson 10

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the five import documents (commercial invoice, proforma, bill of lading, FX contract, import declaration), Do you have …? / Yes, I do / No, I don’t, We need …, and the Screen Check of Unit 2.

The story: Two documents are missing for an import payment. Ana calls Tom at Brava Foods. The student checks the papers in the DOCUMENTS tab, requests what is missing, plays the call with you (you are the client), writes the email and does the Screen Check.

About 54 min: I · Morning Check (4) · II · Paper or Screen? (3) · III · Document Words (7) · IV · Do You Have …? (8) · V · The Simulator (12) · VI · The Missing Papers (6) · VII · The Document Email (6) · VIII · Screen Check (5) · IX · I Can (3).

Open with: “Do you print documents or read them on the screen?”

This lesson closes Unit 2. The student knows these papers in Portuguese very well (fatura, conhecimento de embarque, contrato de câmbio, DI/DUIMP): the glossary is for you. Questions with Do are new: “Do you have …?” (not “You have …?”), short answers “Yes, I do / No, I don’t”. The Screen Check (VIII) mixes all of Unit 2: routine, times, counts, rates, documents. Goal: 7 of 10.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Do you have the bill of lading? · Yes, I do. / No, I don’t. · We need the import declaration. · Can you send …, please?

MORNING CHECK

The fixed ritual: the same kind of screen every lesson, only the data changes. The student reads the screen out loud, one question at a time; then the whole sentence three times (with you, together, alone). The Core 10 list shows the phrases that come back in every lesson; the gold one is new today.

PAPER OR SCREEN?

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

DOCUMENT WORDS

Eight screen words. Tap a word: play the audio, show where it appears on the screen, and the student says it three times (tap I said it each time). The pronunciation tip is only on your screen. The PT button shows the Portuguese if the student is lost.

DO YOU HAVE …?

The explanation in blocks: English on top, Portuguese under each block. Read the card WITH the student, block by block, and play the sentence. Then the student builds the sentence by tapping the blocks, and says it out loud.

THE SIMULATOR

The simulator: a fictional copy of a payments system (Exchange queue, SWIFT Monitor, Unit settlement). Each mission is a task on the screen: the student does it (the mission turns green by itself) and then SAYS the sentence. If the mission is only spoken, tap Mark as done. The student is the expert in the real job: you take care of the English.

THE MISSING PAPERS

A short audio call (American voices, a British voice in some lessons). BEFORE: one question. LISTEN: play it once without stopping (0.75× is fine), then the questions; Show answer after the student tries. The transcript opens only after the audio: read it together and point to the highlighted words.

THE DOCUMENT EMAIL

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

SCREEN CHECK

The Screen Check of the unit: a screen the student has not seen. The student talks about it for two minutes; you tap each item they say correctly. Goal: 7 of 10. Items left open go to the next lesson’s Morning Check.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Unit 2 is done. Screen Check items left open go to Lesson 11.

AS SIGLAS E OS TERMOS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno trabalha com pagamentos internacionais. Você não precisa ser especialista: precisa saber do que ele está falando. Cada cena mostra as siglas dela no alto (só você vê) e no painel “Preparar em 1 minuto”. Se ele perguntar algo técnico, devolva: “Can you explain it to me?” (é conversa real).

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

You have the invoice? (Do you have the invoice?), Yes, I have (Yes, I do), We need of the B/L (We need the B/L), No, I not have (No, I don’t). A1: one correction per answer is enough.
