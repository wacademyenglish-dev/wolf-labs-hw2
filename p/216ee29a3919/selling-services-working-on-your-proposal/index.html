We’re Working on Your Proposal. · Selling Services · Lesson 10

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 10 (present continuous (now) × present simple (routine)). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Leo sends Karen Wells (Lumen Logistics, fictional) a status e-mail about the proposal; then Karen calls for news because her CEO asks every day. The student moves sentences between routine and right now, and writes a status e-mail to a real client.

About 56 min: I · Your Week (6) · II · A Status E-mail (8) · III · “How Is It Going?” (8) · IV · Now or Every Week? (10) · V · Routine or Right Now? (7) · VI · You Choose (5) · VII · Your Status Update (9) · VIII · I Can (3).

Open with: “What are you working on this week? What do you usually do on Friday afternoons?”

Portuguese “estamos trabalhando” makes the -ing easy, but Brazilians drop be (“we working”), keep the base verb (“I am work”) or use -ing for routines (“we are send every month”). One test: a time word for NOW (now, today, this week, right now) → am / is / are + -ing; a ROUTINE word (every, usually, always) → present simple. In the status e-mail, insist on one routine sentence: it shows the client you are organized.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

We’re working on … · She’s checking … · We aren’t waiting for … · We usually send … · every Monday · It’s on time.

YOUR WEEK

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

A STATUS E-MAIL

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

“HOW IS IT GOING?”

A short audio dialogue (American voices). BEFORE: one question. LISTEN: play it once without stopping (0.75× is fine for A1), then the questions; Show answer after the student tries. The transcript opens only after the audio ends: read it together and point to the highlighted words.

NOW OR EVERY WEEK?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

ROUTINE OR RIGHT NOW?

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

YOU CHOOSE

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

YOUR STATUS UPDATE

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. If the student has an open proposal, send the status e-mail to the real client this week.

THE TECH WORDS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno vende serviços B2B. Você não precisa ser especialista; precisa saber do que ele está falando. Não ensine estes termos: deixe que ele explique o trabalho dele (é conversa real).

  • BPO (Business Process Outsourcing): Terceirização de processos: a empresa contratada EXECUTA uma rotina do cliente (folha de pagamento, contabilidade, fiscal, contas a pagar). O cliente para de fazer aquilo dentro de casa.
  • BTO (Business Transformation Outsourcing): Terceirização com transformação: além de executar, a contratada MUDA o processo (tecnologia, automação, integração de sistemas). Vende-se como “melhorar”, não só “fazer por você”.
  • ERP: Sistema de gestão que integra finanças, compras, estoque, RH etc. Exemplos (marcas): SAP, Oracle, TOTVS. Um BPO/BTO não vende o ERP: trabalha COM ele ou o integra.
  • Status update: Atualização de andamento enviada ao cliente (o que está sendo feito, o que falta, se está no prazo).
  • Timeline: Cronograma do projeto (etapas e datas).
  • On time · delayed: No prazo · atrasado.
  • Headcount report: Relatório com o quadro de funcionários (quantos, onde, em que área). Base para o preço da folha.
  • Stakeholder: Pessoa interessada no projeto que precisa ser informada (CEO, CFO, RH, TI). “Our CEO is asking every day” = um stakeholder pressionando.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

We working on it (We’re working on it), I am work on the proposal (I’m working on the proposal), We are send a report every month (We send a report every month), She checks it right now (She’s checking it right now). A1+: one correction per answer is enough.
