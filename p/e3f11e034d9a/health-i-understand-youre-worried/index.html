I Understand You’re Worried · English for Healthcare · Lesson 10 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: empathy with patients and families: naming and validating emotions (I can see…, That’s completely understandable), thanking for information (I’m really glad you told me), following empathy with a concrete action and a time, and saying no kindly with an alternative.

The story: An AHRQ (U.S. Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality) video: a nurse involves a patient’s husband in the recovery plan. Then five recorded worried people (a mother, a patient, a daughter, a nervous patient, a husband) and cold answers turned into caring ones.

About 33 min: I · Patient and Family (8) · II · Five Worried People (11) · III · From Cold to Caring (10) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “What did a health professional say to you once that made you feel cared for?”

Brazilian warmth is an asset; in English, the challenge is the wording. Practice the warm voice too: the same words in a cold voice don’t work. Avoid “Calm down”: it usually makes people angrier.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

I can see you’re worried. · That’s completely understandable. · I’m really glad you told me. · Let me find out and come back in… · Let’s go through it together. · For this part…, but I’ll…

PATIENT AND FAMILY

AHRQ, “CUSP: Effective Patient and Family Communication” (2 min, official captions checked on 15/10/2026).

FIVE WORRIED PEOPLE

Listen or read, then choose one option. The colors show right and wrong, and one line says why. Ask the student WHY before you move on.

FROM COLD TO CARING

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student which phrase they will use tomorrow at work.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Calm down., It’s nothing., That’s not my job., promising what you can’t control. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON
