The Corps Has Rules · Demon Slayer · The Nichirin Blade

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: A boy, his sister, and the rules of the Corps.

The grammar: The Rules of the Corps: Must, mustn’t, have to, don’t have to. Forbidden is not the same as not necessary.

The class (about 56 min): I Warm-up and Put It in Order · II The Words · III The Trailer · IV Line by Line · V What He Needs Most · VI The Rules of the Corps · VII The Nichirin Blade · VIII Real Rules · IX Your Rule Book. No extra scenes: every scene is part of the class.

Open with: “You know Nezuko now. In the trailer, what mustn’t she do? Guess.”

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba (2019–) is the Japanese anime from Koyoharu Gotouge’s manga. Tanjiro Kamado sells charcoal on a snowy mountain; a demon attacks his family, and his sister Nezuko becomes a demon who still protects him. To save her, he trains for two years, passes the Final Selection (seven days on a mountain of wisteria) and joins the Demon Slayer Corps, where every slayer gets a nichirin sword that changes color. The grammar: must / mustn’t, have to / has to / don’t have to and had to, with the key difference: mustn’t = forbidden, don’t have to = not necessary.

A note for the teacher: the trailer has demons and sword fights; nothing violent is on the lesson screens, but watch it once before class. It is the official English dub (0:56). The five frames on the film strip follow the times of the automatic captions, and each moment is told in our own words.

Warm-up and Put It in Order (I): two parts. 1 · Warm-up: three questions about the student’s life, one at a time (big on the screen, with “Ask more” under each and one short line about the anime under the photo). Let them talk; A2: short answers are fine. 2 · Put It in Order: four cards of Tanjiro’s story, shuffled. The student taps them in order (“First… then…”); then the cards show the real order, and the student tells the story with had to / must.

The words (II): promise, save, human, ready and job, before the video, in match the columns and mixed letters. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (IV) lists the lines with the yellow words: tap a line, hear it again, and the student says it like the character.

The ending (IX, Your Rule Book): the student writes four rules for their own life, one with must, one with mustn’t, one with have to and one with don’t have to. The corrector marks the traps (must to, don’t must, he have to). It goes to My notes.

ON SCREEN

The cover is a mountain of purple wisteria at night with old lanterns. I: Warm-up and Put It in Order. III: the trailer in three steps. Before: a stranger on a snowy mountain says something about Tanjiro’s sister; the student taps how to answer (say yes, ask what to do, say you are scared) and the conversation appears. Watch: One word is wrong. Five lines from the trailer (Your big sister’s got you…), each with a ▶ time: tap it and the video jumps to that moment; the student says the right word. After: True or lie? Five sentences, each with a ▶; if it is a lie, the student says the truth. IV: five frames on the film strip. V, What He Needs Most: the student puts five things in order (what Tanjiro needs most to save Nezuko) and defends the first and the last. VI, The Rules of the Corps: a letter from the Corps on dark paper. The student taps the rules in the letter (must protect, mustn’t hurt, has to, don’t have to, had to, Do I have to), then finishes the rule board: five lines with two choices each. The board stays in the corner on the next pages. VII, The Nichirin Blade: a sword lies across the screen with a grey blade in six parts. Six rule cards (Protect people, Hurt humans, Wear the uniform at home…) and three boxes: Must / have to, Mustn’t, Don’t have to. Every card in the right box lights one part of the blade in the blue of water; with six, the whole blade gets its color, and the student says the six rules. VIII, Real Rules: six cards (your job or school, at home, driving, as a child, on Sunday, this class); the student taps a card and says a rule. IX: the rule book.

THE COMMON ERRORS

must to protect, he musts, don’t must, mustn’t and don’t have to swapped (forbidden × not necessary), Tanjiro have to, has carry, you has to, Have I to…? / Must I to…?, must trained, musted, I must work yesterday (had to).

WHAT HE NEEDS MOST (V) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after Line by Line, Tanjiro has promised to save his sister “no matter what it takes”. The student puts five things in order (a strong sword, a good teacher, friends on his team, two years of training, a kind heart), from the most important to the least, and defends the first and the last with must / have to / don’t have to. Then compare with your order. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to bring it to their own life. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk (must to, he have to, don’t must); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About six minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Warm-up and Put It in Order, V What He Needs Most, VII The Nichirin Blade and VIII Real Rules.
  • Listen: II The Words, III The Trailer and IV Line by Line.
  • Focus: VI The Rules of the Corps.
  • Write: IX Your Rule Book.
