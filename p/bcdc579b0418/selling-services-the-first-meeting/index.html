The First Meeting · Selling Services · Lesson 5

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 5 (is · there are · don’t · questions). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Leo’s first meeting with Daniel Price, finance director of Serra Steel (fictional). Then the student does the meeting (you are the director), chooses the next step with a reason, fixes a follow-up e-mail and writes his own.

About 54 min: I · Your Week (5) · II · A First Meeting (9) · III · Your Meeting (12) · IV · The Next Step (7) · V · Fix the Follow-up (8) · VI · Your Follow-up E-mail (10) · VII · I Can (3).

Open with: “Is there a big meeting this week? Do you prepare for meetings?”

Review lesson: the four targets in one real meeting. In III you are the director: read the answer on your screen (short), and let the student lead. In IV there is no wrong choice: the reason is the exercise (“I prefer … because …”). One correction per answer is enough.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

We’re … We aren’t … · We do … We don’t … · How many … are there? Is there …? Do you …? · Is Friday good for …?

YOUR WEEK

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

A FIRST MEETING

A short audio dialogue (American voices). BEFORE: one question. LISTEN: play it once without stopping (0.75× is fine for A1), then the questions; Show answer after the student tries. The transcript opens only after the audio ends: read it together and point to the highlighted words.

YOUR MEETING

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

THE NEXT STEP

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

FIX THE FOLLOW-UP

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

YOUR FOLLOW-UP E-MAIL

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. If the student has a real meeting soon, adapt the e-mail to it.

THE TECH WORDS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno vende serviços B2B. Você não precisa ser especialista; precisa saber do que ele está falando. Não ensine estes termos: deixe que ele explique o trabalho dele (é conversa real).

  • BPO (Business Process Outsourcing): Terceirização de processos: a empresa contratada EXECUTA uma rotina do cliente (folha de pagamento, contabilidade, fiscal, contas a pagar). O cliente para de fazer aquilo dentro de casa.
  • BTO (Business Transformation Outsourcing): Terceirização com transformação: além de executar, a contratada MUDA o processo (tecnologia, automação, integração de sistemas). Vende-se como “melhorar”, não só “fazer por você”.
  • ERP: Sistema de gestão que integra finanças, compras, estoque, RH etc. Exemplos (marcas): SAP, Oracle, TOTVS. Um BPO/BTO não vende o ERP: trabalha COM ele ou o integra.
  • Finance director: Diretor(a) financeiro(a). Às vezes abaixo do CFO; costuma ser quem sente a dor no dia a dia.
  • Shared service center (SSC / CSC): Centro de Serviços Compartilhados: uma área central que faz finanças/RH para todas as unidades. Sem CSC, cada fábrica tem sua equipe (ineficiente).
  • Proposal: Proposta comercial (escopo, prazo, preço).
  • Follow-up: O contato depois da reunião (e-mail curto: obrigado, fatos, próximo passo).
  • Next step · pilot: Próximo passo (2ª reunião, proposta) · piloto/projeto-teste (ex.: “a free first month”).
  • Closing: Fechamento do negócio (assinatura).

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

It don’t is efficient (It isn’t efficient), There is four thousand employees (There are…), We aren’t sell SAP (We don’t sell SAP), Friday is good for you? (Is Friday good for you?). A1+: one correction per answer is enough.
