Either One Works · Two Keys

Your student’s friend Nico has two job offers, one in Denver and one in Portland, and an apartment in each city. He has to sign one lease by Friday at 6 PM, and everything is the same. “Either one works!” Then the one line that matters: his dog, Biscuit, isn’t allowed in either apartment. The grammar: both (the two, yes), either (one or the other), neither (not this one and not that one), the pairs both… and / either… or / neither… nor, and of them. The screen: two apartment keys that get a stamp for each line.

THE TRAP

Many students use a double negative (I don’t like neither, Neither of them don’t allow), put an article before both (the both), and translate “none of the two” and “any of the two” word for word. In English, neither is already the negative; with don’t / can’t, it’s either.

HEADS OR TAILS

Scene IV is the pressure: Nico sends eight nervous messages in the last hour. Read the message and the first words; your student finishes at once. The last message changes everything.

WHEN THEY’RE STUCK

Ask: Yes to the two, or no to the two? Or one or the other? Hold up two fingers.

THE COUNCIL (VI) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Nico has chosen Portland. Now your student sits on the council for his first month: three proposals (dogs in the office, home days, Denver calling again). They vote Yes, Propose a change or No, and defend the vote with both, either or neither; you vote the other way. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk (watch for “the both” and double negatives); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I You and Big Choices, II Two Keys, IV Heads or Tails, V Nico Calls Back, VI The Council, VII Your Two Options.
  • Focus: III The Rule.
  • Write: VIII, their own two options. It goes to their My notes.
