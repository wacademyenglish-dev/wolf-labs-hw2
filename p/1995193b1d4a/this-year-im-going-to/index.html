This Year I’m Going to… · The January Tracker

An easy reader in five short chapters. On December 31, the Carter family writes five New Year’s resolutions; the tracker shows thirty-one days of January for each one. Your student reads each chapter, answers a question and says the person’s line, and the row lights up to the day the resolution stopped (or to the 31st). This is a review lesson for going to (a plan from before, proof in front of you) and will (decided now, a promise, an opinion with I think), plus want to / would like to for goals and was going to for a plan that failed.

WHEN WAS IT DECIDED?

When your student is stuck, ask: Is it on the card, or is he deciding now? Proof, or only an opinion?

THE COUNTDOWN

Scene IV is the pressure: 11:52 PM, eight quick lines at the party. You play each person; your student answers fast. Every right line lights a firework and moves the clock a minute.

AUNT CLARA

Scene VI: you are Aunt Clara on a video call from Mexico. Your student retells January from memory, then asks you back. Your answers are on your screen.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I will to run, I call you tomorrow (as a promise), It snows (with clouds in front of you), I think you do it, I went to clean it, I’m going run, I’d like speak.

GREEN, YELLOW, RED (VII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After Aunt Clara’s call, your student judges four resolutions for their OWN life: green (I’m going to do it), yellow (maybe) or red (no way). There is no right answer: let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Push for going to (a plan), I’ll (decided now) and was going to (a plan that failed). Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I You and Your New Year, IV The Countdown, V Your Year, VI Aunt Clara Calls, VII Green, Yellow, Red.
  • Focus: II The January Tracker, III When Was It Decided?
  • Write: VIII, their card for next January. It goes to their My notes.
