The More I Fight · Solo Leveling · The System

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: The weakest hunter, and a window that makes him stronger every day.

The grammar: The More, the Stronger: The more I fight, the stronger I get.

The class (about 57 min): I Warm-up and The Hunter Rules · II The Trailer · III Scene by Scene · IV Your Level-Up List · V The More, the Stronger · VI The System · VII Your Daily Quest · VIII The Interview. No extra scenes: every scene is part of the class.

Open with: “You know Jinwoo now. In the trailer, the more he fights, the… what? Guess.”

Solo Leveling (2024), a Japanese anime from a Korean web novel, on Crunchyroll. Gates to other worlds opened ten years ago; some people woke up with powers and became hunters, ranked from E to S. Sung Jinwoo is an E-rank, “the weakest hunter of all mankind”, who keeps working because his mother is in hospital. After a terrible double dungeon he sees a blue window nobody else can see, the System, which gives him daily quests and lets him level up. The grammar: the more…, the more…: the + comparative + subject + verb, the + comparative + subject + verb (the more I fight, the stronger I get), short words with -er and long ones with more (the more dangerous), and the short form (the sooner, the better).

A note for the teacher: the trailer has Japanese voices and English subtitles on the picture. It has fights and a little blood, fast, nothing long on screen. The times come from the subtitles (24/09); the System moment (1:04 to 1:24) is ESTIMATED, and each moment is told in our own words.

Warm-up and The Hunter Rules (I): two parts. 1 · Warm-up: three questions about the student’s life, one at a time (big on the screen, with “Ask more” under each and one short line about the anime under the photo). Let them talk; don’t correct the grammar yet. 2 · The Hunter Rules: four rules of this world appear one at a time (the gates, the ranks, the hidden dungeon, the System’s daily quest). For each, the student taps Yes / No way / Only if… and says why; the rules already use the more…, the more…. At the end they invent a fifth rule.

The ending (VIII, The Interview): the teacher is Sung Jinwoo. The student asks five questions (what happens the more you fight, the higher the rank, the more people look at you, what gets easier, the more power you have). Then they swap: the student is a hunter who just woke up with powers. Speaking only.

ON SCREEN

The cover is a dark subway tunnel with a big blue gate. I: Warm-up and The Hunter Rules. II: the trailer in three steps. Before: guess the rule of the System from four clues, one at a time. Watch: True or lie? and a tap about what the student sees, each with a ▶ time: tap it and the video jumps to that moment (the times come from the English subtitles on the image; the video has Japanese voices, so there are no spoken lines to quote). After: the student tells Jinwoo’s story and taps each of six words when they use it, then moves a slider (is it brave or crazy?). III: five frames on the film strip. V: six words to sort into three boxes (the + short word + -er, the more + a long word, the more + a noun); when all six are right, the rule appears. VI, The System: the blue window with the rank (E to S), the XP bar and six daily quests. The student matches the start of each quest to its ending (The more I fight → the stronger I get); every right match ticks a quest and raises the rank. At six, rank S. Then say each full sentence out loud. VII, Your Daily Quest: six cards to open (sleep, phone, coffee, age, a hobby, the class); the student finishes the sentence out loud and taps Got it! for each true one. VIII: the interview.

THE COMMON ERRORS

more I fight, more strong I get (no THE, Portuguese “quanto mais… mais forte”), the more strong, the most I train, …, faster he gets (the second THE), the dangerouser, the more he makes money, the easier gets it, the sooner, the best.

YOUR LEVEL-UP LIST (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the trailer moments, your student ranks five things that make them stronger in real life, then explains the first and the last with the more…, the more… There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it). Then tap “Show my teacher’s order”, explain yours, and agree on one top 3 together. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Warm-up and The Hunter Rules, IV Your Level-Up List, VI The System, VII Your Daily Quest and VIII The Interview.
  • Listen: II The Trailer and III Scene by Scene.
  • Focus: V The More, the Stronger.
  • Write: the homework (the lesson ends speaking).
