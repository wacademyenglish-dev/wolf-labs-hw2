Better Speaking · Episode 5 · Hold Their Attention · B2

Episode 5 of Better Speaking. Kofi Annan speaks to the world about globalization. Richard shows how he holds attention: he invites the listener (Try to imagine…), makes points stronger (The simple fact of the matter is this), uses contrast (all / none) and varies his words (human beings / humanity). Plus: prepare the words for topics you know will come up. The sister lesson for B1 is “Half of the World”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — Who holds your attention, Your opening line. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — Make them listen, Match the move, Fix the traps, Choose the phrase.
  • Write — Your speech opening. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

“Imagine você” becomes Imagine you a world…. “Menos de” becomes less of two dollars. And the /s/–/z/ pair from the episode: advice (noun) and advise (verb).
