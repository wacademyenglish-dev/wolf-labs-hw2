The Weekend Planner · A2 Unit 9 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 9 (If… and verb + verb). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Three friends are going to the mountains this weekend and your student plans the trip. You are Bia, Theo and Kai. Then you are the group chat.

About 45 min: I · Speed Round (6) · II · The Weekend Planner (14) · III · Saturday Problems (7) · IV · The Story in Order (7) · V · Would You Rather? (7) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Sixty seconds. Ready? Start the clock.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

zero conditional · if + imperative · first conditional and unless · want / need / decide + to; enjoy / avoid / finish + -ing · want / ask / tell + person + to · remember / forget / stop + -ing or to

SPEED ROUND

A 60-second speed round. Tap Start the clock, then Right or Pass for each item; the answer is on your screen. At the end of class, play again and beat the score.

THE WEEKEND PLANNER

You are each friend. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the group chat: the student tells the plan with if + will. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

THE STORY IN ORDER

The events are mixed. The student taps them in order; wrong order shows in red. When it’s right, the student tells the whole story with first, then, after that, finally.

WOULD YOU RATHER?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

if it will rain, I enjoy to cook, I want go, she told that I call, don’t forget call, unless it doesn’t rain. A2: one correction per answer is enough.

SATURDAY PROBLEMS (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the plan, Saturday gets complicated three times (the rain, Theo’s boss, the stars at midnight) and your student decides for the group. There is no right answer: let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast quietly while they talk (If it rains, we’ll…, told him to…, stop working); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 7 minutes. The Would You Rather (V) stays light and personal.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Speed Round and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Weekend Planner, III · Saturday Problems, IV · The Story in Order and V · Would You Rather?.
