Interactive Writing · DET Prep · Lesson 13 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the Interactive Writing question: a topic and about 5 minutes to write; then a follow-up question about the same topic and about 3 more minutes. The student learns a clear paragraph shape (topic sentence → support → wrap-up) and varied connectors.

The story: A model paragraph from an invented candidate (a banking app), then the student writes about a time technology made life easier, and answers a follow-up about its disadvantages.

About 39 min: I · A Model Paragraph (9) · II · Better Connectors (8) · III · Five, Then Three (18) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “Which app would you miss most if it disappeared tomorrow?” That is the topic of today.

Do not help during the 5 minutes. After each part, read the text together with the corrector’s red lines, count the connectors and the words, and go through the checklist. The follow-up question tests if the student can extend an idea, not start again.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

topic sentence · Before, … Now, … · Moreover, (one more point) · However, (contrast) · For instance, (example) · Overall, (wrap-up)

A MODEL PARAGRAPH

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

BETTER CONNECTORS

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

FIVE, THEN THREE

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Keep both texts: they are a good base for the Writing Sample in the next lesson.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

and… and… and… (no variety), But at the start of every sentence, like for example, no wrap-up, under 80 words. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · A Model Paragraph.
  • Write: II · Better Connectors and III · Five, Then Three.
  • Focus: IV · I Can.
