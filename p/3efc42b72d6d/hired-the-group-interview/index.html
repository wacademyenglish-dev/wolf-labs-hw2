The Group Interview · WOLF HIRED · Lesson 10 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: group interviews: agreeing (That’s a great point), building (Building on what … said,…; To add to that,…), disagreeing politely (I see it slightly differently, because…), managing turns (Sorry, go ahead. Can I add something?), inviting others (What do you think, …?) and reaching a decision (So, do we all agree on…?).

The story: An Indeed mock group interview with three candidates, then a fictional hotel group task where the teacher plays two candidates.

About 48 min: I · Three Candidates, One Room (9) · II · Build, Don’t Interrupt (12) · III · The Group Task (16) · IV · Team Hot Takes (7) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “Have you ever done a group interview or a group task at work? How did you feel?”

In the group task, play Leo as insistent and Mia as silent. Interrupt the student once on purpose to see if they use “Sorry, go ahead” and come back with “Can I add something?”.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

That’s a great point. · Building on what … said,… · I see it slightly differently, because… · Sorry, go ahead. · Can I add something? · What do you think, …?

THREE CANDIDATES, ONE ROOM

Indeed, how to ace your group interview: a mock interview with commentary (0:49–4:50).

BUILD, DON’T INTERRUPT

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

THE GROUP TASK

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student which phrase felt most natural and which one they need to practice.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I agree with you (fine; add a reason), I don’t agree with you (I see it slightly differently), Wait, I want to talk (Can I add something?), Like Chloe said (Building on what Chloe said). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

TEAM HOT TAKES (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the group task, four hot takes about teamwork: the quiet person, fair or unfair group interviews, interrupting, the leader who speaks last. The student taps 1 to 5 and defends the number; there is no right answer. Take the opposite side every time, so the student must disagree politely, using the lesson’s phrases (I see it slightly differently, because…, Building on that…, Can I add something?). Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it). Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
