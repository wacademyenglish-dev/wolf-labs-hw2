The Thank-You Email · WOLF HIRED · Lesson 11 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the thank-you email: within 24 hours; gratitude (Thank you for taking the time to meet with me), personalization (I really enjoyed hearing about…), enthusiasm (I’m very excited about this opportunity), next steps (I look forward to hearing about next steps), subject lines, a P.S., and following up after a week.

The story: An HR leader’s template and two real emails, a fictional email with five problems, then the student writes an email from fictional interview notes.

About 38 min: I · The Three Ingredients (8) · II · Fix the Email (10) · III · Write Yours (16) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “After your last interview, did you send any message? What did you write?”

After the form, ask the student to write the whole email in the chat as one text, then read it aloud. Also teach the follow-up line for one week later: “I wanted to follow up on my interview for…”.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Thank you for taking the time to meet with me. · I really enjoyed hearing about… · I’m very excited about this opportunity. · I look forward to hearing about next steps. · P.S. Here’s the article I mentioned. · I wanted to follow up on…

THE THREE INGREDIENTS

Self Made Millennial, how to write a thank-you email after the interview (1:44–4:43).

FIX THE EMAIL

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

WRITE YOURS

A practice form in English, filled in with a FICTIONAL person’s data (never the student’s real documents in class). The student reads the profile card and fills in the form; Check the form shows each field right or wrong, with the correct answer.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to save the template and use it after their next real interview.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Thanks for the opportunity of the interview (Thank you for taking the time to meet with me), I stay waiting your answer (I look forward to hearing from you), I look forward to hear (to hearing), Hey!!! (Hi + name). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · The Three Ingredients.
  • Focus: II · Fix the Email and IV · I Can.
  • Write: III · Write Yours.
