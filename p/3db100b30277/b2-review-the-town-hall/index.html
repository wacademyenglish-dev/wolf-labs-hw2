The Town Hall · B2 Unit 8 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 8 (Connecting, contrasting, explaining). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: A public meeting at the town hall: should Main Street be closed to cars? It is played on a board game. On each square you are a different voice of the town (the baker, the taxi driver, a mother, the doctor). Your student agrees, concedes and disagrees, then writes to the council.

About 49 min: I · The Ladder (8) · II · The Public Meeting (15) · III · The Press Conference (7) · IV · Letter to the Council (7) · V · The Hot Seat (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Climb the ladder. Six steps, from easy to hard.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

although, despite, in spite of · no matter how, however much · admittedly, granted, having said that, even so · whereas, while, on the other hand, in contrast · due to, as a result, consequently · so that, in order to

THE LADDER

Six steps from easy to hard. A good answer: tap Go up. Not yet: Try again on the same step.

THE PUBLIC MEETING

A board game around the table. Tap Roll the die: the token moves and the square shows a task that mixes the grammar of the unit. The student does it out loud; tap Done and roll again until the end of the board.

LETTER TO THE COUNCIL

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

despite of, although of the rain, due to it was raining, no matter how I try hard, whereas. (as a new sentence), in order to people can walk. B2: one correction per answer is enough.

THE PRESS CONFERENCE (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the public meeting, the roles turn: the student is the mayor who wants to close Main Street, and you are a journalist. Ask the four questions; the student answers in their role, conceding and contrasting with admittedly, having said that, although, due to and so that. Push hard (PUSH line, only you see it) and wait five seconds in silence after each answer. Then the student announces the final decision on the screen. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. In V · The Hot Seat the roles change again: you answer and the student asks. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Ladder and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Public Meeting, III · The Press Conference and V · The Hot Seat.
  • Write: IV · Letter to the Council.
