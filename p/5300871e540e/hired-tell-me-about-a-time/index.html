Tell Me About a Time · WOLF HIRED · Lesson 4 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: behavioral questions (Tell me about a time when…): STAR (situation, task, action, result) with narrative tenses: past continuous for the background (I was working as…), past perfect for what happened before (we had sent…), past simple for actions, and a result.

The story: A recruiter’s example about keeping customers, a fictional story about an angry client to order, then two of the student’s own stories.

About 41 min: I · Always Finish the Story (8) · II · Situation, Task, Action, Result (9) · III · Two Stories, Two Minutes (20) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “What’s the most stressful day you’ve had at work? What happened?”

The aim of the course is three STAR stories ready (a challenge, a conflict, a success). Write the student’s two stories in the chat as they tell them, and correct tenses together at the end.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Last year, I was working as… · One day,… · I had to… · So I… Then I… · As a result,… · What I learned was…

ALWAYS FINISH THE STORY

Self Made Millennial, behavioral questions and the STAR method (6:00–8:00): an example and the three categories.

SITUATION, TASK, ACTION, RESULT

The events are mixed. The student taps them in order; wrong order shows in red. When it’s right, the student tells the whole story with first, then, after that, finally.

TWO STORIES, TWO MINUTES

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to write their third story (a success) using the same four steps.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

We solved (say what YOU did: I called…), When I was work there (was working), They already sent (they had already sent), and that's it (finish with a result). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · Always Finish the Story.
  • Focus: II · Situation, Task, Action, Result and IV · I Can.
  • Out loud: III · Two Stories, Two Minutes.
