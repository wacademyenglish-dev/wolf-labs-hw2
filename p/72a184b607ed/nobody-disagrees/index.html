Nobody Disagrees · The Chairs

Disagreeing in English is mostly about the first three words. You’re wrong is correct grammar, and it pushes the chairs away from the table. I’m not sure that’s right, I see what you mean, but…, Isn’t it a bit early?, Wouldn’t it be better to…? say the same thing and keep everybody sitting down. Students also say I’m not agree. The screen is a meeting table seen from above: hot lines push the six chairs back; careful lines bring them in.

DIRECT IS NOT WRONG

“You’re wrong” is correct English. The lesson is about the temperature, not the grammar. The careful versions still say no.

THE RECORDINGS

Fifteen short recordings from a meeting about a deadline: the same fact three ways (III), three lines going up or down (V), and a six-turn meeting between Anna and Mark (VI). Each one plays with pause and 0.75×; the line appears only after listening.

THE BAD IDEAS

Scene VII: you are the boss. Read each idea, tap the opening you heard, then push back once with the line only you see. Under pressure, students drop the softener: name it.

SCENE I COMES BACK

Write down your student’s first words in scene I. In scene IX they say that same disagreement three ways.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I’m not agree, Is not it…?, Would not it be…?, I don’t agree you, I’m not sure that it work.

SAY IT OR NOT? (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the boss’s four bad ideas, three real moments: a friend’s logo, a wrong number in a meeting, a brother’s car. Your student decides if they speak, and if they do, they say the careful line out loud. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk (watch for “I’m not agree” and “Is not it”); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I The Thing You Don’t Say, II The Chairs, VII Four Bad Ideas, VIII Say It or Not?, IX Your Line, Three Ways.
  • Listen: III One Fact, Three Ways, V Up or Down, VI The Recorded Meeting.
  • Focus: IV Cool It Down.
  • Write: X, their own careful line. It goes to their My notes.
