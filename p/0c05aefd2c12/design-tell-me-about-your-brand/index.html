Tell Me About Your Brand · English for Designers · Lesson 1 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the first client meeting: open questions (Can you tell me about…?), goals (What are you hoping to achieve?), audience (Who is it for?), what exists, likes and dislikes, budget, deadline and approval.

The story: A real agency video with ten brief questions, then a fictional coffee shop (Bloom & Bean) that wants a new logo and menu.

About 39 min: I · Before the Brief (9) · II · Open the Door (10) · III · Your First Client Call (16) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “What do you design? Graphic, UX or interiors? Tell me about your last client or project.”

Ask the student’s area first and adapt the client: for UX, the coffee shop wants an ordering app; for interiors, it wants to redesign the shop. In the client call (cadeira), give short answers so the student has to ask follow-up questions.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Can you tell me about…? · What are you hoping to achieve? · Who is it for? · What exists already? · What don’t you like about…? · Do you have a budget in mind?

BEFORE THE BRIEF

WebFX, 10 questions to ask a client before a design brief (0:35–3:12).

OPEN THE DOOR

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

YOUR FIRST CLIENT CALL

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to bring a real or imagined client for the next lessons. It will be their project until the final pitch.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

What you want? (What do you want?), For who is it? (Who is it for?), What are you hoping achieve? (hoping to achieve), How much is your money? (Do you have a budget in mind?). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · Before the Brief.
  • Focus: II · Open the Door and IV · I Can.
  • Out loud: III · Your First Client Call.
