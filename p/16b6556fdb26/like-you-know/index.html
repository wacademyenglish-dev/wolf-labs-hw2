Better Speaking · Episode 6 · Like, You Know · B2

Episode 6 of Better Speaking. Conchita Martínez, before the Wimbledon final against her childhood hero, speaks with lots of like and you know, and sounds fluent. Richard shows how to buy thinking time without stopping: fillers (you know, I mean, sort of), That’s a good question plus repeating the question, If I were you, I’d, er…, and the weak /&schwa;/ (taller thun). The sister lesson for B1 is “Nothing to Lose”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — Give me a second, Don’t stop. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — Buy time, Match the filler, Fix the traps, Choose the phrase.
  • Write — Your time-buyers. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

Portuguese fillers in English: tipo, né, então. Too many fillers in a row. And the set phrase with the wrong verbs: If I was you, I will…

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, matches and writes. “Only you see this” is yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. The bar on top shows the season: eight episodes, today’s one lit. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
