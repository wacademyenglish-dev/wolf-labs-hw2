Better Speaking · Episode 7 · Before Paris · B2

Episode 7 of Better Speaking. Steffi Graf, after an injury, wins in Paris and reaches another Wimbledon final. Richard shows how she makes the order of events clear, and adds the words for exact timing: as soon as, just as, while, by the time, afterwards. And a strong message: her small mistakes don’t change her meaning. The sister lesson for B1 is “First of All”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — What happened first, Put it in order. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — Put time in its place, Match the ending, Fix the traps, Choose the time word.
  • Write — Your comeback story. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

Future after as soon as: As soon as I will arrive. “Quando cheguei, já…” becomes Until I got there…. And Steffi’s own small slips (word order, prepositions), which are worth noticing but not worth stopping for.
