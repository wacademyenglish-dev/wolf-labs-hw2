Why Us? · WOLF HIRED · Lesson 5 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: answering “Why us?”: specific facts from research, What drew me to… is…, I’ve always been interested in…, I was impressed by…, I’ve been using… for…, This role is a great fit because…, I’d love to be part of…

The story: A former CEO on what recruiters listen for, a fictional bike-rental company page with a model answer, then turning generic answers into specific ones.

About 36 min: I · Not About You, About Them (8) · II · Read the Company Page (10) · III · From Generic to Specific (14) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “Which company would you love to work for? What do you know about it?”

Before the last scene, open the website of the student’s real target company together (5 minutes) and find two specific facts. Then the student answers “Why us?” about that company.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

I’ve always been interested in… · What drew me to … is… · I was impressed by… · I’ve been using … for… · This role is a great fit because… · I’d love to be part of…

NOT ABOUT YOU, ABOUT THEM

The Companies Expert, “Why do you want to work here?” (0:00–3:26).

READ THE COMPANY PAGE

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

FROM GENERIC TO SPECIFIC

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to research their real target company and bring three specific facts.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I always was interested (I’ve always been interested), What called my attention (What caught my attention / What drew me to), I want to work here because I need (focus on them), I am using your app since 2022 (I’ve been using… since 2022). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · Not About You, About Them and II · Read the Company Page.
  • Out loud: III · From Generic to Specific.
  • Focus: IV · I Can.
