Yes, I Do · The Café Tray

Day five of the race. Fran is in Edinburgh, hot and hungry. A man in a café asks her: Do you like soup? She answers with two words: Yes, I do. On screen, a dark café counter and a slate tray: Yes, I do puts the food on the tray, No, I don’t sends it back, and Yes, I like is refused. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

I like / I don’t like + a thing. Do you like …? The short answer sends back do: Yes, I do. / No, I don’t. Ordering: Water, please. The mistakes: Yes, I like. · No, I don’t like. · I no like pizza. · I like of soup. · You like coffee?

THE VIDEO

The real video in two parts: 1:03–1:38 (hot and hungry) and 1:46–2:07 (the question). There is no audio in this lesson: your voice is the model and the waiter.

OK OR NOT OK? (VI) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Between the tray and the counter, three café rules, green or red: pizza for breakfast, hot soup on a 35-degree day (like Fran), a friend who eats your fries. There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it). The starters keep I like / I don’t like in their mouth. Recast quietly while they talk (I no like → I don’t like); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Every Week, Never, VI OK or Not OK? (green or red, then like / don’t like), VII At the Counter (be a real waiter; tap each part you hear), VIII Your Survey (the student asks you).
  • Listen: II Hot and Hungry, IV The Question.
  • Focus: III Two Plates (the sentence comes before the food moves), V The Short Answer (go fast; don’t explain Yes, I like twice).
  • Write: IX, their café, with the red wavy line for Yes, I like., No, I don’t like., I no like, like of, You like …?, Do you likes. It goes to their My notes.
