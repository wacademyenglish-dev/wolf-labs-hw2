Postcards Home · A1 Unit 9 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 9 (Weather, body and comparing). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Three friends are traveling and they call your student tonight. You are Ana in Lisbon, Ben in London and Kim in Tokyo. Then you are the family at dinner.

About 41 min: I · The Ladder (8) · II · Postcards (15) · III · Your Trip (7) · IV · Would You Rather? (7) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “Pick a door.” There is no order. One minute per door, no notes.

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

the weather with it’s · health: I have a…, I feel…, should · and, but, so, because · comparatives · superlatives · good × well

THE LADDER

Six steps from easy to hard. A good answer: tap Go up. Not yet: Try again on the same step.

POSTCARDS

You are each friend on the phone. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the postcard fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the family at dinner: the student tells you the news about one friend. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

WOULD YOU RATHER?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

is very hot (no it), makes cold, I’m with headache, more big, more good, I sleep good. A1: one correction per answer is enough.

YOUR TRIP (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the three phone calls, the student plans their own trip: the weather, the place, the best thing. Answer first, short, so they hear the sentence. Then let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it): a yes / no or one-word question. Recast mistakes quietly (“It’s hot” for “is hot”); at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one light personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Ladder and V · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · Postcards, III · Your Trip and IV · Would You Rather?.
