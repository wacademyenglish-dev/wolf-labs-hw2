The Hollywood Fixer · The Spotlight

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: Your student is a fixer on a film set.

The grammar: The Missing Person: Same words. One person of difference.

Short class (about 52 min): I The Desk · II Four Voicemails · III The Missing Person · IV The Spotlight · V Set Notes · VI Say Takes Nobody · VIII Three Crises · IX The Press Conference. Skip if you’re short on time: VII (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 58 min.

Open with: “What does your family want you to do this year?”

Your student is a fixer on a film set. At eleven a photo of the star, Nadia, leaked, and a sentence with a missing person almost cost her her name. The language is want / ask / tell + person + to. The person in the gap does the second verb; an empty gap sends it back to the first person (she asked to delete = she deletes). And say never takes a person.

THE PAGES

  • III The Missing Person: six lines in three pairs, side by side. The student reads each out loud, taps a line and then the person who does the second verb.
  • V Set Notes: six notes. The student builds how they pass each one on, out loud, then taps Check and says it to you as if you were the person. The rule is in the corner.
  • VI Say Takes Nobody: six lines, Right or Wrong. If a line is wrong, the student says it right.
  • VIII Three Crises: three crisis cards. The student solves each one out loud (what the director wants, what you tell the crew, what you ask the studio) and you play everybody. Then tap the twist and react to it.

WHO DOES IT?

When your student is stuck, ask one question: Who does the second verb?

THE SPOTLIGHT

Scene IV: let the light stop on the wrong person before you say anything. The light is the correction.

NOBODY LIED

Scene VII: Theo didn’t lie. He left a person out. Don’t let it turn into a debate about honesty; the answer is grammatical.

YOU PLAY EVERYBODY

Scene VIII: you are the director, the actors and the reporter. React to the student’s orders as if they were real.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

The studio wants that she says nothing, she told me do it, he said me, told you don’t come.

THE PRESS CONFERENCE (IX) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

The lesson now ends at the studio gate: your student is the fixer, you are the reporters. Ask the five questions one by one (only you see your secret: the phone log). Wait five seconds in silence after each question, then use the PUSH line to make it harder. Every answer needs a person in the gap; recast quietly while they talk, and at the end ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. To close, they choose the one sentence for the headline and say why. The writing from the old scene IX now lives in the homework (send the link at the end).

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I The Desk, V Set Notes, VIII Three Crises, IX The Press Conference.
  • Listen: II Four Voicemails (the transcript opens after they listen).
  • Focus: III The Missing Person, IV The Spotlight, VI Say Takes Nobody, VII Who Asked Who.
  • Write: in the homework (send the link at the end of IX).
