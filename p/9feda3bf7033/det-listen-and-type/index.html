Listen and Type · DET Prep · Lesson 6 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the Listen and Type question: the student hears one sentence (up to three times) and types it exactly. Fast speech hides things: linked words (wanna), weak small words (of sounds like “a”), quiet endings (-ed, -s) and words with the same sound (they’re / their).

The story: No story: eight sentences from everyday life, in five accents (American, British, Australian, Indian, Irish, Canadian), recorded with neural voices.

About 37 min: I · Where Words Hide (9) · II · Eight Sentences (16) · III · Memory (8) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: Say fast: “I wanna go.” Ask: “How do you write it?” The answer is the lesson.

The audio plays up to three times, like the test. Teach the three listens: meaning, details, check. Punctuation does not count in our check; spelling does.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

want to / going to (not wanna / gonna) · weak small words: of, a, to · quiet -ed and -s · they’re / their / there · to / too · your / you’re · three listens: meaning, details, check

WHERE WORDS HIDE

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

EIGHT SENTENCES

Listen and Type: the audio plays up to 3 times, like the test. The student types, then Check compares word by word: green is right, red is missing or misspelled. Read the tip out loud.

MEMORY

A memory game. The student opens two cards; when they match, the student makes a sentence with the pair (example under Show answer).

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. If the student misses small words, play the sentence again and ask them to count the words first.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

wanna, a cup a tea, yesterday I walk, their late, your late. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · Where Words Hide and II · Eight Sentences and III · Memory.
  • Focus: IV · I Can.
