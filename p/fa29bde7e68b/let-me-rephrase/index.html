Let Me Rephrase · C1 Unit 8 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on rephrasing (in other words, that is to say, what I mean is, to put it another way / simply / bluntly, or rather, let me rephrase that) and on asking for clarity (what exactly do you mean by…?, are you saying that…?, so, if I understand correctly…).

The story: A financial adviser (invented) explains a bad quarter to a confused client with technical words, and has to rephrase again and again.

About 47 min: I · Lost in Explanation (6) · II · The Appointment (10) · III · Same Idea, Clearer (9) · IV · Speed Round (6) · V · Who Explains Worst? (7) · VI · Explain It to Me (9).

Open with: “Tell me about a time an expert explained something you didn’t understand.”

C1 speakers are not perfect: they repair. Show the student that rephrasing is a skill, not a failure. Watch the Portuguese traps: “ou seja” → or be, “quer dizer” → want to say. In the hot seat, explain YOUR job in a deliberately complicated way, so the student has to ask for clarity.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

In other words / That is to say · What I mean is… · To put it another way / simply / bluntly · or rather (correcting yourself) · Let me rephrase that · What exactly do you mean by…? / Are you saying that…?

LOST IN EXPLANATION

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

THE APPOINTMENT

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

SAME IDEA, CLEARER

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

SPEED ROUND

A 60-second speed round. Tap Start the clock, then Right or Pass for each item; the answer is on your screen. At the end of class, play again and beat the score.

EXPLAIN IT TO ME

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

or be (for ou seja), what I want to say is, in other way, what do you want to say with that, in another words, I didn’t understand (only). C1: one correction per answer is enough.

WHO EXPLAINS WORST? (V) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

In the middle of the lesson, your student ranks five people from the hardest to understand to the easiest (a bank manager, IT support, a lawyer, a mechanic, a teenager explaining an app) and explains their top and last with in other words, what I mean is, to put it simply. There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence, then tap Show my teacher’s order, defend yours, and agree on one top 3 together. Use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it personal. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I · Lost in Explanation, V · Who Explains Worst? and VI · Explain It to Me.
  • Listen: II · The Appointment.
  • Focus: III · Same Idea, Clearer and IV · Speed Round.
