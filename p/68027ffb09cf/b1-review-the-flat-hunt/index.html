The Flat Hunt · B1 Unit 8 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 8 (Describing and choosing). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student needs a new flat and visits three: a city center flat, a garden flat and a top-floor flat. You are each owner. In the end, the student tells a friend which flat they’d rather take.

About 49 min: I · Six Doors (8) · II · Three Flats (15) · III · The Story in Order (7) · IV · The Residents’ Meeting (7) · V · The Hot Seat (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Six doors. Choose one, open it, and do the challenge.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

who, which, that · where · make, let, help + person + verb · stop + -ing · hear / see + someone + -ing · would rather, prefer

SIX DOORS

Six doors, one per topic of the unit. The student picks any door and does the task from memory. Tap Got it or Review; amber doors come back as a short list “to review”.

THREE FLATS

You are each owner. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the friend: the student describes the flat with which / where / who and says I’d rather… Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

THE STORY IN ORDER

The events are mixed. The student taps them in order; wrong order shows in red. When it’s right, the student tells the whole story with first, then, after that, finally.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

the flat what has a garden, the street where I live there, she makes you to recycle, I’d rather to live, I heard a dog to bark, I prefer cook than order. B1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE RESIDENTS’ MEETING (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

The student has moved in; now it is the first building meeting. Three proposals (dogs, music after 8 p.m., a barbecue roof) and the student votes Yes, Propose a change or No, with make / let, stop + -ing and who / which / where. You are the neighbor who wants the opposite. Wait five seconds in silence after each vote, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Six Doors and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · Three Flats, III · The Story in Order, IV · The Residents’ Meeting and V · The Hot Seat.
