The Crimson Diamond · The Case Board

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: At 10:04 PM the alarm rings at the city museum and the Crimson Diamond is gone.

The grammar: An Alibi Is a Tense: Five cards. Each one asks for a sentence.

Short class (about 45 min): I Your Alibi · II An Alibi Is a Tense · III The Question · IV Keep the -ing · V Suspect One: Arthur · VI Suspect Two: Eleanor · XI Case Closed. Skip if you’re short on time: VII, VIII, IX, X (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 72 min.

Open with: “What were you doing yesterday at 10 PM?”

At 10:04 PM the alarm rings at the city museum and the Crimson Diamond is gone. Three people were inside: Arthur, the night guard; Eleanor, a guest; Marco, the barman. Your student interviews all three, puts red strings on the board and finds the alibi that doesn’t close. The language is the past continuous for alibis: What were you doing at ten? I was + -ing, I wasn’t …, Were you …? Yes, I was.

TWO PIECES

When your student is stuck, ask: Where is the first piece (was / were)? Where is the -ing?

YOU ARE MR. SMITH

In scene IX you play Eleanor’s husband. Say you were getting champagne at the bar. Your student knows the bar was shut. Let them catch you, and only then let them name the thief.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

What you were doing? (the helper didn’t jump), I was sleep (no -ing), He working (no was) and They was.

THE JURY (X) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After your student catches Mr. Smith, they decide what is fair for Arthur, Eleanor and Marco. There is no right answer: let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. The last word is one minute on Mr. Smith’s excuse, then you play his lawyer.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Your Alibi, IX The Husband, X The Jury.
  • Focus: II–IV the pieces and the question, VIII The Board.
  • Listen: V–VII the three suspects (the transcript opens after they listen).
  • Write: XI, their alibis for yesterday. It goes to their My notes.
