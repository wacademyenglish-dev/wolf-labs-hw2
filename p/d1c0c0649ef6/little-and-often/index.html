Better Speaking · Episode 2 · Little and Often · B2

Episode 2 of Better Speaking. Korean football coach Park Hang-seo talks about fans who prefer the “beer shop” to the stadium. Richard shows two things: talking your way around a word you don’t know, and stress: sentence stress that changes the meaning (a BIG black cat / a big BLACK cat) and word stress (FOOTball, teLEvision). The title is his advice: practice little and often. The sister lesson for B1 is “What Do You Call It?”. No points, no timer.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — How do you practice, The beer shop trick. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — Stress is meaning, Hear the contrast, Fix the traps, Where is the stress.
  • Write — Your little and often plan. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

Portuguese word stress carried into English: deve-LOP, comfor-TA-ble, ho-TEL said as Portuguese. Flat sentence stress, where every word weighs the same. And stopping when the word doesn’t come, instead of explaining around it.
