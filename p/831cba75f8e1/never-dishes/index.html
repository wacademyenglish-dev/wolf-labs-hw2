You Never Do the Dishes · Seven Squares

Two people, one small apartment, one bad week. Marco and Bea argue with two words: never and always. Then somebody opens the week and counts. Nobody was right. The hour teaches the four words in the middle as the words you need to be fair. On screen, the week is graph paper: seven squares you can count. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

always 7 · usually 5–6 · often 3–4 · sometimes 1–2 · rarely <1 · never 0 (a guide, not a rule). The place: before a normal verb (she always makes), after am / is / are (he is never late). never and rarely are already negative. The mistakes: she makes always, he never is, I don’t never, sometime.

THE AUDIO

The fight (four lines) and the fair sentences (four lines) are real recordings, with pause and 0.75×; the words appear after listening. The speakers are British: they say rubbish; the American word is trash.

YOUR CALL (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the evidence, three light choices, one of three each: who does the dishes in your student’s home, who is more right in the fight (Marco, Bea or nobody), and one rule for their fridge door. There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) for a yes/no follow-up. The starters keep the word before the verb (I usually wash) and after BE (They are never fair). Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Your Week (numbers only; write them down, they come back in IX), III Seven Squares (count out loud before each sentence), VI The Evidence (let the silence happen after the first one), VIII Your Call (choose one of three, three times).
  • Listen: II The Argument (say it back with the same angry face), VII Say It Fairly.
  • Focus: IV Before the Verb and V Except With BE (don’t merge them).
  • Write: IX, their own week, with the red wavy line for the wrong place, don’t never and sometime. It goes to their My notes.
