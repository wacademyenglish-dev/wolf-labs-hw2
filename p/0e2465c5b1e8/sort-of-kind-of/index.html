Better Speaking · Episode 8 · Sort Of, Kind Of · B2

Episode 8, the last one of Better Speaking. ABBA explain their name and why Swedes speak such good English. Richard shows vague language: sort of, kind of, -ish (reddish, ten-ish, “Ish.”), about, like, more or less, or something, and stuff. It closes the season, so leave time for the student’s favorite tip. The sister lesson for B1 is “Eight-ish”. No points, no timer.
