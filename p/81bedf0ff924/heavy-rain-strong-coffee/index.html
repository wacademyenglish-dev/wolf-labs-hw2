Heavy Rain, Strong Coffee · C1 Unit 1 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on collocations (adjective / adverb + noun or adjective): heavy rain, strong coffee, deeply grateful, bitterly cold, highly recommended.

The story: A travel blog, The Wandering Fork (invented): 48 hours in Lisbon under heavy rain. The student reads it, finds the word pairs, upgrades plain sentences and writes a review.

About 56 min: I · Your Weather, Your Coffee (7) · II · The Blog (10) · III · Rainy Day Ranking (7) · IV · Which One Sounds Native? (9) · V · Upgrade It (7) · VI · Memory (8) · VII · Your Review (8).

Open with: “Before the words: tell me about your weather and your coffee today.”

C1 students already have the grammar. What they lack is the word a native would choose. Don’t teach a list: let the student meet the pairs in the blog, notice them, and then use them. Portuguese pushes them to “strong rain” and “very grateful”: that is the target of today.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

heavy + rain, traffic, smoker, workload · strong + coffee, accent, wind, opinion · deeply + grateful, moved, concerned · bitterly + cold, disappointed · highly + recommended, unlikely, qualified · a narrow escape, a quick bite

YOUR WEATHER, YOUR COFFEE

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

THE BLOG

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

WHICH ONE SOUNDS NATIVE?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

UPGRADE IT

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

MEMORY

A memory game. The student opens two cards; when they match, the student makes a sentence with the pair (example under Show answer).

YOUR REVIEW

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

strong rain, very grateful (weak: deeply grateful), very very cold (bitterly cold), I very recommend, highly cold, a strong traffic. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

RAINY DAY RANKING (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the blog: five plans for a rainy Saturday in Lisbon. Your student ranks them (1 is the first choice) and defends the top one and the last one with word pairs from the blog. There is no right order. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it). Then tap Show my teacher’s order and agree on one top 3 together. Recast quietly while they talk (heavy rain, not strong rain); at the end ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I · Your Weather, Your Coffee and III · Rainy Day Ranking.
  • Listen: II · The Blog and VI · Memory.
  • Focus: IV · Which One Sounds Native? and V · Upgrade It.
  • Write: VII · Your Review.
