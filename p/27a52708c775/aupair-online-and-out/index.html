Online and Out · Au Pair · Lesson 16 of 24

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: safety online (no photos of the kids without permission, camera location off, no addresses or routines) and outside (ride-share checks, telling someone where you go, phone charged, drinks, meeting new people in public places). Polite language: Can I…, or would you prefer…? Just so you know… I’d rather not…

The story: A TODAY report: a cyber safety expert finds a family’s private details from their posts (we show only 1:03–3:50). Then twelve situations and polite ways to ask and inform.

About 34 min: I · What a Post Can Show (10) · II · Safe or Not Safe? (12) · III · Ask Politely (8) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “How many photos did you post last week? What could a stranger learn from them?”

The full report starts with a mention of an inappropriate website; the lesson plays only 1:03–3:50. Keep the tone practical, not scary. Many host families have written social media rules: the student should ask about them in the first week.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Can I share this, or would you prefer I didn’t? · location off · Just so you know… · I’m running late · I’d rather not… · Could you please take that down?

WHAT A POST CAN SHOW

TODAY, “Why You Should Rethink Posting Photos Of Your Children On Social Media”, only 1:03–3:50 (captions checked on 15/10/2026). TV news speed: play it twice.

SAFE OR NOT SAFE?

One situation at a time. The student says which side it goes on and WHY, then taps. The color confirms and one line explains. The two columns build a board the student can photograph at the end.

ASK POLITELY

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to check her camera location setting today, in class, with you.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

posting first, asking later, sharing addresses or routines, getting into a car without checking. B1: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · What a Post Can Show.
  • Focus: II · Safe or Not Safe? and IV · I Can.
  • Out loud: III · Ask Politely.
