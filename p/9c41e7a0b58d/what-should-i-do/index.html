What Should I Do? · The On-Air Desk

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: What do I do?

The grammar: No To, No S: Should never changes. No TO. No S. No DO.

Short class (about 52 min): I Your Problem · II Last Night’s Show · III No To, No S · IV Two Different Questions · V The Letter Stack · VI Asking for Advice · VIII The Big Decision · X Off Air. Skip if you’re short on time: VII, IX (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 66 min.

Open with: “What’s one small problem you have this week?”

What do I do? is correct English, but it asks about a task, not for advice. This hour teaches What should I do?, Should I…?, You should… and You shouldn’t…. Your student hosts a late-night radio advice show: letters arrive in a stack, and every letter answered with one should and one shouldn’t pushes a fader up. Then the phone lines open and you call in.

SHOULD NEVER MOVES

You should to go, she shoulds, they don’t should: the classic three. Cover everything after should and ask what is left.

THE FADERS

Scenes V and VII: tap SHOULD and SHOULDN’T when you hear them. The faders are for fun; the two sentences are the point.

YOU CALL IN

Scene VII: you are five callers. Keep the problems small and real, and push back once (Oh, but…) so your student gives a second piece of advice.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

What do I do? (asking for advice), What I should do?, should to, shoulds, don’t should, and advice that rises at the end and sounds unsure.

THE BIG DECISION (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Three new callers (Wendy, Tom and Lucy), each with three choices. Your student picks the best one for the caller and says why with should and shouldn’t. There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence after each question, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast quietly while they talk (should to go → should go); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Your Problem, V The Letter Stack, VII The Phone Lines, VIII The Big Decision, IX Your Turn to Call.
  • Listen: II Last Night’s Show (the transcript opens after they listen).
  • Focus: III No To, No S, IV Two Different Questions, VI Asking for Advice.
  • Write: X, a letter to the show and its answer. It goes to their My notes.
