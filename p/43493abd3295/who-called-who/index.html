Who Called Who · The Round Table

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: Your student’s first language often asks both questions with no helper verb, so they build Who did call Caio?

The grammar: The Rule: Doer: ask the sentence. Other one: bring in DID.

Short class (about 47 min): I Ten People, One Bill · II Who Did What · III Two Questions · IV The Rule · V Bea Asks · VI The Question Mark · IX The Message. Skip if you’re short on time: VII, VIII (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 60 min.

Open with: “The last time you went out with a group: who chose the place?”

Your student’s first language often asks both questions with no helper verb, so they build Who did call Caio? and Who Marina called?. English has two shapes. When WHO is the doer, the sentence already is the question: Who called Caio? When WHO is the other person, DID comes in and the verb goes bare: Who did Marina call? Ten friends had dinner, and the bill is still Bea’s. The screen is the round table seen from above, with arrows from the doer to the other person.

THE ARROW

When your student is stuck, point: Are you asking about the start of the arrow, or the end?

SAME FACT, TWO ANSWERS

Scene V: Who texted Davi? (Iuri) and Who did Iuri text? (Davi). Let your student feel the difference.

BEA’S NOTEBOOK

Scene VIII: you hold the answers (they are on your screen). Your student asks; you answer and tap Answered, and the arrows appear. Caio never paid.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Who did call you?, Who you called?, Who did Sara drove home?, What did happen?

THE NEW EXERCISES

Scene IV: the student matches each rule to its example, and only then the rule cards open. Scene VI: the table shows an arrow with a question mark; the student says the question out loud and taps Check (the rule stays in the corner). Scene VII: find the broken part of six questions and say the fix.

THE NEXT DINNER (VI) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Scene VI is now a light negotiation: your student is Bea planning next Friday, and you are Marina. Read each of Marina’s lines; your student answers with the right question (Who ordered…?, Who did you invite?, What happened?). Your secret (only you see it): Marina ordered the three desserts; confess it with the PUSH, then close the deal together. Wait five seconds in silence before they ask. If you hear Who did order…?, just point at the arrow and let them fix it; at the end, ask them to fix one or two questions themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Ten People, One Bill, III Two Questions, VI The Next Dinner, VIII Who Didn’t Pay?
  • Listen: V Bea Asks (the transcript opens after they answer).
  • Focus: II Who Did What, IV The Rule, VII One Word In, One Word Out.
  • Write: IX, a message to Caio. It goes to their My notes.
