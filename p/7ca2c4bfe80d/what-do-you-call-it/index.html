Better Speaking · Episode 2 · What Do You Call It? · B1

Episode 2 of Better Speaking, B1 version. Park Hang-seo doesn’t know the word for a bar, so he says “beer shop”, and everybody understands. The language is how to explain a word you don’t know: a thing you use to…, a place where…, a person who…, you know, when…. The sister lesson for B2 is “Little and Often”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — When the word doesn’t come, Don’t say the word. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — Explain it with other words, What is it, Fix the traps, Choose the right word.
  • Write — Three secret words. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

“Uma coisa que serve para” becomes a thing that serves for. People get which: a person which fixes cars. And places get that with no preposition: a place that you buy bread.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, matches and writes. “Only you see this” is yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. The bar on top shows the season: eight episodes, today’s one lit. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
