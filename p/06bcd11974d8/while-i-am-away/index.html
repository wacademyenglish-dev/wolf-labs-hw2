While I Am Away · The Live Cameras

Marta leaves her farm for a week, and the student looks after it. On the fridge, a page of routines (every day). On the screen, four LIVE cameras (now): the red dot blinks, the clock runs, the rain falls. On day three, the other house-sitter’s answers give him away: he copied the page and was never there. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

I feed / he feeds = every day (one piece). I am / he is / they are + -ing = now (two pieces). The verbs a camera can’t film take no -ing: want, need, know, see, understand. Two questions: What do you do? (job) × What are you doing? (this minute). Portuguese pushes He feeding and I am work every day.

THE OTHER HOUSE-SITTER (VII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after Day Three, your student chooses three times about the other house-sitter (sleeping or working at noon, watching TV or helping, not there on day three). There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) for a yes/no follow-up. The starters keep both presents in their mouth (He works at six / Nobody is feeding the horses). Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — I You, Live; VII The Other House-Sitter (choose, then say it); VIII The Video Call (first you are the owner and ask; then swap).
  • Listen — V The First Reports and VI Day Three (voice messages: NOW or EVERY DAY; THERE or NOT THERE). Play Marta first.
  • Focus — II Four Cameras, One Page; III Two Presents; IV The Live Caption (BE, then the -ing form).
  • Write — IX Your Report to Marta, with the red wavy line for He feeding, I am work, I am wanting, -ing + every day and he feed. It goes to their My notes.

THE MYSTERY

In Day Three, messages 3 and 5 are live with no -ing (I see a fox, I need more hay): that’s the stative rule doing its job. Let the student notice the routines before you say it. Then the page and the full buckets close the case.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps and writes on their own screen; the camera clock shows each person’s own time. G opens this guide.
