My Weekend · Au Pair · Lesson 22 of 24

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: life outside the house: telling the weekend in the past simple (went, visited, met, ate), planning a trip with going to and will, and including a safety plan.

The story: Wiktoria, a real au pair from Poland living near Chicago (Au Pair in America vlog), spends a weekend in New York with friends. Then the Monday morning chat with the host family and a weekend plan.

About 36 min: I · Wiktoria in New York (10) · II · How Was Your Weekend? (10) · III · Your Weekend Plan (12) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “What did you do last weekend?” Listen for the past simple.

The planned vlog (Ola’s) has no captions, so we use Wiktoria’s weekend in New York. The full weekend off every month runs from Friday evening to Monday morning: a perfect time for trips.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

I went / we visited / I met / we ate · It was great! · I’m going to… · Maybe we’ll… · by train / by bus · I’ll share my location with…

WIKTORIA IN NEW YORK

Au Pair in America, “Life with APiA | Wiktoria’s 4th vlog” (6 min, captions checked on 15/10/2026). Natural speech by a Polish speaker: good model of “real” non-native English.

HOW WAS YOUR WEEKEND?

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

YOUR WEEKEND PLAN

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to list three places she wants to visit near the city of her future family.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I go (for the past), we visit (for the past), I’m going to went, I will to go. A2+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · Wiktoria in New York.
  • Out loud: II · How Was Your Weekend?.
  • Write: III · Your Weekend Plan.
  • Focus: IV · I Can.
