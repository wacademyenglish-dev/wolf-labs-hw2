Bear That in Mind · C1 Unit 1 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on formal collocations: carry out a review, raise a concern, address an issue, draw a conclusion, reach an agreement, meet a deadline, pose a risk, bear in mind, take into account.

The story: Ana Reyes (invented) emails her teams before the review of the Atlas project. Then the student chairs the meeting: you are Finance (Carla), Engineering (Dev) and Marketing (Sam).

About 55 min: I · The Email (10) · II · Which Verb? (9) · III · Make It Formal (7) · IV · Find the Mistakes (7) · V · The Atlas Jury (7) · VI · The Meeting (15).

Open with: “Before an important meeting, what do you usually send or read?”

The student knows the nouns. The problem is the verb: Portuguese gives “take conclusions”, “reach the deadline”, “lift a concern”. Let the email show the pairs, let the student notice them, then the meeting makes them use the verbs under a little pressure.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

carry out a review / a survey · raise a concern, address an issue · draw a conclusion, reach an agreement · meet a deadline · pose a risk · bear in mind, take into account

THE EMAIL

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

WHICH VERB?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

MAKE IT FORMAL

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

THE MEETING

You are each team. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the director: the student sums up each team with the formal verbs. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

take conclusions, reach the deadline, lift a concern, do a review (too informal), have in mind, take in consideration. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE ATLAS JURY (V) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Before the meeting, three small cases from the Atlas project, each with evidence: the missed deadline (Dev or Ana?), Sam’s posters printed before the date was agreed, and Carla’s business-class trip. Your student gives a verdict and defends it in formal English (Bearing in mind that…, I’ve drawn the conclusion that…). There is no right verdict. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it). Recast quietly while they talk (raise a concern, not lift); at the end ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. This lesson has no personal opening, so use one early, during the email.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · The Email.
  • Focus: II · Which Verb? and III · Make It Formal and IV · Find the Mistakes.
  • Out loud: V · The Atlas Jury and VI · The Meeting.
