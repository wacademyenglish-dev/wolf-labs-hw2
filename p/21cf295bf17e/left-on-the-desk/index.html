Left on the Desk · The Compressor

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: A colleague left yesterday.

The grammar: The Desk at 7:50: Your colleague left yesterday. A meeting starts at 9 AM. The handover is one page. Read it cold.

Short class (about 52 min): I Your Desk · II The Desk at 7:50 · III The Memo · IV The Compressor · V Does It or Receives It? · VI Who Did It? · VIII The Hot Seat · IX Nine O’Clock. Skip if you’re short on time: VII (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 59 min.

Open with: “Where did it come from?”

A colleague left yesterday. A meeting starts at nine. The handover is one page long, and every line is missing two words. The proposal discussed yesterday was rejected = the proposal that was discussed. Students read discussed as a past tense and think the proposal discussed something. The rule: cut who / which / that + be, move nothing else. -ING: the noun does it. -ED: the noun receives it, and the person who did it disappears unless by keeps them. No BE, no cut. On screen, a compressor: the sentence is a rail of word blocks with a word counter; cutting the right pair makes the blocks slide together; cutting a wrong block cracks the rail.

LET THEM FALL IN

Scene II: let the student answer before any grammar. “The memo doesn’t say” is a correct answer.

THE RECORDING

Scene III: the colleague’s voice memo, with pause and 0.75×; the transcript opens after listening. Play it as often as needed. Lines 2 and 5 are the same trap.

THE COMPRESSOR

Scene IV: the student reads the long version, taps the blocks to cut, then reads the short one. Two sentences have nothing to cut; say so. Cutting who in “the woman who called you” makes a new sentence.

THE SCENE

Scene VII: a mystery in an office at 7 AM, with four evidence markers on the photo. Push for detail: which one? left where? found by whom?

THE HOT SEAT (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Next to Ana’s handover there was an envelope marked Private, and your student read it: a job offer from another company. You are Mr. Hale, the director. Ask the five questions on the screen (Next question), in role, and decide on the spot how hard to press; your secret goal is under your badge. Your student decides how much to tell. There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence after each question, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) when they protect Ana. Recast quietly into compressed phrases (the letter which was left → the letter left); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. Close with Your decision: what they do when Ana comes back.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Your Desk, VII The Scene, VIII The Hot Seat.
  • Listen: III The Memo.
  • Focus: II The Desk at 7:50, IV The Compressor, V Does It or Receives It?, VI Who Did It?
  • Write: IX, their object from scene I and their own handover, compressed. It goes to their My notes.
