Look at My Pictures · Three Frames

The race from London to Edinburgh: Bob is on a train, Fran is on a bike, and Paula is still in London, taking photos. The lesson is her album. Three frames are drawn on the screen: AT is a pin on the map (one point), IN is a box around you, ON is a surface or a line under you. A photo only sticks in the right frame; in the wrong one, it falls off. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

at / in / on with places: at the shops, at work · in London, in the park, in the kitchen · on the bus, on a bridge, on the street. Home takes no word: I’m home. And very comes before the word: very big. The common mistakes: in the bus, at London, at home / go to home, big very. A taxi or a car is small: you sit in it.

YOUR PICK (IX) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after Paula’s video, three short choices, one of three each: where you are on a free day in London, the best place for Paula’s next photo, and where you are on Saturday at ten. There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it). The starters keep at / in / on (and home with no word) in their mouth. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Your Last Photo, II The Race, VI Very (zoom and say it bigger), VII Paula’s Album, IX Your Pick (one of three, then the sentence), X Your Three Photos.
  • Listen: II, IV and VII have audio with pause and 0.75×. The student hears first; the transcript opens after. Every transcript was checked against the audio.
  • Focus: V The Three Frames (eight photos to drop).
  • Write: XI Your Album, five captions, with the red wavy line for in the bus, at London, at home, in work, in a bridge, on the park and big very. It goes to their My notes.

THE VIDEO

Two pieces of the same video: 0:02–1:10 (the start of the race) and 2:16–3:20 (Paula in London). Before: a guess. During: tap the six words. After: one question, then the transcript.

SCENE I

Don’t correct. Tap the word the student used (in, on, at, or none). Scene XI brings it back: “In scene I your teacher heard…”.
