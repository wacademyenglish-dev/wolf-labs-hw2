I’m Not an Astronaut · Mission Control

A science teacher wakes up alone on a spaceship (Project Hail Mary, 2026). The screen is mission control: the crew of the Hail Mary is off the radar, and the student is the controller on duty. They scan six rooms of the ship and build the report for Earth, line by line: Nobody is in the lab. Somebody left the microscope on. The hour has ONE target: English says no only once. Portuguese says it twice (não vi ninguém), so “I didn’t see nobody” WILL come out.

Warm-up and The Interview (I): two parts. 1 · Warm-up: three questions about the student’s life, one at a time (big on the screen, with “Ask more” under each and one short line about the film under the photo). Let them talk; A2: short answers are fine. 2 · The Interview: you are Ryland Grace: the card with his facts shows only on your screen (dashed border). You just woke up on a spaceship and you remember almost nothing. The student asks questions and taps each one; answer with somebody / nobody / nothing (“Nobody is here.”). Then the student guesses who you are, and the photo shows the answer.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — I Crew Files (six people from their life; tap who they named), VI Earth Is Calling (you are Eva on the phone), VII Off the Record.
  • Listen — III Incoming Transmission: three guesses, the official trailer, then ONE screen, the Mission debrief: the video stays on the left, six True or Lie lines on the right (tap ▶ to hear that moment again), and a ship map that lights one room for every right answer (“Crew found 3 / 6”). On a Lie the student says the true line. When all six are done, Eva asks one last question (“Would you go?”): the student taps an answer and says the sentence out loud.
  • Focus — III Decode the Signal (six real lines), IV One No Is Enough (four cards and three traps), V Scan the Ship (the student builds every report line from pieces; one extra piece is always the trap, and Check explains it without giving the answer).
  • Write — VIII The Mission Report. The red wavy line marks the double no, nobody didn’t, anybody in a yes sentence, nobody in a question and nobody are; the browser marks spelling. It goes to their My notes.

THE TRAP

In scene I, collect the double noes (“I don’t call nobody”) and say nothing: write them down. The rule comes in IV, from the trailer line “I’ve never done anything”. In VI, a no can never repeat: if it does, tap “Same no again”.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student opens the files, flips the cards, decodes, builds the reports and writes. Only you judge the speech (“who did they name?”, “Said it”, “Answered”). Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
