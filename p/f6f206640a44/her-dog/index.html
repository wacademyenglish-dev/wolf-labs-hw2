Her Dog · The Waiting Room

An easy reader in five chapters, and the title is the wrong sentence. A vet’s waiting room on a dark Tuesday morning: a woman holds the leash of a big old dog, and for four chapters everybody in the room (and your student) says her dog. In chapter five the vet reads the name on the card, and it is a man’s name. The story is the one rebuilt on 23/09; in the Live version the student reads line by line, makes a bet that comes back in chapter five, watches the patient card fill in, and turns the dog’s tag with a finger.

THE GRAMMAR

His, her, their, its choose by the owner, never by the thing. In Portuguese the possessive agrees with the thing, so his mother about a woman’s mother is not carelessness: it is the first language. Say this once, then let the scenes work. No S on the word (her books, never hers books); its has no apostrophe. When you don’t know the owner, English won’t let you finish: Whose is it?

IN THE WAITING ROOM (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Back in the waiting room, your student chooses between two things for three people: his bag on the chair or on the floor, her phone old or new, their cookies big or small. No right answer. Answer first yourself, as a model. Then let your student choose and say the whole sentence with his / her / their. Wait five seconds in silence, and ask the PUSH line (only you see it): a yes / no question. Recast quietly while they talk (a man with her bag → his bag). About 5 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. There are three; use one when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Your People (a diagnosis: don’t correct), the five chapters (read line by line), III Whose Dog?
  • Focus: V Find the Owner First (tap the owner before the word appears), VI Eight Cards, VIII OK or Not OK? (out loud), X You Have to Know First.
  • Write: XII Six of Your Own, with the red wavy line for it’s name, hers brothers and a man or woman with the wrong word in the same sentence. It goes to their My notes.

CHAPTER FIVE

Let the silence happen before you explain anything. The bet from scene III comes back on the screen. Only the bet “Nobody in this room knows yet” wins.
