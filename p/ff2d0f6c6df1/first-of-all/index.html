Better Speaking · Episode 7 · First of All · B1

Episode 7 of Better Speaking, B1 version. Steffi Graf tells her month in order: first Paris, then Wimbledon. Callum puts Richard’s morning in order. The language is sequencers: first of all, then, after that, finally. The sister lesson for B2 is “Before Paris”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — Your day, Fix the order. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — In order, Put Richard’s day in order, Fix the traps, Choose the right word.
  • Write — Your perfect day. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

“Depois” at the start becomes After, I went…. Firstly of all. And “no final” becomes in the final.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, matches and writes. “Only you see this” is yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. The bar on top shows the season: eight episodes, today’s one lit. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
