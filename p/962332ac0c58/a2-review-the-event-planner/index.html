The Event Planner · A2 Unit 6 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 6 (Plans and predictions). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student plans events. You are three clients: a couple with a garden wedding, a grandmother turning 80, and a manager with an office party. Then you are the team.

About 45 min: I · Six Doors (8) · II · The Event Planner (14) · III · On the Day (6) · IV · Your Agenda (6) · V · Would You Rather? (7) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Pick a door.” There is no order. One minute per door, no notes.

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

at / on / in with time · prepositions of movement · going to for plans · will for quick decisions, promises and predictions · present continuous for arrangements · predictions with will / might

SIX DOORS

Six doors, one per topic of the unit. The student picks any door and does the task from memory. Tap Got it or Review; amber doors come back as a short list “to review”.

THE EVENT PLANNER

You are each client. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the team: the student explains the plan with at / on / in, going to and will. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

YOUR AGENDA

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

WOULD YOU RATHER?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

in Sunday, at June, I will to call, enter in the room, I will meet him at ten (booked), I think it rains tomorrow. A2: one correction per answer is enough.

ON THE DAY (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the client meetings, something goes wrong at each event. Your student chooses green (go on), yellow (fix it) or red (change the plan) and decides on the spot with I’ll… There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it). Listen for will (decision now) × going to (plan). Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Six Doors and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Event Planner, III · On the Day and V · Would You Rather?.
  • Write: IV · Your Agenda.
