The Casting · A2 Unit 8 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 8 (Done, not yet). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: A TV travel show needs a new host. Your student is the producer at the casting. You are three candidates: Luana, Omar and Sam. Then you are the director.

About 49 min: I · The Die (8) · II · The Casting (14) · III · The First Trip (7) · IV · Memory (8) · V · The Hot Seat (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Roll the die. The number chooses the first challenge.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

Have you ever…? · the detail in the past (I went there in 2019) · the right participle · already / yet / just · since / for · never, once, twice

THE DIE

Tap Roll the die: the number chooses one of the six topics (no repeats until all six are done). The student does the challenge from memory.

THE CASTING

You are each candidate. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the director: the student tells you about one candidate with the present perfect and the past. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

MEMORY

A memory game. The student opens two cards; when they match, the student makes a sentence with the pair (example under Show answer).

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I have gone there in 2022, I have saw, I live here since 2020, I haven’t never, did you ever…? (for have you ever), I’ve already went. A2: one correction per answer is enough.

THE FIRST TRIP (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

A light negotiation right after the casting: you are the new host (any of the three), and you want a first trip to a place you haven’t been to yet. Your student, the producer, has a short budget (South America or Europe, not Asia). Your secret, only on your screen: Patagonia is OK if you get one free day. Ask the four questions, wait five seconds in silence after each, then use the PUSH line (another show has offered you Japan) before the student closes the deal on screen. No right answer. Recast quietly while they talk (have been / went, already / yet); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.
