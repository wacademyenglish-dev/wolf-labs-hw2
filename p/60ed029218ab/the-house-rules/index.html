The House Rules · B1 Unit 6 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new B1 lesson on obligation (must, have to), prohibition (mustn’t), no obligation (don’t have to) and advice (should). The big point: mustn’t ≠ don’t have to.

The story: The student is moving into Flat 3 (invented) with two strangers. There are rules on the fridge, Leo ate somebody’s cheese, and Ana left an angry note about her yogurt.

About 42 min: I · The Rules on the Fridge (9) · II · Mustn’t or Don’t Have To? (10) · III · The Angry Note (7) · IV · Leo in the Hot Seat (7) · V · Your House Rules (9).

Open with: “Have you ever shared a house or a room? What was the hardest part?”

Brazilians often use “mustn’t” to mean “não precisa”. Spend time on that pair in Notice. Keep it light: shared flats and fridge notes are funny. The writing card is about the student’s real home.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

must (obligation, often your own rule) · have to (obligation, often an outside rule) · mustn’t (forbidden) · don’t have to (not necessary) · should (advice) · he has to / he doesn’t have to

THE RULES ON THE FRIDGE

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

MUSTN’T OR DON’T HAVE TO?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

THE ANGRY NOTE

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

YOUR HOUSE RULES

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

must to (must + verb), mustn’t for “não precisa” (don’t have to), he have to (he has to), don’t must (mustn’t / don’t have to), he musts (he must). B1: one correction per answer is enough.

LEO IN THE HOT SEAT (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the angry note, a kitchen meeting: the student is Leo, and you are Ana, the yogurt owner. Ask the four questions on the screen; the student answers in character with must, have to, mustn’t, don’t have to and should, and decides whether to defend himself or say sorry. Wait five seconds in silence after each question, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make Ana harder to please. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · The Rules on the Fridge.
  • Focus: II · Mustn’t or Don’t Have To? and III · The Angry Note.
  • Out loud: IV · Leo in the Hot Seat.
  • Write: V · Your House Rules.
