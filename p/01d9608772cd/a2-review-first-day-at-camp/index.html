First Day at Camp · A2 Unit 7 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 7 (Rules and advice). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student is a camp leader at a summer camp by a lake. The first day happens on a board game around the table: rules, advice and questions from new campers.

About 49 min: I · Bingo (8) · II · The Board (15) · III · Find the Mistakes (7) · IV · The Worried Mom (7) · V · Small Talk (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Make a line on the bingo card. Any square first.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

should / shouldn’t · have to / has to / don’t have to · Do I have to…? / had to · must / mustn’t

BINGO

A 3×3 card of quick tasks. The student picks a square and does it out loud; tap Done. Three in a line (across, down or diagonal) is a BINGO. Keep going for a second line if there is time.

THE BOARD

A board game around the table. Tap Roll the die: the token moves and the square shows a task that mixes the grammar of the unit. The student does it out loud; tap Done and roll again until the end of the board.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

SMALL TALK

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

must to wear, don’t must, he have to, should to bring, yesterday I must, haven’t to. A2: one correction per answer is enough.

THE WORRIED MOM (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

A short hot seat in the middle of the review: the night before camp, you are Mrs. Lima, the nervous mom of Theo (10). Ask her four questions; your student, the camp leader, answers with have to, must, should. Your secret (only you see it): you want to call Theo every night at ten. Use the PUSH line to make it harder, then the student decides the exception on screen. No right answer. Wait five seconds in silence after each question, recast quietly while they talk, and at the end ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Bingo and III · Find the Mistakes and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Board, IV · The Worried Mom and V · Small Talk.
