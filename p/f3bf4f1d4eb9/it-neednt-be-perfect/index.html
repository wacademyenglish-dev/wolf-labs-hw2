It Needn’t Be Perfect · C1 Unit 2 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on the present: needn’t / don’t need to / don’t have to / there’s no need to (not necessary) versus mustn’t / aren’t allowed to (forbidden). The past (needn’t have) is in another lesson of the unit.

The story: Marta (invented) leaves a welcome note on the kitchen table of the Blue House, a holiday home by the beach. At the end, the student writes house rules for a guest.

About 47 min: I · Your Rules (6) · II · The Welcome Note (9) · III · Kind or Strict? (9) · IV · The Beach Council (8) · V · Find the Mistakes (7) · VI · Your House Rules (8).

Open with: “At your job or school, what do you have to do that you don’t like?”

Even advanced Brazilian students say “you mustn’t pay, it’s free”. That sounds like a law. Focus on the feeling of each sentence: kind host or strict guard? The welcome note shows the contrast; the bingo and the party message make the student choose fast.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

needn’t + verb (no to) · don’t have to / don’t need to · there’s no need to · mustn’t · aren’t allowed to · kind host or strict guard: the tone

YOUR RULES

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

THE WELCOME NOTE

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

KIND OR STRICT?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

YOUR HOUSE RULES

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

you mustn’t pay, it’s free, you needn’t to worry, you don’t must, you don’t need bring, there’s no need bring, you don’t allowed to. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE BEACH COUNCIL (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the rule, your student sits on the beach council and votes on three new rules for the Blue House: Yes, Propose a change, or No. There is no right answer; a good vote says what is necessary and what is forbidden (needn’t, mustn’t, aren’t allowed to). Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the vote harder. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I · Your Rules and IV · The Beach Council.
  • Listen: II · The Welcome Note.
  • Focus: III · Kind or Strict? and V · Find the Mistakes.
  • Write: VI · Your House Rules.
