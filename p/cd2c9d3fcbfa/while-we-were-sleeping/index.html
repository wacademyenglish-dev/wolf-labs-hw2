While We Were Sleeping · Invasion · The Night Feeds

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: One night, four ordinary people, four corners of the world.

The grammar: Was Doing, When It Happened: She was cooking when the lights went out.

The class (about 45 min): I Warm-up and In the Dark · III The Trailer · VI Was Doing, When It Happened · VII The Night Feeds · VIII Your Alibi · IX The Interview. II The Words and V Line by Line are short listening scenes. No extra scenes: every scene is part of the class.

Open with: “You know the four people now. In the trailer, what was each person doing when it happened? Guess.”

Invasion (Apple TV, 2021) follows ordinary people in different countries on the night something arrives on Earth: a sheriff in Oklahoma on his last day at work, a mother in New York, a young woman in Tokyo, a soldier in the desert. Strange things happen everywhere at the same moment, and every one of them remembers what they were doing when it happened. The grammar: the past continuous (was / were + -ing, the long action) cut by the past simple (when + the sudden moment), while + -ing, and the question What were you doing when…?

A note for the teacher: the trailer is tense but not graphic (gunfire in a desert patrol, a family running from home, someone shouting for help); watch it once before class. Nothing scary is on the lesson screens. The trailer has no timed captions now, so the five frames are estimated, and each moment is told in our own words.

Warm-up and In the Dark (I): two parts. 1 · Warm-up: three questions about the student’s life, one at a time (big on the screen, with “Ask more” under each and one short line about the show under the photo). Let them talk; don’t correct the grammar yet. 2 · In the Dark: the screen is covered and only the sound plays, from 0:18 to 0:34: a leader speaks to the whole world (“My fellow citizens of the world…”). The student says who is talking, to whom and how he sounds, and imagines what people were doing while they listened; then take off the cover.

The words (II): prepared, incidents, connected, safe and encounter, before the video, in hear it in the video and mystery meaning. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (V) lists the lines with the yellow words: tap a line, hear it again, and the student says it like the character.

The ending (IX, The Interview): the teacher is the sheriff on his last day; the student asks what he and the town were doing when it happened. Then swap: the student is themselves on that night. Speaking only.

ON SCREEN

The cover is a dark suburban street with all the lights out. I: Warm-up and In the Dark. III: the trailer in three steps. Before: a 30-second clock: the student tells the last time the power went out (I was … when …). Watch: three taps (who is speaking? what are the incidents? what does he say about them?), each with a ▶ time: tap it and the video jumps to that moment. After: One word is wrong, three lines from the speech, each with a ▶ time; the student says the right word. V: five frames on the film strip. VI, Was Doing, When It Happened: six clues to sort into two boxes: the long action (was / were + -ing) and the moment that cut it (past simple). Then the student says them together: “The sheriff was finishing his last day when the lights went out.” VII, The Night Feeds: six lanes, one per place (Tokyo, Oklahoma, Long Island, a school bus, Paris, you), each with its local time. The student matches each moment (“Tokyo · the signal stopped”) to what people were doing (“Mitsuki was watching a launch”); every right pair fills the lane with a long teal bar that stops at the red line (the moment). The rule stays in the corner. At the end, all the bars meet the red line: it happened everywhere at once. VIII, Your Alibi: Ask Me: the student builds five questions (What were you doing…?), asks the teacher and listens to the alibi. IX: the interview.

THE COMMON ERRORS

was watch, I sleeping, the sheriff were, people was / the kids wasn’t, when the lights were going out (the moment: went out), while she cooked, didn’t sleeping, What you were doing?, What did you doing?, is patrolling … when they heard.

THE TOWN COUNCIL (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the trailer, the town meets the morning after the invasion. Three motions: your student votes Yes, No or Propose a change, and says why with was / were + -ing and when. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the vote harder. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Warm-up and In the Dark, IV The Town Council, VII The Night Feeds, VIII Your Alibi and IX The Interview.
  • Listen: II The Words, III The Trailer and V Line by Line.
  • Focus: VI Was Doing, When It Happened.
  • Write: the homework (the lesson ends speaking).
