Writing Sample · DET Prep · Lesson 14 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the Writing Sample: at the end of the test, one topic, about 30 seconds to prepare and 5 minutes to write. It has no score, but universities read it with the results. The student learns the formal register and a three-part plan with a strong last sentence.

The story: An invented candidate’s sample with good ideas and the wrong register (gonna, super cool, u, stuff). Then the student’s own sample about a five-year goal.

About 41 min: I · The Right Register (9) · II · Find the Mistakes (7) · III · Five Years, Five Goals (7) · IV · The Real Thing (14) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “Would you write ‘gonna’ in an email to a university?” Then: “And in a text to a friend?” The difference is the lesson.

This text can be read by admissions teams, so it should sound like the student at their best: clear, polite and personal. After the 5 minutes, read it together with the corrector and the checklist, and save the final version: it is useful for real applications too.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

going to, not gonna · you, not u · specific adjectives: practical, challenging · In addition, / Finally, · I believe / In my view · For these reasons, …

THE RIGHT REGISTER

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

THE REAL THING

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to keep this text: next lesson, the mock test ends with the same kind of task.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

gonna / wanna, u, super cool, stuff, also also, thats. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

FIVE YEARS, FIVE GOALS (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Before the Writing Sample, the student ranks five life goals for the next five years (a degree abroad, a better job, confident English, saving money, time with friends and family) and explains the top one and the last one. Then they open your order, compare, and you agree on one top 3 together. They arrive at the five-minute writing with a goal and a reason ready. Wait five seconds in silence before they start. Use the PUSH line when the answer is too safe. Recast quietly while they talk, and push the formal register of the lesson (I believe, In my view, In addition, To achieve this, For these reasons; no gonna, no stuff); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Right Register and II · Find the Mistakes and V · I Can.
  • Write: IV · The Real Thing.
  • Out loud: III · Five Years, Five Goals.
