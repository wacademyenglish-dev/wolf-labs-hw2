Can I Speak to Mr. Duarte? · Selling Services · Lesson 17

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 17 (phone calls: This is… · I’m calling about…). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Chapter 2 of the course story “Leo’s First Big Client”: Leo calls Hugo Duarte, CEO of Orla Beverages (fictional), for four days, and Nina, the assistant, always says “Sorry, he’s busy”… until she remembers the man from the elevator. The student answers six assistant lines (audio), finds the Brazilian traps, then calls YOU: you are Nina in the Arena. A mini-debate (phone or e-mail first?) and his own call script close the lesson.

About 55 min: I · Your Calls (5) · II · Easy Reader: The Gatekeeper (8) · III · The Assistant Says… (6) · IV · “This Is Leo”, Not “Here Is Leo” (9) · V · The Gatekeeper (10) · VI · Mini-Debate: Phone or E-mail? (6) · VII · Your Call Script (8) · VIII · I Can (3).

Open with: “Who answers the phone at your company? Do you like calling new clients?”

Cold calls: the student must pass the gatekeeper. Four chunks: This is… / I’m calling about… / Can I speak to…, please? / Could you take a message, please? Brazilian traps: “Here is Leo” or “I am Leo speaking” (from “aqui é o Leo”), “calling for”, “talk with”, “get a message”. In V you are Nina: say “Sorry, he’s busy” first and help only if he is polite and clear. In VI (mini-debate) always take the OTHER side and give one reason; he answers with “I don’t agree because…”.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Hi, this is … from … · I’m calling about … · Can I speak to …, please? · Could you take a message, please? · Hold on, please.

YOUR CALLS

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

EASY READER: THE GATEKEEPER

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

THE ASSISTANT SAYS…

Listen or read, then choose one option. The colors show right and wrong, and one line says why. Ask the student WHY before you move on.

“THIS IS LEO”, NOT “HERE IS LEO”

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

THE GATEKEEPER

Arena do Cliente: YOU are the client. Read the client card on your screen (only you see the answers). The student asks; you answer ONLY what was asked, short and a little busy. Tap each fact when the student finds it. At the end, Show answer has model questions. (Soon the AI can play the client; the card stays the same.)

MINI-DEBATE: PHONE OR E-MAIL?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

YOUR CALL SCRIPT

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask him to make one real cold call this week with his script and to tell you what the gatekeeper said.

THE TECH WORDS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno vende serviços B2B. Você não precisa ser especialista; precisa saber do que ele está falando. Não ensine estes termos: deixe que ele explique o trabalho dele (é conversa real).

  • BPO (Business Process Outsourcing): Terceirização de processos: a empresa contratada EXECUTA uma rotina do cliente (folha de pagamento, contabilidade, fiscal, contas a pagar). O cliente para de fazer aquilo dentro de casa.
  • BTO (Business Transformation Outsourcing): Terceirização com transformação: além de executar, a contratada MUDA o processo (tecnologia, automação, integração de sistemas). Vende-se como “melhorar”, não só “fazer por você”.
  • ERP: Sistema de gestão que integra finanças, compras, estoque, RH etc. Exemplos (marcas): SAP, Oracle, TOTVS. Um BPO/BTO não vende o ERP: trabalha COM ele ou o integra.
  • Gatekeeper: “Porteiro”: a assistente, secretária ou recepcionista que filtra as ligações para o diretor. Passar pelo gatekeeper é uma habilidade de vendas.
  • Cold call: Ligação fria: ligar para alguém que não conhece você nem pediu o contato.
  • Take / leave a message: Anotar um recado (quem atende: take) · deixar um recado (quem liga: leave).
  • Hold on · on hold: “Aguarde na linha” · a ligação em espera (com musiquinha).
  • Executive assistant: Secretária executiva / assistente de diretoria. Tratar com cortesia: ela decide se você chega ao chefe.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Here is Leo / I am Leo speaking (This is Leo), I’m calling for the proposal (calling about), Can I talk with…? (Can I speak to…?), Can you get a message? (Could you take a message?). A1+: one correction per answer is enough.
