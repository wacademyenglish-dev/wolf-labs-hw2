Better Speaking · Episode 5 · Half of the World · B1

Episode 5 of Better Speaking, B1 version. Kofi Annan, from Ghana: half of humanity has never made a phone call. The language is quantifiers: all of, most, most of, half of, some of, none of. The sister lesson for B2 is “Hold Their Attention”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — How many, Guess the survey. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — All, most, half of, Match the number, Fix the traps, Choose the right word.
  • Write — Your world in numbers. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

“A maioria das pessoas” becomes the most of people. “A metade de” becomes the half of. And “nenhum dos” becomes nobody of.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, matches and writes. “Only you see this” is yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. The bar on top shows the season: eight episodes, today’s one lit. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
