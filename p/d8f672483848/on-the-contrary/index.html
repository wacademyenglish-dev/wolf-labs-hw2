On the Contrary · C1 Unit 7 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on contrast with precision: on the contrary (no, the opposite is true) × on the other hand (both sides are true) × conversely / in contrast to (two different things compared) × far from it / quite the opposite.

The story: Lia Morel (invented) writes an opinion column: “Are big cities bad for us?” She denies myths, shows two sides and compares cities and towns.

About 51 min: I · The Column (10) · II · Opposite or Other Side? (9) · III · Bingo (8) · IV · Hot Takes (8) · V · City or Village? (8) · VI · Your Column (8).

Open with: “Big city or small town: where would you rather live? Why?”

Brazilian speakers often use on the contrary for “por outro lado”. The key test: are you DENYING the first idea (on the contrary) or ADDING another side (on the other hand)? The column shows both; the bingo and the would-you-rather make the student choose fast; the column at the end is real argumentative writing.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

on the contrary: no, the opposite · on the one hand… on the other hand · conversely · in contrast to / with · far from it, far from + adjective · quite the opposite

THE COLUMN

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

OPPOSITE OR OTHER SIDE?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

BINGO

A 3×3 card of quick tasks. The student picks a square and does it out loud; tap Done. Three in a line (across, down or diagonal) is a BINGO. Keep going for a second line if there is time.

CITY OR VILLAGE?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

YOUR COLUMN

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

it’s expensive; on the contrary, it’s beautiful, was it easy? on the other hand, it was hard, in contrast of, far from it is cheap, in the other hand, at the contrary. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

HOT TAKES (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

In the middle of the lesson, your student rates four unpopular opinions (boredom, eating alone, learning after 30, holidays with no plan) from 1 to 5 and defends the number: deny with on the contrary / far from it, or weigh both sides with on the one hand / on the other hand. There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence, then take the opposite number and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: II · Opposite or Other Side? and III · Bingo.
  • Out loud: IV · Hot Takes and V · City or Village?.
