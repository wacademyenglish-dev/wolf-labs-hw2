Walk Me Through Your CV · WOLF HIRED · Lesson 2 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: walking through a CV: overall statement, past simple for finished jobs (I worked… from… to…, two years ago), present perfect for the current job (I’ve worked here since…, for…), passive (I was promoted / hired), why I left, explaining a gap, why this job.

The story: A recruiter’s formula with a teacher who becomes an office manager, then the student’s own CV with the teacher as interviewer.

About 46 min: I · Ninety Seconds, Not Ten Minutes (9) · II · Finished or Still True? (12) · III · The Follow-Up Questions (14) · IV · Career Hot Takes (7) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “How many jobs have you had? Which one taught you the most?”

Ask the student to open their CV (or LinkedIn) before the follow-up scene. If there is a real gap or a short job, practice it: calm, short, positive, then back to the present.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

I’m a … professional, focused on… · I’ve been … at … since… · Before that, I worked at … for… · I was promoted to… · I was looking for… · This role is the right next step because…

NINETY SECONDS, NOT TEN MINUTES

Self Made Millennial, how to answer “Walk me through your resume” (4:39–7:50): the formula and a career changer example.

FINISHED OR STILL TRUE?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

THE FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to prepare a one-sentence answer for the weakest point of their CV (a gap, a short job, a change).

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I have worked there in 2019 (I worked there in 2019), I work here for three years (I’ve worked here for three years), since two years (two years ago / for two years), I promoted in 2021 (I was promoted). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

CAREER HOT TAKES (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the follow-up questions, four hot takes about careers: five years in one job, a gap on the CV, title or salary, your first job. The student taps 1 to 5 and defends the number with their own story; there is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence, then take the opposite side and use the PUSH line (only you see it). This is where the tenses really happen: recast quietly while they talk (I’ve worked there since…, I left two years ago, I was promoted…); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
