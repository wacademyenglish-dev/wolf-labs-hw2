The Hotel Inspector · B1 Unit 4 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 4 (How much, how, what kind). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student is a secret hotel inspector at the Hotel Marlow (invented). You are three guests in the lobby: a businesswoman, a family of five and a travel blogger. In the end the student gives the hotel its stars.

About 42 min: I · Bingo (8) · II · The Secret Inspector (15) · III · Your Review (7) · IV · The Complaints Desk (8) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “Nine squares. Pick one, answer, and make a line.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

too much, too many, not enough · few or a few, little or a little · both, either, neither · -ed or -ing · strong adjectives: absolutely exhausted

BINGO

A 3×3 card of quick tasks. The student picks a square and does it out loud; tap Done. Three in a line (across, down or diagonal) is a BINGO. Keep going for a second line if there is time.

THE SECRET INSPECTOR

You are each guest. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then the student gives the stars and explains, with too much / not enough and both / neither. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

YOUR REVIEW

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

WOULD YOU RATHER?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

too much people, not enough hot (word order), I was very boring (for bored), very exhausted (for absolutely exhausted), both of them didn’t (for neither), a little friends. B1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE COMPLAINTS DESK (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the stars, your student decides three complaints at the Hotel Marlow with the evidence on the table: the noisy room, the family and the blogger. There is no right answer: let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then defend the other side and use the PUSH line (only you see it). Recast quietly (too much noise, not enough towels, absolutely exhausting); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Bingo and V · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Secret Inspector and IV · The Complaints Desk.
  • Write: III · Your Review.
