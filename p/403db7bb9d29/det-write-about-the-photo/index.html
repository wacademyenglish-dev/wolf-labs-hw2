Write About the Photo · DET Prep · Lesson 12 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the Write About the Photo question: a photo and about one minute to write about it (several photos in a row). The student learns to build one or two RICH, correct sentences: place + details + adjectives + with / which / where.

The story: No story: three photos (a tent by a lake, a carpentry workshop, a park in autumn), no people.

About 41 min: I · Build It Up (9) · II · Find the Mistakes (7) · III · Three Photos (14) · IV · The Ad Deal (7) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: Show photo 1 and ask for ONE sentence out loud. Then: “Now let’s make it twice as rich in the same time.”

Quality beats quantity in this task. A student who writes one perfect, rich sentence does better than one who writes five short sentences with mistakes. After each photo, use the red underlines of the corrector together.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

there is / there are · a place: by, near, in front of · two adjectives · with + detail · which (things) / where (places) · very + adjective

BUILD IT UP

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

THREE PHOTOS

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Count the adjectives and the joining words in each answer: that is the student’s score today.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

in this photo have, near of, there is two, what for which, very much dark, must to be. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

THE AD DEAL (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the three photos, the rich sentence becomes a job. The student writes the ad for the camping photo; you play Rita, the owner, who wants “luxury” and “the best lake in the country”. Her secret limit is on your screen only: she gives up “luxury” if the campfire and the calm lake are in the first sentence. Ask the four questions, insist a little, then close the deal on one sentence together. Wait five seconds in silence before they start. Use the PUSH line when they give in too fast. Recast quietly while they talk, and push the lesson’s formula (place + details + adjectives + with / which / where); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Write: I · Build It Up and III · Three Photos.
  • Out loud: IV · The Ad Deal.
  • Focus: II · Find the Mistakes and V · I Can.
