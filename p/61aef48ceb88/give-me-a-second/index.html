Give Me a Second · The Hourglass

The silence is the mistake: not the wrong word, the empty second. When a word doesn’t come, the student stops, or switches language. This hour gives them four moves that buy time. The screen is an hourglass that drains during ten seconds of silence; every move said out loud flips it. No points, but the scoreboard counts moves against silences.

THE MOVES

Hold on, let me think (a little time) · That’s a good question (a lot) · How do I put it… (a lot: you’re searching) · It’s a kind of… / It’s the thing you use for… (no word? describe it).

THE HOURGLASS

In scene IV, you start the sand (button under the hourglass). Every move the student says and taps flips it; if it runs out, it counts as a silence. Ask hard questions on purpose, and don’t rescue them. If they switch language, point at the hourglass: describe, don’t translate.

THE AUDIO

Two recordings (That’s a good question, actually and How do I put it…). The other two lines in scene V are in your voice: say them as you would in real life.

PLACE YOUR BET (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Three people hit a hard second: Ana in a job interview, Leo without the word ladder, Mia on a call with her boss. Your student bets on what each one does and says why; bet too. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it): it turns the story into their own hard second, so the hourglass rule still holds. Then press Check: every ending is one of the four moves. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Where You Stop (count the silences), IV The Hourglass, VI Say It Without the Word, VII Six About You, VIII Place Your Bet.
  • Focus: II Four Moves, III Keep the Floor. Listen: V.
  • Write: IX, a hard answer with a move and “it’s a kind of”. It goes to their My notes.
