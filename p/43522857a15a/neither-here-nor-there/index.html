Neither Here Nor There · C1 Unit 9 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on nuanced opinion phrases: I’m all for it, I’m in two minds, I have mixed feelings, I’m sitting on the fence, I couldn’t care less, it’s not my cup of tea, it’s neither here nor there, if you ask me, the way I see it, for what it’s worth, to be fair.

The story: City Radio’s phone-in (invented): should the main streets close to cars on Sundays? Marta is all for it, Bruno is in two minds, Dona Celia couldn’t care less, Tom has mixed feelings.

About 47 min: I · Hot Topics (6) · II · The Phone-In (10) · III · What’s the Temperature? (9) · IV · Sunday Streets (8) · V · The Opinion Board (14).

Open with: “What do you think about working from home?”

Opinions have a temperature. The student moves beyond “in my opinion” to strong, middle and zero-interest positions. Big trap: “I couldn’t care less” means zero interest. The phone-in shows five temperatures; the board lets the student choose their own on everyday topics.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

I’m all for it / I’m totally against it · I’m in two minds / I have mixed feelings · I’m sitting on the fence · I couldn’t care less / not my cup of tea · it’s neither here nor there (irrelevant) · if you ask me, the way I see it, for what it’s worth, to be fair

HOT TOPICS

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

THE PHONE-IN

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

WHAT’S THE TEMPERATURE?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

MEMORY

A memory game. The student opens two cards; when they match, the student makes a sentence with the pair (example under Show answer).

THE OPINION BOARD

A board game around the table. Tap Roll the die: the token moves and the square shows a task that mixes the grammar of the unit. The student does it out loud; tap Done and roll again until the end of the board.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I couldn’t care less (understood as caring a lot), I’m agree, in two minds of it, not my cup of coffee, in my opinion, I loved it (redundant), I’m in the fence. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

SUNDAY STREETS (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

This scene replaces the Memory game. The phone-in worked: the streets are closed to cars on Sundays. Now your student is on the city council and solves three problems from the first month (Bruno’s shop, the street music, the cost), choosing an option and giving their opinion with the right temperature: all for it, in two minds, neither here nor there. There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the decision harder. Recast quietly while they talk (watch for I’m agree); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON
