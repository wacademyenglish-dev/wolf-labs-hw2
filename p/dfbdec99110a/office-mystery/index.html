The Office Mystery · The Proof Meter

A prototype disappeared from a desk overnight. Three people stayed late. The student deduces about last night, and the modal never moves: must have, might have, can’t have + the past participle. The choice between them is not grammar, it’s evidence: how much proof do I have? On screen, a proof meter (a half-moon with three bands and a needle) and a floor plan with seven numbered yellow evidence markers that change color when the deduction is right.

MUST IS NOT A STRONG MIGHT

It’s what’s left after every other door is shut. Elena FITS; fitting is not proof. In scene IV, card 5 tempts a MUST: ask whether there is any other explanation.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Somebody must took it · He must have went · It mustn’t have been Marcus (impossible = can’t have) · She couldn’t make it when the student means a deduction (scene VII).

DON’T CORRECT SCENE I

Write the student’s first sentence about last night word for word. It comes back in scene X: read it back and wait for them to fix it themselves.

THE RECORDINGS

Three alibis in scene V, each with pause and 0.75×; the claims and the transcripts open after all three. Arthur says it was raining at 8:15; Elena says the rain started around ten; and Arthur talks about tea before anybody asks.

YOU ARE THE BOSS

Scene VIII: after each point, tap to read the push-back, and insist once. Let wrong forms pass; note them and give feedback at the end.

THE DEAL (VII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the seven markers, your student knows it was Arthur. Now you ARE Arthur, and you try to make a deal before they see the boss. Your secret limit is on your screen only. Ask the three questions in character, wait five seconds in silence, and let them use the proof. Use the PUSH line to make it harder, then close (or don’t close) the deal together. Recast quietly while they talk (must have taken, can’t have, not “mustn’t have”); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. Then go straight to VIII: the boss.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I The Desk at Seven, IV How Much Proof?, VI Seven Markers, VII The Deal, VIII The Report to the Boss, IX Your Own Last Night.
  • Listen: V Three Voices.
  • Focus: II The Door Log, III Now, or Last Night?
