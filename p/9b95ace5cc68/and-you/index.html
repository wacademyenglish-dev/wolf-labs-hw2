And You? · Keep the Ball in Play

Your student can answer questions. The problem: the conversation stops on their answer, every time. This hour is a table-tennis match: every answer must send the ball back. When it comes back, the ball crosses the table; when it doesn’t, it falls and the rally starts again. No timer, no points: the rally only shows how long the conversation lived.

THE GRAMMAR

They asked you: answer, then And you? / What about you? (the same). They told you: the echo copies their verb or its helper: I’m tired → Are you? · I work at night → Do you? · I’ve got a dog → Have you? · I can swim → Can you? · I lived in Lisbon → Did you? The voice goes UP.

YOUR TOP 3 (VII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the strangers, your student ranks three small-talk topics: the weather, food, your job. There is no right order. They tap 1, 2, 3, say their number one and send the ball back to you; answer it, then show your own order and agree on one top 3 together. Wait five seconds in silence before helping; the PUSH line (only you see it) is a quick yes or no. Recast quietly; at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Every answer should end with a return: And you?

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — I Four Seconds (don’t fill the silence: it is the argument of the hour), IV The Echo, VI Rally with Strangers, VII Your Top 3, VIII The Café Queue. Almost the whole hour is talk.
  • Listen — V Did It Come Back? (the same answer twice: dead, then alive).
  • Focus — II Four Holes, III Two Ways to Send It Back.
  • Write — IX Your Small Talk, with the red wavy line for Am you?, Work you? and And you. without the question mark. It goes to their My notes.

THE RALLY

In VI and VIII you judge: “hit” = an answer plus a return; “ball dropped” = an answer alone (the rally starts again). In VIII you open each topic with the line on the card, answer their returns naturally and ask again. Four hits close a topic.
