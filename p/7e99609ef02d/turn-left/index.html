The Challenge · Episode 8 · Turn Left

Episode 8. They’re in Edinburgh! First Holyrood, at the end of the Royal Mile; then the castle, at the top of the hill. The language is directions: turn left, turn right, go up, go down, at the end of. No points, no timer.

THE TRAP

Word by word from Portuguese: “vire à esquerda” becomes turn to the left, “no fim da rua” becomes in the end of the road, and “subir” becomes go to up.
