The Case Presentation · English for Healthcare · Lesson 14 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: presenting a case to colleagues: the order (who and presenting complaint, history, examination, tests, diagnosis, treatment, outcome, plan), signposts (presented with, has a history of, on examination, showed, ruled out) and the passive (was admitted / treated / referred / discharged).

The story: A fictional case (cellulitis in a 45-year-old builder with diabetes) presented on a ward round, then a fictional dental abscess case for the student to present.

About 38 min: I · A Model Case (10) · II · Active to Passive (10) · III · Present Your Case (14) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “When was the last time you presented a case? How long did it take?”

Case presentation styles vary by country and specialty; the shape taught here is general. If the student brings a real case, it must be anonymized (no names, no identifying details).

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

This is…, a …-year-old … who presented with… · has a history of… · On examination,… · The … showed / ruled out… · was diagnosed with / was started on… · was discharged with follow-up…

A MODEL CASE

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

ACTIVE TO PASSIVE

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

PRESENT YOUR CASE

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to prepare a real (anonymized) case for the conference lesson.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

He was admitted in Monday (on Monday), She was treat (treated), the exams showed (the tests showed), telling the story out of order. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · A Model Case.
  • Focus: II · Active to Passive and IV · I Can.
  • Out loud: III · Present Your Case.
