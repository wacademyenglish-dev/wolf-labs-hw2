I Have a Headache · Maple Street Clinic

Monday, 8 AM. It’s still dark outside, and the waiting room of the Maple Street Clinic is full. Nurse Rosa calls the tickets; the red panel lights each number, and the student gives each patient a voice. The twist: the first patient says I am with a headache, and Rosa looks around: “With who? What does Mr. Hill have?” Then the clinic clock runs from 9 AM to noon (the hands move with every answer), and the last ticket is Rosa’s own. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

I have a + problem (a headache, a cold, a fever, a cough, a sore throat). I feel + how (tired, sick, dizzy). My + body part + hurts. A first taste of advice: You should + verb (no questions or negatives yet).

The Portuguese trap: “estou com” becomes I am with a headache; “tenho febre” drops the article; “dói minha cabeça” puts the verb first; “me sinto” brings I feel me; “fico tonto” becomes I stay dizzy. The worst: “estou resfriado” comes out as I am cold, which is only the temperature. Also a pain of throat, toss for cough, You should to rest. All of them are the wrong options in the scenes.

WOULD YOU RATHER (VI) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After ticket 21, three light choices about everyday care: tired, a cold, advice for a friend. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it): a quick yes or no. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I You and Your Health, II The Waiting Room, IV Nine to Noon, V Your Ticket, VI Would You Rather, VII Your Health.
  • Focus: III The Rule.
  • Write: VIII A Note to the Doctor, with the red wavy line for every trap above. It goes to their My notes.

YOU ARE THE VOICES

No recording in this lesson. In the waiting room and on the clock you read what happens; the student answers at once, then taps what they said. In scene V you are Nurse Rosa at the front desk.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The tickets, the clock and the wheel move on both screens. G opens this guide.
