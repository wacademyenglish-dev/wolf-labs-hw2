The City Survey · A2 Unit 1 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 1 (Habits and right now). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student does a city survey at night. You are three people in the street: a nurse, a student and a taxi driver. Then you are the boss.

About 46 min: I · The Die (8) · II · The City Survey (15) · III · One More Hour? (6) · IV · Your Answers (6) · V · Would You Rather? (7) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Pick a door.” There is no order. One minute per door, no notes.

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

present simple for habits · present continuous for now · simple × continuous · asking for clarification · buying time · too / either, so do I / neither do I

THE DIE

Tap Roll the die: the number chooses one of the six topics (no repeats until all six are done). The student does the challenge from memory.

THE CITY SURVEY

You are each person. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the survey card fills in. The last line is a reaction: say something and wait for “Me too / Me neither”. Then you are the boss: the student reports on one person with usually and right now. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

YOUR ANSWERS

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

WOULD YOU RATHER?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

she is usually working (habit), he buying a pizza, me too (after a negative), repeat, please, she work, I am agree. A2: one correction per answer is enough.

ONE MORE HOUR? (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

A light negotiation right after the survey: it’s 11 PM and you, the boss, want three more people. Your secret limit is on your screen only (two people, or tomorrow morning): don’t say it first. Ask the four questions, let them answer in the role with usually / right now, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line. They close by choosing a deal. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Die and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The City Survey, III · One More Hour? and V · Would You Rather?.
  • Write: IV · Your Answers.
