Tell Me About Yourself · WOLF HIRED · Lesson 1 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the interview pitch: present simple for who you are (I’m a…), past simple for where you started, present perfect for experience (I’ve worked… since / for…), one achievement with a number, what the company needs (I understand you’re looking for…) and why you (That’s why I’m confident…).

The story: A career coach’s four tips, a fictional customer support pitch to put in order, then the student’s own pitch (or a fictional marketing graduate).

About 40 min: I · What They Really Want (9) · II · Put the Pitch in Order (9) · III · Your First 60 Seconds (18) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “Do you have an interview coming up? What job? If not, what’s your dream job?”

Ask the student’s real target job in the first minute and use it all lesson. Brazilian candidates often start with age, city and family; model the professional start. Time the final pitch and ask for a second try with one improvement.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

I’m a … with … years of experience in… · I started my career at… · Since…, I’ve been… · I’m most proud of… · I understand you’re looking for… · That’s why I’m confident I can…

WHAT THEY REALLY WANT

Linda Raynier, how to answer “Tell me about yourself” (1:34–6:20): four tips.

PUT THE PITCH IN ORDER

The events are mixed. The student taps them in order; wrong order shows in red. When it’s right, the student tells the whole story with first, then, after that, finally.

YOUR FIRST 60 SECONDS

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to record their pitch on their phone and listen once before the next lesson.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I have 28 years and I live in… (start with your profession), I work here since 2021 (I’ve worked here since 2021), I am working with marketing for two years (I’ve been working in marketing for two years), I have experience in (with) customers. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: II · Put the Pitch in Order and IV · I Can.
  • Out loud: III · Your First 60 Seconds.
