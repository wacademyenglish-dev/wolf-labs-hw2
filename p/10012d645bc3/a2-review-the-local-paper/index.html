The Local Paper · A2 Unit 2 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 2 (Stories in the past). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student is a reporter for the neighborhood paper. You are three witnesses of yesterday’s news: a cat on a church roof, old coins in the park, and a wedding cake on the sidewalk. Then you are the editor.

About 47 min: I · Bingo (8) · II · The Local Paper (14) · III · The Editor’s Choice (6) · IV · The Story in Order (7) · V · The Hot Seat (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Make a line on the bingo card. Any square first.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

past simple: regular and irregular · did / didn’t + verb · subject questions (Who called?) × object questions (Who did you call?) · first, then, after that, finally · and then, so, because

BINGO

A 3×3 card of quick tasks. The student picks a square and does it out loud; tap Done. Three in a line (across, down or diagonal) is a BINGO. Keep going for a second line if there is time.

THE LOCAL PAPER

You are each witness. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the editor: the student dictates one story in order, with first / then / finally. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

THE STORY IN ORDER

The events are mixed. The student taps them in order; wrong order shows in red. When it’s right, the student tells the whole story with first, then, after that, finally.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Who did call…? (subject), he didn’t went, after, he… (for after that), goed / buyed, did you saw?, because so. A2: one correction per answer is enough.

THE EDITOR’S CHOICE (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the interviews, your student makes three hard choices about the stories: the front page, the coins, the cake. There is no right answer: let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Listen for the past (I chose, I told, didn’t + verb). Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Bingo and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Local Paper, III · The Editor’s Choice, IV · The Story in Order and V · The Hot Seat.
