Better Speaking · Episode 4 · Sound Like Someone · B2

Episode 4 of Better Speaking. Björk talks about her albums Debut and Post with very natural English. Richard shows why: she speaks in chunks (take it as it comes, the ups and downs, a week in the life of, a piece of advice), and she has copied the sounds of London (the t that disappears in better, little). The advice: choose one person and copy the way they speak. The sister lesson for B1 is “I Just Had to Do It”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — Your English model, Copy the rhythm. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — Speak in chunks, Finish the chunk, Fix the traps, Choose the chunk.
  • Write — Your chunk notebook. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

Chunks translated from Portuguese: an advice, do a mistake, highs and lows parts. And speaking word by word, which makes even correct English sound slow.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, matches and writes. “Only you see this” is yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. The bar on top shows the season: eight episodes, today’s one lit. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
