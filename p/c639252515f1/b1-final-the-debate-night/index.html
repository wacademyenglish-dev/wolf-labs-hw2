The Debate Night · B1 Unit 10 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 10 (Closing Book B1). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: The last review of Book B1. It’s debate night at the town theater, played on a board game. On many squares you disagree, so the student has to answer with however, despite and even though. Then the student writes an opening speech.

About 51 min: I · Memory (8) · II · Debate Night (15) · III · Your Opening Speech (7) · IV · Backstage (7) · V · The Hot Seat (8) · VI · I Can (3) · VII · The Next Book (3).

Open with: “Open two cards. Find the pair, then make the sentence.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

purpose: to, in order to, so that · cause and result: because of, leads to · contrast: however, despite, even though · emphatic do · bring / take, come / go · say it without the word

MEMORY

A memory game. The student opens two cards; when they match, the student makes a sentence with the pair (example under Show answer).

DEBATE NIGHT

A board game around the table. Tap Roll the die: the token moves and the square shows a task that mixes the grammar of the unit. The student does it out loud; tap Done and roll again until the end of the board.

YOUR OPENING SPEECH

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. This is the last review of Book B1. After I Can there is one more short scene, VI The Next Book, with what Book B2 brings.

THE NEXT BOOK

The last scene of the book: three cards with what the next book brings. Read them together and ask which one the student wants first.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

despite of, despite it was raining, so that to, even though of, it leads in, I do went (for I did go). B1: one correction per answer is enough.

BACKSTAGE (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

The lesson is already a debate, so this moment is different: three small dilemmas backstage, five minutes before the student goes on stage (the microphone stops working, the opponent is a nervous best friend, which prize to take). No right answer: the student chooses and says why with even though, despite, so that and in order to. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: II · Debate Night, IV · Backstage and V · The Hot Seat.
  • Write: III · Your Opening Speech.
