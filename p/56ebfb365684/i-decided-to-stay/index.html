I Decided to Stay · Postcards from a Gap Year

The student is on a gap year: Galway, Kyoto, Queenstown. From every stop, a postcard goes home to Grandma Ruth. Then the last night at a hostel on a lake: the flight home leaves at 6 AM, eight friends keep asking questions… and the student decides to stay. The grammar: the first verb chooses the form of the second. want, need, plan, hope, decide + to (I want to stay) · enjoy, avoid, finish, mind, keep + -ing (I enjoy working here). Never to staying.

THE PORTUGUESE TRAP

In Portuguese almost every verb takes the plain infinitive after it, so the student says I enjoy to travel, I avoid to eat late, Do you mind to wait? and nothing sounds wrong to them. After the rule the opposite error arrives: I want going, I want to working. Also I decided for staying.

THE POSTCARDS AND THE NIGHT

Scene II comes before the rule: collect the errors, correct nothing out loud; a right version postmarks the card. Scene IV is the pressure: each right answer moves the clock one hour, from 10 PM to 6 AM. Be each friend with a different voice, fast.

A NOTE

Keep it A2. like, love, hate, start take both forms: accept both. remember, forget, stop change meaning: another day.

THE NEW MANAGER (VI) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after Mom’s call, your student is the new manager of Finn’s hostel and makes three hard calls: a guest who can’t pay, a friend who forgets to pay, a party at home. There is no right answer: let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Push for decide / want / plan + to and enjoy / mind / avoid + -ing. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I You and the World, II Postcards Home, IV The Last Night, V Mom Calls, VI The New Manager, VII Your Choices.
  • Focus: III The Rule.
  • Write: VIII, a postcard to Grandma Ruth with one to and one -ing. It goes to their My notes.
