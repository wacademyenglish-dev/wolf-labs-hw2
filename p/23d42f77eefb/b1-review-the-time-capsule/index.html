The Time Capsule · B1 Unit 3 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 3 (Before and after). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: In 2006 a class buried a time capsule in the school garden. Today your student opens it on a board game: every square is an object from 2006. In the end, the student plans a new capsule for 2046.

About 42 min: I · The Die (8) · II · Open the Capsule (15) · III · The 2046 Capsule (7) · IV · The Hot Seat (8) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “Roll the die. The number chooses the challenge.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

past perfect · past perfect for the story behind · was going to: plans that changed · kept + -ing, ended up + -ing · going to or will · future continuous: will be doing

THE DIE

Tap Roll the die: the number chooses one of the six topics (no repeats until all six are done). The student does the challenge from memory.

OPEN THE CAPSULE

A board game around the table. Tap Roll the die: the token moves and the square shows a task that mixes the grammar of the unit. The student does it out loud; tap Done and roll again until the end of the board.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

when I arrived, the show already started, I was go to, kept to say, ended up to work, will flying, I’m going to answer it (a decision now: I’ll answer it). B1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE 2046 CAPSULE (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the 2006 capsule, your student ranks five objects for the 2046 capsule: which one will surprise people most? They explain their top one and their last one, then open your order and agree on one top 3 together. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Recast quietly (will be paying, had never seen, was going to); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
