First, Then, Finally · The Wishgranter

Under a fountain in a park lives a tiny, bored Wishgranter. One day a young man and a young woman throw their coins at the same second, both wishing for love; the machine stops, and the little hero has to go up into the park to bring them together. The student watches the short film (no words) in four sealed chapters, puts eight storyboard panels in order around the fountain, learns the rule, then changes the ending on the Wishgranter’s control panel. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

A story in the past simple, in order: First → Then / After that / Next → Finally / In the end. Regular verbs -ed; irregular ones learned (came, went, threw, fell, met). didn’t + base. The mistakes: After, alone, At the end,, the story slipping into the present, throwed / falled / comed, stoped, didn’t wanted.

THE FILM

An animated short with no words, 4:48, in four chapters that stop by themselves. Every answer is the student’s own narration.

THE BEST MOMENT (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the film and the new endings, your student ranks five moments of the film, from the best to the worst. There is no right answer: let them order the list, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it): a moment from their own life, told in order. Then open your own order and agree on one top 3 together. Push for the past (threw, went, fell, met) and First / Then / Finally. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: II The Short Film.
  • Out loud: I You and Your Stories, III The Storyboard (before the rule: tap the version you heard), V Change the Ending (eight “what if” cards, fast), VI Your Story, VII Tell a Friend (you are Sam; the big question closes it), VIII The Best Moment (they rank, then you show your order).
  • Focus: IV The Rule.
  • Write: IX, a real story in four steps, with the red wavy line for After, alone, At the end,, regularized irregular verbs, stoped and did + past. It goes to their My notes.

No audio in this lesson: your voice is Sam’s.
