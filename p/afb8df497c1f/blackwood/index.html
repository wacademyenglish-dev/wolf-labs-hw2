Blackwood · The Recorder

Tonight your student is the voice of Blackwood Night Radio, 88.1. A hiker left a voice recorder at the station; it was found in an empty cabin in the forest. On the tapes: a couple lost in the rain, something outside that copies what it hears and cannot open a door, and the notebook of a man called Halloran (1961). While the tapes play, the town starts hearing voices, and the last call of the night comes from the other side of the studio door. Every sentence has two tracks: something was happening when something happened.

THE TAPE WITH TWO TRACKS

Scene V is the heart of the lesson. The long action is a blue track; the short event is a red pin. Your student says the sentence, you tap what you heard, the pin drops into the track, and your student says it again starting with While.

THE TAPES ARE REAL AUDIO

Scenes II, IV, VI, VIII and IX have recordings: the narrator, Ana, four men from 1961, and sounds. The transcript opens only after each track is played.

SCENE IX IS THE PHYSICAL ONE

If you can, dim the light in your room. Read slowly. In part 3, say “It’s me. Open the door.” in your student’s way of speaking. Then your student decides: open the studio door, or keep it closed. The ending changes.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

It rained when we arrived (background in the simple past), while the engine stopped, I was walk, they was, What you were doing?, What did you doing?

THERE IS NO SCORE

No points, no timer, no lives. A wrong tap shows what happened, and your student says it again.

YOUR FIRST MOVE (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after Tape 1, your student is in the car when the engine stops: forest, rain, no other cars. They put four moves in order, from first to last. There is no right order. Let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then ask them to explain their top one and their last one. Use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder, then show your own order and agree on a top 3 together. Recast quietly while they talk (I was driving when it stopped); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
