What He Says, What He Does · Spy x Family · The Double Dossier

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: A spy, an assassin, a girl who reads minds, and a dog.

The grammar: Every Day × Right Now: He works every day. Right now, he’s spying.

The class (about 56 min): I Warm-up and Two Truths and a Lie · II The Trailer · III Scene by Scene · IV Place Your Bet · V Every Day × Right Now · VI The Double Dossier · VII Your Two Lives · VIII The Debate. No extra scenes: every scene is part of the class.

Open with: “You know the family now. In the trailer, what is Loid really doing? Guess.”

Spy x Family (2022) is the Japanese anime from Tatsuya Endo’s manga, set in Berlint, a city like Berlin in the 1960s. Loid Forger says he works at a hospital; in fact he is Twilight, the best spy in the country, and for a mission he needs a family, fast. His wife Yor says she works at City Hall, but at night she is an assassin; his daughter Anya can read minds. Nobody knows the others’ secrets, except Anya. The grammar: present simple for routine and facts (he works at a hospital, every day) × present continuous for now (right now he is spying), and the feeling verbs that never take -ing (like, love, know, want, understand).

A note for the teacher: the trailer has Japanese audio with English subtitles in the picture; the lesson plays the whole trailer (2:00). It has some action, but nothing violent is on the lesson screens. The times were read from the picture on 25/09, and each moment is told in our own words.

Warm-up and Two Truths and a Lie (I): two parts. 1 · Warm-up: three questions about the student’s life, one at a time (big on the screen, with “Ask more” under each and one short line about the anime under the photo). Let them talk; A2: short answers are fine. 2 · Two Truths and a Lie: three rounds about the family (Loid, Anya, Yor). The student reads the three sentences, says which one is the lie and why, then taps. In the last round, they say two truths and one lie about their own life, and you guess.

The ending (VIII, The Debate): “It is OK to lie to your family to protect them.” The student picks a side; the teacher takes the other one. Three reasons on each side and three helping phrases. Speaking only.

ON SCREEN

The cover is an old European bridge at night. I: Warm-up and Two Truths and a Lie. II: the trailer in three steps. Before: three cards (Loid, Yor, Anya): tap one and guess what that person really does. Watch: five sentences with a missing word, each with a ▶ time that jumps to that moment of the trailer; the student taps the word that fits. After: three guessing games, “Who is she / he?”: clues one by one, and “I know it!” shows the answer. III: five frames on the film strip. IV, Place Your Bet: three bets on what happens next in the show; Check shows the answer. V, Every Day × Right Now: six sentences to put in three boxes (every day, right now, feelings with no -ing). When all six are in the right box, the rule board appears, and it stays in the corner on the next pages. VI, The Double Dossier: an open folder in two pages: the olive page is the cover story, WHAT HE SAYS; the dark page is the truth, WHAT HE DOES. For each clue the student says the sentence out loud and taps the right page; the entry is stamped in. With six, the folder gets a CLASSIFIED stamp. VII, Your Two Lives: six questions to build (What do you usually do…? What are you doing right now?). The student builds one, taps Check, asks the teacher, and answers. VIII: the debate.

THE COMMON ERRORS

Loid work, he is work, right now he spies, look! she reads (say it, the corrector can’t see the look!), he spying, every day she is working, isn’t liking, Are you understand?, Is Loid go…?, Do Loid…?, What do you doing?, What you are doing?, Papa and Mama is.

PLACE YOUR BET (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after Scene by Scene, the trailer stops at the start of the family. The student bets on what happens next in the show, three times: Loid and Yor (they get married that same week, as a secret deal), Anya’s interview at the elite school (she gets in at the last minute) and the family’s new dog (he can see the future). They choose, say why with every day / right now, and only then tap Check. Wait five seconds in silence before they choose, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to bring it to their own life. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk (he work, is liking); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About six minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Warm-up and Two Truths and a Lie, IV Place Your Bet, VI The Double Dossier, VII Your Two Lives and VIII The Debate.
  • Listen: II The Trailer and III Scene by Scene.
  • Focus: V Every Day × Right Now.
  • Write: the homework (the lesson ends speaking).
