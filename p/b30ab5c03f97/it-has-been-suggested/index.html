It Has Been Suggested · C1 Unit 8 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on impersonal reporting for memos, minutes and formal emails: It has been suggested / proposed that…, It was agreed / decided that…, It is widely believed / hoped that…, It has come to our attention that…, There is thought to be…, It should be noted that…

The story: Northwind Ltd (invented) sends a memo to all staff after a board meeting: a trial four-day week, a risk for customer service, and people working unpaid on Fridays.

About 43 min: I · The Memo (10) · II · Personal or Impersonal? (9) · III · The Negotiation (8) · IV · Find the Mistakes (7) · V · Your Memo (9).

Open with: “Would you like a four-day work week? What could go wrong?”

The reporting passive (is said to) was in B2. Today is the register of memos and minutes, where nobody wants a name attached. Two traps: suggest + to (never) and the formal subjunctive (that the practice stop). The writing card is a real memo: let the student write about their own team, family or building.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

It has been suggested / proposed that… · It was agreed / decided that… · It is widely believed / hoped that… · It has come to our attention that… · There is thought / said to be… · the formal subjunctive: that he attend

THE MEMO

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

PERSONAL OR IMPERSONAL?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

YOUR MEMO

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

it was suggested to start, it agreed that, it has come to our attentions, it was proposed that he attends (formal: attend), there is thought that there is, we should to. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE NEGOTIATION (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

In the middle of the lesson, your student is the head of customer service and negotiates the four-day week with you, the Northwind director. Your secret limit is on your screen only: say no twice, and give in only if they offer a Friday rotation. Ask the four questions, wait five seconds in silence after each, and push with the PUSH line (only you see it). Close with The deal: they report it with “It was agreed that…”. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · The Memo.
  • Out loud: III · The Negotiation.
  • Focus: II · Personal or Impersonal? and IV · Find the Mistakes.
  • Write: V · Your Memo.
