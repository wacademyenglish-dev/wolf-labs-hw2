Final_v3_FINAL · English for Designers · Lesson 9 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: managing revisions and deadlines: a round of revisions, sign off, by vs until, the future perfect (I’ll have finished… by…), Could you send me… by…?, Just a heads-up, and a short status update (done, in progress, next, risk).

The story: A fictional email thread about posters for a book festival, then a designer’s to-do list that becomes a status update.

About 44 min: I · The Email Thread (10) · II · By or Until? (9) · III · The Friday Call (7) · IV · Your Status Update (14) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “What’s the worst file name you have ever saved? final_final_v7?”

There is no video in this lesson. In the status update (transforma), after the six sentences, ask the student to write the whole update as one email in the chat, and correct it together.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

This will be our second and final round of revisions. · Could you send me… by…? · I’ll have finished… by… · Once you sign off, I’ll… · Just a heads-up:… · I’m currently working on…

THE EMAIL THREAD

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

BY OR UNTIL?

One situation at a time. The student says which side it goes on and WHY, then taps. The color confirms and one line explains. The two columns build a board the student can photograph at the end.

YOUR STATUS UPDATE

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to send you a real status update of one of their projects, in English, before the next lesson.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

until Friday (as a deadline: by Friday), I will have finish (I will have finished), Once you will sign off (Once you sign off), I’m working in the mobile version (on). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

THE FRIDAY CALL (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

The email thread from I continues on the phone. Your student is Lia; you are Tom, and on Friday afternoon you ask for a new sponsor logo, a new headline, the files on Saturday and no extra cost. Your secret limit (only you see it) is softer: the printer takes files until Monday 9 AM, the logo is a must but the headline is not, and you can pay a small fee. Ask the four lines in order, insist a little, wait five seconds in silence after each, and don’t give your limit first. Then close the deal on screen and use the PUSH line. Recast quietly while they talk (I’ll have finished … by …, by / until, Once you sign off …, Just a heads-up); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
