The Press Conference · B1 Unit 9 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 9 (Asking politely and reporting). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student is a TV reporter at a big press conference. You are three famous guests (invented): an astronaut back from space, a football coach who lost the final, and a pop star with a new song. Then the student reports to the studio.

About 48 min: I · Speed Round (6) · II · The Press Room (15) · III · The Exclusive (8) · IV · Find the Mistakes (7) · V · On the Sofa (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Sixty seconds. Ready? Start the clock.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

question tags · tags and intonation · indirect questions · reported speech: said, told · reported questions and requests

SPEED ROUND

A 60-second speed round. Tap Start the clock, then Right or Pass for each item; the answer is on your screen. At the end of class, play again and beat the score.

THE PRESS ROOM

You are each guest. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the studio: the student reports with she said she was…, he told us… Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

ON THE SOFA

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

she said me, he said he is tired (no backshift), could you tell me where is the station, you were tired, wasn’t you, she asked are you OK, he said he will. B1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE EXCLUSIVE (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the press conference, the student wants an exclusive: twenty minutes, live, with the pop star. You are her manager. You ask four questions (the student answers in role with indirect questions, tags and “my boss told me…”), then you close a deal. Your secret limit is on your screen only: you say yes to live only if the student offers the new song as the first question. Wait five seconds in silence after each question, and use the PUSH line to resist. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 8 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Speed Round and IV · Find the Mistakes and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Press Room, III · The Exclusive and V · On the Sofa.
