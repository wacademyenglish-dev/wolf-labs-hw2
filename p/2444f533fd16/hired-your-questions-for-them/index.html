Your Questions for Them · WOLF HIRED · Lesson 8 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: asking questions at the end: indirect questions with statement order (Could you tell me what the team is like? Could you tell me if…?), I was wondering…, I’d love to know…, asking about success, challenges, advice and next steps, and closing warmly.

The story: An interview coach’s five questions, then turning direct questions into polite ones, then the student interviews the teacher.

About 39 min: I · Never Say “No Questions” (7) · II · Direct to Polite (14) · III · Your Turn to Interview Them (14) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “In your last interview, did you ask any questions at the end? What did you ask?”

Indirect question word order is the main grammar point: correct “Could you tell me what is the team like?” every time. In the role play, give real answers so the student can react before asking the next question.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Could you tell me what… is? · Could you tell me if…? · I was wondering… · I’d love to know… · How would you measure success…? · What are the next steps?

NEVER SAY “NO QUESTIONS”

CareerVidz, top 5 questions to ask at the end of an interview (0:00–2:55).

DIRECT TO POLITE

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

YOUR TURN TO INTERVIEW THEM

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to write three questions for their real target company, in indirect form.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Could you tell me what is the team like? (what the team is like), I was wondering how do you measure (how you measure), Could you tell me if there is? (correct!), No, you covered everything (always ask something). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · Never Say “No Questions”.
  • Focus: II · Direct to Polite and IV · I Can.
  • Out loud: III · Your Turn to Interview Them.
