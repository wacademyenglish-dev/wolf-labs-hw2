Only Then Did I See · C1 Unit 4 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on only + inversion: Only then / Only later did I…; Only when / Only after + clause, then inversion in the main clause; Only by + -ing did I…; Only if…, will you…

The story: An actress (invented) writes about ten years of stage fright, the night she froze on stage, and the grandmother’s diary she found in the attic.

About 41 min: I · How Do You Learn? (6) · II · The Memoir (10) · III · Where’s the Verb? (9) · IV · The Night She Froze (8) · V · Your Turning Point (8).

Open with: “How do you learn best? Let’s choose.”

This structure marks the turning point of a story. Students usually invert in the wrong clause: show that with only when / after / if, the first part is normal and the MAIN clause inverts. The memoir is emotional; read it slowly. The writing card is the student’s own turning point: let it be personal.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Only then did I… · Only later did I… · Only when + clause, did I… · Only after + noun / clause, did I… · Only by + -ing did I… · Only if…, will you…

HOW DO YOU LEARN?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

THE MEMOIR

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

WHERE’S THE VERB?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

YOUR TURNING POINT

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Only then I understood, Only when did the lights go down I froze, Only by practise I improved, Only if you rehearse you will succeed, Only after did I finish I relaxed, Only later I realized. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE NIGHT SHE FROZE (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the rule, your student is the jury for three people from the night the actress froze: the director, the woman who posted the video, and the stage manager on his phone. They read the evidence and give a verdict, starting with Only (Only if… will…, Only when… did…). There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the verdict harder. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON
