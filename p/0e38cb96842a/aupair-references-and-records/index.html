References and Records · Au Pair · Lesson 6 of 24

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: references (letters from people who saw the student with children) and the health form: allergies, vaccinations, medication, physical limitations.

The story: A fictional reference from the director of a fictional daycare in Recife about Mariana, and Mariana’s health information from a fictional doctor.

About 34 min: I · A Reference Letter (10) · II · Health Words (8) · III · The Health Form (12) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “If a family could call one person to ask about you with children, who would it be?”

Health is private: in class, always use the character. If the student wants to talk about her own allergies or medication to prepare her real form, let HER decide what to share. References must be real people who agreed to be contacted; relatives usually do not count.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

To whom it may concern, · I recommend her without hesitation · I’m allergic to… · I don’t have any allergies · My vaccinations are up to date · I take medication for… / none

A REFERENCE LETTER

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

HEALTH WORDS

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

THE HEALTH FORM

A practice form in English, filled in with a FICTIONAL person’s data (never the student’s real documents in class). The student reads the profile card and fills in the form; Check the form shows each field right or wrong, with the correct answer.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Homework: list two real references (name, role, how they know the student, email) and ask them before the application.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I have allergy of, my vaccines are in day, I take remedy, leaving a field empty instead of writing none. A2+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · A Reference Letter.
  • Out loud: II · Health Words.
  • Write: III · The Health Form.
  • Focus: IV · I Can.
