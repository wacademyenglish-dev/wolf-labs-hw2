The Auction House · Going Once, Going Twice

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: Tonight you and your student run an auction.

The grammar: The Jump: The describing word jumps in front of the thing. Then a or an?

Short class (about 49 min): I Your Treasure · III Catch Six · V The Jump · VI The Misprint · VII The Scale · IX Your Pick · XII Lot 00. Skip if you’re short on time: VIII, X, XI (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 69 min.

Open with: “Where did you get it?”

Tonight you and your student run an auction. Things arrive with a lot number and a dark lot card, and nothing sells without a correct description. In scene VI you compete for the lots: every bid is a new sentence, and the price only goes up when the sentence is right. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

The describing word goes before the thing: a small lamp. A or an by the sound: an old clock, a new clock; an umbrella, but a new umbrella. The for a thing you already said: There is a lamp. The lamp is small. Four pairs: big / small · old / new · heavy / light · cheap / expensive. The mistakes: a old clock, a clock old, and a for something already on the table.

THE AUDIO AND THE VIDEO

The clip is from BBC Learning English (0:05–1:08): two boys play a memory game. After it, the two lists play again slower, listen first, transcript after. Scene III starts with six short clips: listen, then a or an. Scene V has the auctioneer’s voice for four of the words. Every audio has pause and 0.75×; all transcripts were checked against the audio.

The words (II): bored, remember, prize, late and applause, before the video, in match the columns and the missing word. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (IV, extra): every line of the video; tap one to hear it again, and the lines with yellow words open the explanation.

YOUR PICK (IX) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

The lesson already has the bid, the sale and the coffee after the sale; here your student decides. Three short choices, one of three each: what to buy with 100 dollars, a gift for a best friend, one new thing for home. There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it). The options keep a / an + describing word, and the starters move to the (the second time). Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: II The Words, III Catch Six and IV Line by Line.
  • Focus: V The Jump and VIII The Bid.
  • Write: XII Lot 00.
