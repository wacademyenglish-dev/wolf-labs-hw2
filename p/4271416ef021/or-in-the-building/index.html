Or in the Building · The City Map

Taking THE out doesn’t make a sentence shorter. It makes you somebody else. At school is a student in class; at the school is a parent at the gate. The same goes for in prison / at the prison and in bed / in the bed. And a few never take THE: at work, at home, go home, go to bed, by bus. The screen is a city map: nobody in the voice messages says where they are, and the student drops a pin, INSIDE (one of them, no THE) or AT THE DOOR (only there, THE).

THE JURY (VII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the wrong door, your student is the jury for three small cases where one article changed the place: the hour at the gate, Leo and his card, Sam “at home” but in bed. They read the evidence and give a verdict; there is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the verdict harder. Recast quietly while they talk (at work, at home, never at the work); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — I Where Are They Now? (their people, right now), VII The Jury (three small cases, after the wrong door), VIII Monday, 8 AM (their places).
  • Listen — III The Map: the nine voice messages of the old lesson. The speaker is British on purpose: one message says in hospital (in the US: in the hospital). Tell the student.
  • Focus — II Two Messages, IV The Person Decides (four cards, four traps), V Station Signs (∅ or THE), VI The Wrong Door (every line is correct English; one sends him to the wrong place).
  • Write — IX Your Week. The red wavy line marks at the work, at the home, go to home, by the bus and go to the bed. It goes to their My notes.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student plays, pins, taps and writes. Only you open the follow-ups (“Answered”). Every question has a Show answer. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
