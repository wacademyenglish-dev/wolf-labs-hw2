Better Speaking · Episode 3 · I Think You Should… · B1

Episode 3 of Better Speaking, B1 version. Václav Němeček, a Czech footballer in China, says what he thinks in English. The language is opinion and advice with should / shouldn’t and I don’t think… should. The sister lesson for B2 is “In My Opinion”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — What do you think, What do you think. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — I think you should, Find the advice, Fix the traps, Choose the right word.
  • Write — Your advice. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

The to after should: You should to practice. “Eu concordo” becomes I am agree. And “acho que não” becomes I think that no.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, matches and writes. “Only you see this” is yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. The bar on top shows the season: eight episodes, today’s one lit. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
