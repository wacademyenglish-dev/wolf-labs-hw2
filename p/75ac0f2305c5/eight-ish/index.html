Better Speaking · Episode 8 · Eight-ish · B1

Episode 8, the last one of Better Speaking, B1 version. ABBA: “We sold like eight or nine golden records.” The language is approximate numbers and times: about, around, -ish (eight-ish), more or less. It closes the season. The sister lesson for B2 is “Sort Of, Kind Of”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — More or less, How much? How long. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — Not exact, Say it another way, Fix the traps, Choose the right word.
  • Write — Your week, more or less. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

“Mais ou menos de” becomes more or less of 20 minutes. Times without at: meet in the eight-ish. And age with have: she has around of 30 years.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, matches and writes. “Only you see this” is yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. The bar on top shows the season: eight episodes, today’s one lit. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
