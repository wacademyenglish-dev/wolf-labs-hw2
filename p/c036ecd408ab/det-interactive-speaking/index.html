Interactive Speaking · DET Prep · Lesson 10 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the Interactive Speaking question (new since July 2025): a voice asks several questions about one topic, one at a time, and the student answers each in about 35 seconds, with no preparation time. The key: answer + one detail + one example, in the tense of the question.

The story: No story: one interview about free time, six recorded questions that move from habits (present simple) to change (present perfect), opinion, imagination (would) and advice.

About 36 min: I · Answer + 1 + 1 (9) · II · Six Questions (16) · III · Quick Choices (7) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: Ask, without warning: “What did you have for breakfast?” Then: “That was Interactive Speaking: no time to prepare.”

Play each question ONCE, like the test, then tap Start the clock. Do not repeat the question unless the student really did not hear it. After each answer, check the list and read the model; then move on quickly: rhythm matters in this lesson.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

answer first · + one detail · + one example · Do you…? → I do / I usually… · Have you / Has it…? → present perfect · If you had…? → I would…

ANSWER + 1 + 1

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

SIX QUESTIONS

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

QUICK CHOICES

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. If the student freezes, teach a 2-second starter: “Well, I’d say…” or “That’s a good question…”.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

one-word answers, I am agree, has it changed? — yes, I start, if you had… — I will, long silence before answering. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Answer + 1 + 1 and IV · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · Six Questions and III · Quick Choices.
