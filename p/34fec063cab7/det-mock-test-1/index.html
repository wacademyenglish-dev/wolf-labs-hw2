Mock Test 1 · DET Prep · Lesson 15 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the first full mock test: every question type of the course, in order, with the real clock. All content is original (never from the real test). Teach nothing during the tasks; give feedback only at the end of each part.

The story: No story: a full practice test. Five parts: Read and Select → Fill in the Blanks and Read and Complete → Listen and Type → a conversation at Student Services and a short text → speaking and writing (photo, topic, two recorded questions, writing sample).

About 46 min: I · Read and Select (3) · II · Fill in and Complete (7) · III · Listen and Type (5) · IV · Listening and Reading (7) · V · Speaking and Writing (20) · VI · Results (4).

Open with: “Today is test day. Water, quiet room, phone away. Ready?” Keep the tone calm and serious.

It takes about 50 minutes. Write down, for each part, what went well and one thing to fix: that list is the plan for the last lesson. There is no official score here; do not promise a number. Use the checklist results to say which parts are strong and which need work.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Read and Select · Fill in the Blanks · Read and Complete · Listen and Type · Interactive Listening and Reading · Speaking and Writing

READ AND SELECT

Read and Select: the words stay blurred until you tap Start the clock. The student taps only the REAL English words. At the end: green = found, dashed amber = missed, red = a fake word tapped (that costs points in the test).

FILL IN AND COMPLETE

The gaps: the student types only the missing letters (the box moves to the next gap by itself). With a clock, the text stays blurred until you start it. Check paints right and wrong and shows the full word. Spelling counts.

LISTEN AND TYPE

Listen and Type: the audio plays up to 3 times, like the test. The student types, then Check compares word by word: green is right, red is missing or misspelled. Read the tip out loud.

LISTENING AND READING

Listen or read, then choose one option. The colors show right and wrong, and one line says why. Ask the student WHY before you move on.

SPEAKING AND WRITING

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

RESULTS

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. For each line the student does NOT tap, choose one lesson of the course to review before Mock Test 2.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

tapping fake words, spelling in the gaps, missing small words in dictation, stopping before the clock in speaking, informal register in the sample. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Read and Select and VI · Results.
  • Write: II · Fill in and Complete.
  • Listen: III · Listen and Type and IV · Listening and Reading.
  • Out loud: V · Speaking and Writing.
