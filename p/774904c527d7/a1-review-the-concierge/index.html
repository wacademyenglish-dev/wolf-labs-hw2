The Concierge · A1 Unit 5 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 5 (Do, don’t, let’s). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student is the concierge of a small hotel, with a map on the desk. You are three lost tourists.

About 47 min: I · The Ladder (8) · II · The Concierge (15) · III · Find the Mistakes (7) · IV · Your City Tour (5) · V · The Hot Seat (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Pick a door.” There is no order. One minute per door, no notes.

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

the imperative in steps · Don’t + verb · first, then, finally · let’s / let’s not / Shall we…? · directions (turn left at…, it’s on your right) · polite orders with please

THE LADDER

Six steps from easy to hard. A good answer: tap Go up. Not yet: Try again on the same step.

THE CONCIERGE

In this mission the student does not ask: the student TELLS. You ask the way to a place; your card (only you see it) has the route on the map. The student says the way in five parts (start, straight, turn, where it is, one warning); tap each part when you hear it. Any good way counts. Then plan the evening together: the student suggests with let’s, let’s not and Shall we…?. Listen for the three slips on your screen.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

You turn left (verb first!), No take the bus, Not open, Let’s to go, Let’s going, turn left in the bank. A1: one correction per answer is enough.

YOUR CITY TOUR (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

A short break in the middle of the review: a tourist visits the student’s own city. Two light choices: a market or a museum, on foot or by bus (Let’s…, First… Then…, Don’t…). Answer first yourself, as a model. Then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and ask the PUSH line (only you see it): a yes / no question. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves. About 5 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. There are three; use one when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Ladder and III · Find the Mistakes and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Concierge, IV · Your City Tour and V · The Hot Seat.
