So That Nobody Gets Lost · B2 Unit 8 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new B2 lesson on expressing purpose (so that + subject + can / will / won’t; in order to / so as to + verb; in order not to) and reason (because + sentence; because of / due to + noun).

The story: A new night trail opens in Pine Ridge Park (invented). Ranger Ana’s notes explain every lantern, marker and rule.

About 37 min: I · The Ranger’s Notes (10) · II · Purpose or Reason? (9) · III · The Ranger’s Dilemmas (8) · IV · Your Trail (10).

Open with: “Have you ever been lost on a trail, in a city or in a big building? What happened?”

Two Portuguese traps: “para que” becomes “for that” or “for to”, and “por causa de” becomes “because the floods”. The test question for every sentence: what for? (purpose) or why? (reason). The writing card uses a real place the student knows.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

so that + subject + can / will · in order to / so as to + verb · in order not to / so as not to · because + sentence · because of / due to + noun · purpose × reason

THE RANGER’S NOTES

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

PURPOSE OR REASON?

One situation at a time. The student says which side it goes on and WHY, then taps. The color confirms and one line explains. The two columns build a board the student can photograph at the end.

YOUR TRAIL

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

for to see (to see / in order to see), so that nobody get (gets), because the floods (because of the floods), in order to not (in order not to), for that people can (so that people can). B2: one correction per answer is enough.

THE RANGER’S DILEMMAS (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Ranger Ana has three hard choices: a wedding party at the camp, the closed river path and the phone signal on the night trail. Your student chooses one option, or says their own idea, and explains it with purpose and reason (so that…, in order not to…, because of…). There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: II · Purpose or Reason?.
  • Out loud: III · The Ranger’s Dilemmas.
  • Write: IV · Your Trail.
