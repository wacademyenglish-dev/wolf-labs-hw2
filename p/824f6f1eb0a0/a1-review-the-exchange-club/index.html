The Exchange Club · A1 Unit 7 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 7 (What I like). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student runs the club night at a conversation club. You are three new members: Ana, Joe and Mei.

About 46 min: I · Bingo (8) · II · The Exchange Club (15) · III · Club Night (5) · IV · Find the Mistakes (7) · V · Would You Rather? (7) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Pick a door.” There is no order. One minute per door, no notes.

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

love / like / hate + -ing · he / she likes + -ing · like it / like them · object pronouns after the verb (call her) · pronouns after with / for / to · call (no to)

BINGO

A 3×3 card of quick tasks. The student picks a square and does it out loud; tap Done. Three in a line (across, down or diagonal) is a BINGO. Keep going for a second line if there is time.

THE EXCHANGE CLUB

You are each member. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the member card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then the student chooses a partner and says why, with loves …ing and it / him / her. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

WOULD YOU RATHER?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

she loves cook, I like (no it), I call she, with I, call to me, he like playing. A1: one correction per answer is enough.

CLUB NIGHT (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the student chooses a partner, a light ranking of the three club activities (cooking, video games, walking). Tap “Show my teacher’s order” to give yours; the student says why with love / hate + -ing, it / them. Answer first yourself, as a model. Then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and ask the PUSH line (only you see it): a yes / no question. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves. About 5 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. There are three; use one when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Bingo and IV · Find the Mistakes and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Exchange Club, III · Club Night and V · Would You Rather?.
