SBAR Handover · English for Healthcare · Lesson 13 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: handover and escalation with SBAR (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation): who you are and the problem in one sentence, only the relevant background, findings with numbers and a clear concern (I’m worried that…), a clear request (Could you…? Would you consider…?).

The story: An official AHRQ TeamSTEPPS video: a night nurse calls a doctor about an 82-year-old patient who is suddenly confused (possible sepsis). Then a physiotherapist’s SBAR in order (suspected clot), and two cases for the student (fictional).

About 36 min: I · An SBAR Call (8) · II · SBAR in Order (8) · III · Your Handover (16) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “How do you call a doctor at 3 a.m. in your hospital? What do you say first?”

SBAR comes from teamwork and patient safety programs (TeamSTEPPS is from the U.S. Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality and the Department of Defense). Numbers matter: practice saying them clearly (one hundred and ten over sixty; eighty-nine percent).

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

This is … from… · I’m calling about… · He / She was admitted… with… · Her … is … (numbers) · I’m worried that… · Could you…? / Would you consider…?

AN SBAR CALL

AHRQ, “TeamSTEPPS: SBAR in Inpatient Medical Teams” (2 min, official captions checked on 15/10/2026). American English, real medical vocabulary.

SBAR IN ORDER

The events are mixed. The student taps them in order; wrong order shows in red. When it’s right, the student tells the whole story with first, then, after that, finally.

YOUR HANDOVER

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to write an SBAR for a real (anonymized) case from their work.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

telling the whole history before the problem, no numbers, no clear request, I think maybe perhaps… (too vague). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.
