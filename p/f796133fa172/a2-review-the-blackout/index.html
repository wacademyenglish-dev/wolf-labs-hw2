The Blackout · A2 Unit 3 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 3 (What was happening). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Last night at nine the lights went out in your student’s building. Your student is the building manager. You are three neighbors, then the electric company.

About 48 min: I · Memory (8) · II · The Blackout (14) · III · The Building Meeting (7) · IV · Find the Mistakes (7) · V · Small Talk (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Open two cards. Find the pair, then make the sentence.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

past continuous: was / were + -ing · the question: What were you doing? · when / while: past continuous × past simple · used to / didn’t use to · would for old habits

MEMORY

A memory game. The student opens two cards; when they match, the student makes a sentence with the pair (example under Show answer).

THE BLACKOUT

You are each neighbor. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the electric company: the student says what was happening in one apartment at nine. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

SMALL TALK

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

she were cooking, I was cook, when the lights go out, I use to (past), did you used to, what you were doing?. A2: one correction per answer is enough.

THE BUILDING MEETING (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the interviews, your student is the judge at the building meeting: three complaints from the blackout, each with three facts. There is no right answer: let them choose a verdict, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Listen for was / were + -ing and when / while. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · Memory.
  • Out loud: II · The Blackout, III · The Building Meeting and V · Small Talk.
  • Focus: IV · Find the Mistakes and VI · I Can.
