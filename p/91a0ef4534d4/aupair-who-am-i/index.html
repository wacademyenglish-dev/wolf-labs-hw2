Who Am I? · Au Pair · Lesson 1 of 24

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the first lesson of the Au Pair course (24 lessons, 6 modules: the profile, forms and documents, the interviews, your rights and your safety, life in the house, limits and life outside). Today: introducing yourself to a host family with the present simple and can.

The story: Laura, a real au pair from Spain (AuPairCare video), tells how nervous she was and how her host family welcomed her. Then the student answers a host mom’s first questions and writes five lines about herself.

About 47 min: I · Meet Laura (10) · II · Say It Right (9) · III · Tell Me About You (9) · IV · Your First Lines (9) · V · Your First Choice (6) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “If a family could ask you only one question, what would you like them to ask?”

This course takes the student from the agency profile to her first week in the house, with what she needs to know to be protected. The tone is warm: many students are nervous about leaving home. Validate that (Laura was nervous too).

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

I am 22 (years old) · I’m from… · I live with… · I love + -ing · I can + verb · she loves (-s)

MEET LAURA

AuPairCare, “What is an Au Pair | Meet Laura from Spain” (2 min, captions checked on 15/10/2026). BEFORE: what does an au pair do? WATCH once. AFTER: five questions. Play it twice if needed: it is real, natural English with a Spanish accent.

SAY IT RIGHT

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

TELL ME ABOUT YOU

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

YOUR FIRST LINES

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to keep the five lines: in Lesson 2 they become the start of the letter to the family.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I have 22 years, I can to drive, my mom work, I love play. A2+: one correction per answer is enough.

YOUR FIRST CHOICE (V) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the five lines, three quick choices before the first call: which family to call first (the city family with three kids or the farm family with a baby), which ONE photo goes on the profile, and which ONE “I can” line the family reads first. There is no right answer: let the student choose and say why. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast quietly while they talk (I love + -ing, I can + verb, she loves); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: II · Say It Right and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: III · Tell Me About You and V · Your First Choice.
  • Write: IV · Your First Lines.
