What’s Included? · Selling Services · Lesson 28

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 28 (it includes · it doesn’t include · it / them). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: The student reads Bluepeak’s one-page proposal for Oakridge Paper (fictional): payroll for 1,800 employees, what is included and what isn’t. Nora Bell calls with three questions (training, SAP licenses, travel). He sorts eight items into INCLUDED × NOT INCLUDED, debates discounts, fills in the CRM after the call and writes a four-line summary of a real proposal.

About 59 min: I · Your Proposals (5) · II · The One-Page Proposal (8) · III · “Is Training Included?” (7) · IV · It Includes, Send It (8) · V · Included or Not Included? (7) · VI · The Debate: Is a Discount a Good Idea? (6) · VII · The CRM Record (7) · VIII · Your 4-Line Summary (8) · IX · I Can (3).

Open with: “How many proposals do you send in a month? What do clients always ask about?”

Two small things that Brazilians lose: the -s of “it includes” (“the price include”, “it don’t include”) and the object pronoun, because Portuguese drops it (“vou mandar hoje” → “I’ll send today”; it must be “I’ll send it today”), plus “we check they” instead of “them”. In V ask him to SAY the full sentence before tapping. In VI take the other side and push a little. In VII the form checks each field; numbers can be 1,800 or 1800.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

It includes … / It doesn’t include … · Does it include …? Is … included? · I’ll send it today. · We prepare them and send them by …

YOUR PROPOSALS

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

THE ONE-PAGE PROPOSAL

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

“IS TRAINING INCLUDED?”

A short audio dialogue (American voices). BEFORE: one question. LISTEN: play it once without stopping (0.75× is fine for A1), then the questions; Show answer after the student tries. The transcript opens only after the audio ends: read it together and point to the highlighted words.

IT INCLUDES, SEND IT

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

INCLUDED OR NOT INCLUDED?

One situation at a time. The student says which side it goes on and WHY, then taps. The color confirms and one line explains. The two columns build a board the student can photograph at the end.

THE DEBATE: IS A DISCOUNT A GOOD IDEA?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

THE CRM RECORD

A practice form in English, filled in with a FICTIONAL person’s data (never the student’s real documents in class). The student reads the profile card and fills in the form; Check the form shows each field right or wrong, with the correct answer.

YOUR 4-LINE SUMMARY

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. If he has a real proposal, help him write the “What’s included / What’s not included” box in English.

THE TECH WORDS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno vende serviços B2B. Você não precisa ser especialista; precisa saber do que ele está falando. Não ensine estes termos: deixe que ele explique o trabalho dele (é conversa real).

  • BPO (Business Process Outsourcing): Terceirização de processos: a empresa contratada EXECUTA uma rotina do cliente (folha de pagamento, contabilidade, fiscal, contas a pagar). O cliente para de fazer aquilo dentro de casa.
  • BTO (Business Transformation Outsourcing): Terceirização com transformação: além de executar, a contratada MUDA o processo (tecnologia, automação, integração de sistemas). Vende-se como “melhorar”, não só “fazer por você”.
  • ERP: Sistema de gestão que integra finanças, compras, estoque, RH etc. Exemplos (marcas): SAP, Oracle, TOTVS. Um BPO/BTO não vende o ERP: trabalha COM ele ou o integra.
  • Scope (scope of services): Escopo: a lista do que o serviço inclui e do que não inclui. Base da proposta e do contrato.
  • Out of scope · charged separately: Fora do escopo · cobrado à parte (ex.: viagens, mudanças no sistema do cliente).
  • License (software license): Licença de uso do software (SAP, Oracle…). Normalmente o cliente já paga direto ao fabricante.
  • Tax reports: Obrigações/relatórios fiscais e trabalhistas que a empresa entrega ao governo (no Brasil: eSocial, DCTFWeb etc.).
  • Vacation pay: Pagamento de férias (no Brasil, com o 1/3 constitucional): parte do cálculo da folha.
  • CRM record (opportunity): Registro da oportunidade no CRM: empresa, contato, valor, data e próximo passo.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

The price include training (The price includes training), It don’t include travel (It doesn’t include travel), I’ll send to you today (I’ll send it to you today), We check they every month (We check them). A1+: one correction per answer is enough.
