The Flatmate · A1 Unit 6 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 6 (My day). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: There is a free room in your student’s apartment. You are three people who want it: Sofia, Marcus and Hana.

About 46 min: I · Memory (8) · II · The Flatmate (15) · III · Your Day (6) · IV · House Rules (5) · V · The Hot Seat (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Pick a door.” There is no order. One minute per door, no notes.

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

like / don’t like, Do you like…? · he / she + -s · don’t / doesn’t, Does…? · telling the time · dates and ordinals · always / usually / never

MEMORY

A memory game. The student opens two cards; when they match, the student makes a sentence with the pair (example under Show answer).

THE FLATMATE

You are each person. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the owner: the student tells you about one person with he / she + -s and says who is the best flatmate. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

YOUR DAY

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

she work, he doesn’t cooks, Yes, I like (for Yes, I do), she don’t never cook, in seven o’clock, in May 5th. A1: one correction per answer is enough.

HOUSE RULES (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the student writes about their day, two light house rules for the new flatmate: who washes the dishes, and loud music until when. Choose one of three, with always / never and the time. Answer first yourself, as a model. Then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and ask the PUSH line (only you see it): a yes / no question. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves. About 5 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. There are three; use one when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · Memory.
  • Out loud: II · The Flatmate, IV · House Rules and V · The Hot Seat.
  • Write: III · Your Day.
  • Focus: VI · I Can.
