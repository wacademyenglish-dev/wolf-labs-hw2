Low-Fi First · English for Designers · Lesson 6 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: presenting unfinished work: still, for now, just a first sketch, haven’t … yet; the future of the project (will, might, going to); and asking for a specific kind of feedback (Does … make sense? What’s missing?).

The story: A short NN/g video about showing sketches to leaders, then two fictional early designs: a pet clinic wireframe and a small apartment floor plan.

About 38 min: I · Is It Too Early? (6) · II · Final or Not Yet? (12) · III · Present the Sketch (16) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “Show me (or describe) the messiest sketch you ever made for a project. Did the client see it?”

Act as a manager who comments on the colors of a gray wireframe; the student must redirect: “Please ignore the colors for now. What we’re testing is…”.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

This is still a rough version of… · Please ignore … for now. · … might …, depending on… · What we’re testing here is… · Next week, we’re going to… · Does … make sense?

IS IT TOO EARLY?

Nielsen Norman Group, presenting low-fidelity prototypes to stakeholders (0:00–1:22).

FINAL OR NOT YET?

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

PRESENT THE SKETCH

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to bring an early sketch of their own project for the feedback lessons.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

This is the final? (Is this the final version?), We will to test (We will test), Maybe the sofa will can go (The sofa might go), You like it? (Does the order make sense?). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · Is It Too Early?.
  • Focus: II · Final or Not Yet? and IV · I Can.
  • Out loud: III · Present the Sketch.
