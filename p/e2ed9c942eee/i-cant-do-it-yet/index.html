I Can’t Do It… Yet · Naruto Shippuden · The Training Scroll

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: From the worst student to a hero… almost.

The grammar: Can, Can’t, Yet: Can + verb. Never to, never -s.

Short class (about 49 min): I The Village · II The Words · III The Trailer · V Can, Can’t, Yet · VI The Training Scroll · VII Your Ninja File · VIII Questions. Skip if you’re short on time: IV (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 54 min.

Open with: “Naruto can’t do a simple trick at school. Now, after two years, what can he do? Guess.”

Naruto Shippuden (2007–2017) is the Japanese anime from Masashi Kishimoto’s manga. Naruto Uzumaki grows up alone; at ninja school he is the worst student, and his dream is to be Hokage, the leader of the village. In Shippuden he comes home after two and a half years of training. The trailer is the official English one (VIZ Media). The grammar: can / can’t for ability (I can run, I can’t fly), questions with Can you…? (Yes, I can / No, I can’t), and the magic word yet: I can’t do it… yet means not now, but one day.

A note for the teacher: the trailer has fights, but no blood. It has no captions; the five marks follow the timing of a local transcription of the audio, and each moment is told in our own words.

Warm-up and Put It in Order (I): two parts. 1 · Warm-up: three questions about the student’s life, one at a time (big on the screen, with “Ask more” under each and one short line about the anime under the photo). A1: short answers are fine. 2 · Put It in Order: four cards of Naruto’s story, shuffled. The student taps them in order (“First… then…”); then the cards show the real order, and the student tells the story with can / can’t.

The words (II): grown up, impressive, strength, prepared and save, before the video, in match the columns and the word in your life. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (V) lists the lines with the yellow words: tap a line, hear it again, and the student says it like the character.

The ending (IX, Questions): five questions with can and can’t (what you do very well, what you can’t do yet, two languages, your best friend, what a ninja can do that you can’t). The student answers, then asks the teacher two of them. Speaking only.

ON SCREEN

The cover is an anime training ground in a forest at dusk. I: the village (the show, the boy, the story). III: the trailer in three steps. Before: clues, one by one, to guess who is coming home. Watch: One word is wrong (say the right one) and Which word did you hear?, each line with a ▶ time: tap it and the video jumps to that moment. After: an old friend says “you got all grown up. Impressive.” and the student picks how Naruto answers (the answer changes); then a slider (can Naruto do everything?) and the student says why. IV: five frames on the film strip. V, Can, Can’t, Yet: Naruto’s notebook has five mistakes (can to fly, cans, can copies, don’t can, Do you can). The student says the right words out loud, then taps Check; the rule appears. The rule stays in the corner on the next pages. VI, The Training Scroll: six seals on an ink scroll (Naruto as a kid, Naruto now, Sakura, Gaara, a question, Naruto’s dream). For each one the student says if the sentence is true or a lie, and if it is a lie, says the true sentence out loud. Every right answer lights a seal, fills the chakra bar and raises the rank from Academy to Hokage. VII, Your Ninja File: the student builds six Can you…? questions out loud (swim, cook, drive, an instrument, languages, one thing you can’t do yet), taps Check and interviews the teacher. VIII: the questions.

THE COMMON ERRORS

He can to fly, she cans, he can makes, I don’t can, No, I not can, Do you can…?, Can you to…?, he is can.

OK OR NOT OK? (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the trailer: three things from Naruto’s story, now in your student’s life. Training every day for two years, stopping and trying again tomorrow, and friends who say “Show us!” (sing for them?). Two colored buttons, OK or Not OK, and an almost-ready sentence with can / can’t … yet. There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then wait five seconds in silence and use the PUSH line (only you see it). Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I The Village, IV OK or Not OK?, VII The Training Scroll and VIII Your Ninja File.
  • Listen: II The Words, III The Trailer and V Line by Line.
  • Focus: VI Can, Can’t, Yet.
  • Write: IX Questions.
