The Expedition · B2 Unit 1 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 1 (The past in layers and the future). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: A research team has just come back after fourteen months on a remote island. Your student is making the documentary. You are three explorers: Ines (biologist), Teo (engineer) and Maya (photographer, who found a diary from 1962).

About 42 min: I · The Ladder (8) · II · The Documentary (15) · III · The Arena (7) · IV · In the Cabin (8) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “Climb the ladder. Six steps, from easy to hard.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

past perfect · past perfect continuous · used to × be / get used to + -ing · future perfect and future continuous · by × until · was going to

THE LADDER

Six steps from easy to hard. A good answer: tap Go up. Not yet: Try again on the same step.

THE DOCUMENTARY

You are each explorer. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then the student records the voice-over in layers (had been…, will have…). Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

IN THE CABIN

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I’m used to sleep, I was counting for six hours when… (for had been counting), by next year I will publish, until Friday (for a deadline: by Friday), nobody lived there for thirty years (for had been living), I was going to called. B2: one correction per answer is enough.

THE ARENA (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the documentary, one real argument: the station needs money, so should the island open to tourists or stay closed? The student picks a side and defends it for one minute with had been …ing, get used to and will have done; then they swap and defend the other side. Wait five seconds in silence before they start, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Ladder and V · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Documentary and III · The Arena and IV · In the Cabin.
