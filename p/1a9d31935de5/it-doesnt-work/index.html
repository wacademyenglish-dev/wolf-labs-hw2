It Doesn’t Work · The Night Technician

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: A laundromat at 11:30 at night.

The grammar: The S Moves: The S never disappears. It moves.

Short class (about 51 min): I Your House · II Eight Machines · III The S Moves · IV One, or More? · V This or That · VI The Service Form · VII The Signs · XI Your Service Form. Skip if you’re short on time: VIII, IX, X (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 72 min.

Open with: “What doesn’t work well in your house?”

A laundromat at 11:30 at night. Eight machines on the screen: six turning, two with a handwritten sign. The student is the night technician with a service form: they build a Does it…? question for each machine out loud, tap Check, and the form lights OK or ✗. Along the way: a woman at the back who isn’t there to wash anything, and a sign on the door that says WE DON’T CLOSE. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

don’t / doesn’t + a plain verb; Do / Does…? The S never disappears: it moves (she works → she doesn’t work). The common mistake writes it twice: she doesn’t works, Does it gets hot? Choose by counting: one thing takes doesn’t / does; more than one takes don’t / do.

THIS OR THAT (V) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

In the middle of the night shift, three light choices about the student’s own laundry: fix the machine or call someone, wash at night or in the morning, a book or the phone while they wait. Answer first yourself, as a model. Then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and ask the PUSH line (only you see it): a yes / no question. Recast quietly while they talk (it don’t work → it doesn’t work); at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves. About 5 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. There are three; use one when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Your House, II Eight Machines, VI The Service Form (six checks), VII The Signs (match each sign to its sentence), VIII The Support Call, IX The Woman at the Back, X The Door.
  • Focus: III The S Moves (tap the S and watch it move into DOES), IV One, or More? (sort the things into ONE and MORE THAN ONE), V This or That (would you rather, out loud).
  • Write: XI Your Service Form, with the red wavy line for it don’t, doesn’t works, Does it gets, Do it and it not work. It goes to their My notes.

THE SUPPORT CALL

Round 1: the student calls with a broken washing machine; you are support. Round 2: you call, the student is support and asks at least three Does it…? questions. Tap the parts you hear; the classic mistake has its own button. A one-minute timer is on the screen.

THE LAST TWO SCENES

The woman: the student opens six lines one by one and taps I know it when they can say why she is there. The door: read slowly. Don’t finish the sentence for them.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The machines, the form and the signs move on both screens. G opens this guide.
