The Old Photo · Maple Falls, 1970

An easy reader in five short chapters. Grandma Rose gives the student her old photo album: Maple Falls, her small town, in 1970. The photos are faded sepia. On the back of each one Grandma wrote a short chapter. The student reads it, answers one question, then describes the photo: when the description is right, the photo gets its color back. Then Grandma asks about the town today. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

Then: there was (one) · there were (many). No: there wasn’t a… / there weren’t any… Question: Was there…? How many… were there? Now: there is / there are. The mistakes: had for a place (In 1970 had a cinema), there was two, weren’t no, the present for 1970, and There was…? as a question.

WOULD YOU RATHER? (VII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After Grandma Rose’s album, the student chooses: a Saturday night in Maple Falls (the little cinema or the music in the square), then life in 1970 or today. Answer first, then let them choose; wait five seconds in silence and let them say why with there was / there were / there wasn’t. The PUSH line (only you see it) is a quick yes or no. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one light personal question. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I You and Your Town, II Grandma’s Album, IV Then and Now (you are Grandma; answer fast), V Your Town (a wheel in a fixed order), VI Cousin Leo Calls (you are Leo), VII Would You Rather? (Maple Falls or today: the student chooses).
  • Focus: III The Rule (five wrong lines to fix out loud).
  • Write: VIII, the empty page: their own town then and now, with the red wavy line for was two, were a, had, weren’t no, the present for the past and There was…?. It goes to their My notes.

THE BIG QUESTION

What was there in your town when you were a child, and what is there now? Give it time: this is where the real conversation is.
