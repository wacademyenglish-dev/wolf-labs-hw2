The Fifth Seat · The Arctic Gate

The ship Polaris leaves for the Arctic in three days, and one seat is empty. Four people applied. The screen is the gate: four dark files with a job icon and no faces. The student asks Is she a doctor?, opens the evidence and stamps TRUE or FALSE, until the liar appears. Then the student interviews for the seat too. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

He is / She is + a / an + job: She is a doctor. He is an engineer. A country takes no article: from Egypt. Questions: the verb jumps to the front, it is not copied: Is she a doctor? Yes, she is. / No, he isn’t. The mistakes: She is doctor (the job with no article) and Is she is…?

THE AUDIO

Every line has pause and 0.75×; the transcript opens after listening; all were checked against the audio. In scene III the candidates speak for themselves (Hello, I am Nadia. I am a doctor…) and the student says it about them (She is a doctor). In scene VII Ivo’s file has the truth: He isn’t an engineer. He is a cook.

ON BOARD (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Three days before the Polaris leaves, three light “would you rather” choices, two options each: the cabin neighbour (Tomás or Mei), a job on the ship for one day, and a friend on board. There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) for a yes/no follow-up. The starters keep He is a pilot / She is a translator / He isn’t a… in their mouth. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. The big decision (who gets the seat) stays in IX.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: II Radio Log, III Four Files, V The Verb Jumps.
  • Out loud: I Your Job, VII Who Is Lying, VIII On Board (would you rather, three times), IX The Interview (six questions, then the student decides who goes, with because).
  • Focus: IV The Missing Word (a, an or nothing), VI Stamp the Files (ask BEFORE opening the evidence).
  • Write: X, the file of their family, with the red wavy line for she is doctor, Is she is, a engineer, from the Egypt and he a pilot. It goes to their My notes.

IN THE LIVE ROOM
