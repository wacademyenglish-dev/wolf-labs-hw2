The Jury Room · B2 Unit 3 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 3 (The past that didn’t happen). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: A wedding cake arrived three hours late and the couple went to court. Your student leads the jury. You are three witnesses: the baker, the driver (two churches with the same name!) and the intern who wrote half the address.

About 42 min: I · The Die (8) · II · The Witnesses (15) · III · The Council Vote (7) · IV · The Hot Seat (8) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “Roll the die. The number chooses the challenge.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

third conditional · mixed: past cause, present result · mixed: present fact, past result · should have · could have · turning points: I wouldn’t be here

THE DIE

Tap Roll the die: the number chooses one of the six topics (no repeats until all six are done). The student does the challenge from memory.

THE WITNESSES

You are each witness. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the judge: the student gives the verdict with should have / if … had … Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

if he would have called, she should had checked, if I had moved, I would have been there now (for would be), could called, if I didn’t choose (for hadn’t chosen), if I wasn’t shy, I would asked. B2: one correction per answer is enough.

THE COUNCIL VOTE (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the verdict, the student votes on three proposals: a refund for the couple, a second chance for the driver, a new rule for interns. Yes, No, or propose a change; then they say why with should have, could have and if … had. There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the vote harder. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Die and V · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Witnesses, III · The Council Vote and IV · The Hot Seat.
