Walk Me Through It · English for Designers · Lesson 4 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: presenting design decisions: the walkthrough (Let me walk you through…, Our goal was to…), decision + reason (We chose… because…, We moved… so that…, We used… to…) and answering opinions (Yes, … Can you help me understand…?).

The story: A video about getting designs approved, then a fictional gym app (FitNook) presented in a design review.

About 34 min: I · Before the Meeting (8) · II · A Walkthrough (10) · III · Decision + Reason (12) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “Think of a design you made. Why did you choose those colors? Say it in one sentence.”

For graphic designers and interior designers, swap the app for a poster or a room; the structure is the same. Insist on the reason: when the student says “we chose blue”, ask “why?” until a so that / because appears.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Let me walk you through… · Our goal was to… · We chose… because… · We moved… so that… · As a result,… · Yes,… Can you help me understand…?

BEFORE THE MEETING

Synthesis, how to present design decisions to stakeholders (3:41–5:43): preparation, the pause, lead with yes, help me understand.

A WALKTHROUGH

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

DECISION + REASON

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to bring one screen, poster or room of their own to explain in the next lessons.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

We choosed (We chose), so that people can to reach (so that people can reach), for make it faster (to make it faster), I like this color (give a reason, not a taste). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · Before the Meeting and II · A Walkthrough.
  • Focus: III · Decision + Reason and IV · I Can.
