Where Are You From? · Street Voices

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: Nadia Brooks does street interviews at a market in London.

The grammar: The Rule: Question word, helper, person, verb. The little word goes at the end.

Short class (about 48 min): I You and Your City · III Real Voices · V The Lost Questions · VI The Rule · VII Live at 7 PM · IX Your Pick · XI After the Show. Skip if you’re short on time: VIII, X (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 62 min.

Open with: “Where do you live? Is it a big city or a small town?”

Nadia Brooks does street interviews at a market in London. Today six people answered, but the wind broke her microphone, and the recorder kept only the answers. The student hears each answer and gives back the question; every right question fills the empty line on an ID card. Then, live at 7 PM, eight questions with does stamp a passport. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

wh + do / does + person + verb: Where do you live? What does she do? With be, no do: Where are you from? When is your birthday? from / with at the end. does takes the s (Where does she live?). who for a person, what for a thing or a job.

THE TRAP

Many learners put the little word first (From where are you? With who you live?) and ask without a helper (Where you live?) or mix be and do (Where are you live? Where do you from?). Also: What do you make? for a job, What is your favorite singer?, Where does she lives?, Where do she work?

THE VIDEO

Four real people from BBC Learning English say where they are from and where they live, slowly and clearly, in four parts. Tap WHERE / WHAT / WHO / WHEN every time the student hears one.

The words (II): originally, diverse, shops, museums and markets, before the video, in match the columns and hot seat. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (IV, extra): every line of the video; tap one to hear it again, and the lines with yellow words open the explanation.

YOUR PICK (IX) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After Nadia’s microphone, three light choices: a guest for the show, a new city for one year, who you live with there. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it): a quick yes or no. Each answer ends with a question back to you, so answer it. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
