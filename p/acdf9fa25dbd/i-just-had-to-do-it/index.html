Better Speaking · Episode 4 · I Just Had to Do It · B1

Episode 4 of Better Speaking, B1 version. Björk thought about her first album for ten years; it wasn’t perfect, but she “just had to do it anyway”. The language is obligation in the past: had to / didn’t have to / Did you have to…?. The sister lesson for B2 is “Sound Like Someone”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — When you had to, Then and now. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — I had to, Find the reason, Fix the traps, Choose the right words.
  • Write — A hard week. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

Must in the past: I musted study. “Tive que” becomes I had that work. And the question without did: You had to wear a uniform?

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, matches and writes. “Only you see this” is yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. The bar on top shows the season: eight episodes, today’s one lit. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
