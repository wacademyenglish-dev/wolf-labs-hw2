The Factory Tour · B1 Unit 7 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 7 (The passive). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student is the guide at Cacau Lume, a small chocolate factory (invented, opened in 1952). The tour is a board game: on every square the student explains how something is done, in the passive. You are the curious visitor.

About 48 min: I · The Ladder (8) · II · The Tour (15) · III · Unpopular Opinions (7) · IV · Find the Mistakes (7) · V · Would You Rather? (7) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Climb the ladder. Six steps, from easy to hard.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

present passive · past passive · the passive when the person is not important · the passive with by · present perfect passive for news · have / get something done

THE LADDER

Six steps from easy to hard. A good answer: tap Go up. Not yet: Try again on the same step.

THE TOUR

A board game around the table. Tap Roll the die: the token moves and the square shows a task that mixes the grammar of the unit. The student does it out loud; tap Done and roll again until the end of the board.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

WOULD YOU RATHER?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

it opened (for was opened), is grow, is make, designed from (for by), has created (for has been created), I cut my hair (for I had my hair cut). B1: one correction per answer is enough.

UNPOPULAR OPINIONS (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the tour, three statements to agree or disagree with: made by hand or by machines, getting your hair cut by a friend, keeping ugly old buildings. The student taps a number from 1 to 5 and defends it with the passive; then you take the opposite number and argue back. Wait five seconds in silence before they answer, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Ladder and IV · Find the Mistakes and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Tour, III · Unpopular Opinions and V · Would You Rather?.
