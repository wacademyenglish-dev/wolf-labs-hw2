The Closing Ceremony · C1 Unit 10 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 10 (Closing Book C1). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: The closing ceremony of the course (invented). The board walks through every unit of Book C1 one last time. Then the student writes and gives the closing speech, and asks YOU anything.

About 53 min: I · The Ladder (8) · II · The Whole Book (16) · III · The Closing Speech (10) · IV · The Running Order (7) · V · Questions for Your Teacher (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Climb the ladder. Six steps: the six tools of a great speech.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

openings: the hook · signposting · tricolon and parallelism · antithesis · the story arc · the close: a call to action

THE LADDER

Six steps from easy to hard. A good answer: tap Go up. Not yet: Try again on the same step.

THE WHOLE BOOK

A board game around the table. Tap Roll the die: the token moves and the square shows a task that mixes the grammar of the unit. The student does it out loud; tap Done and roll again until the end of the board.

THE CLOSING SPEECH

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

QUESTIONS FOR YOUR TEACHER

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. This is the last review of Book C1, and of the whole course. After the stamp, congratulate the student: Book C1 is complete.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

sorry for my English (in a speech), it was my teacher who she made me, never I had felt, according to me, I couldn’t care more, in conclusion, to conclude. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE RUNNING ORDER (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

With the closing speech written, your student negotiates stage time with you as Ms. Hale, the ceremony organiser. Ask her four questions; the student answers in role with the Unit 10 tools (tricolon, “it’s not about…; it’s about…”, signposting, “all I ask is…”). Your secret limit: three minutes before the fireworks, if the speech has a strong hook and no slides. Wait five seconds in silence after each question, use the PUSH line (only you see it), recast quietly while they talk, then close the deal on screen and ask for one or two self-corrections.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
