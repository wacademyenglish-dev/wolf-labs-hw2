Open Mic Night · How to be funny in English

Based on the BBC Learning English video about not getting the joke. The screen is a small comedy club: a neon sign, one microphone, one spotlight. The grammar comes from the video itself: even if (it doesn’t matter if this is true) and whether… or (it doesn’t matter which one). No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — Warm-up, Knock knock, Your humor, Funny toolkit, Open mic. After each answer a follow-up appears in the middle, with starters the student can tap and a Help.
  • Listen — The video, in three steps. It stays locked until the student answers the two Before questions.
  • Focus — Grammar, Three traps, Practice.
  • Write — Write your joke. The student writes on their device; you see every letter; SAVE sends it to their My notes.

THE TRAP

Portuguese “mesmo que” becomes even that. “Seja… ou…” loses the or: whether you’re playful, be yourself. And after don’t the past sneaks in: I don’t understood.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, votes and writes. The boxes marked “Only you see this” are yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
