My Childcare Experience · Au Pair · Lesson 4 of 24

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: telling experiences with children in the past simple. The video shows how an agency screens au pairs: application, interview, at least 200 hours of childcare experience, references, background check, health certificate, training.

The story: A Cultural Care video for host families explains the screening. Then Camila’s story (invented) with mistakes, and the student tells a real story.

About 47 min: I · How Agencies Check You (9) · II · Memory (8) · III · Find the Mistakes (7) · IV · Your Best Story (12) · V · What Did She Do? (7) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “How many hours have you spent with children in your life? Let’s count: family, babysitting, school, church, volunteering.”

The program rule says au pairs need at least 200 hours of documented infant care experience to be placed with children under two. Help the student list real hours with dates and a contact person: never invent experience.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

took · made · read · taught · fed · sang · didn’t + base verb · One day, … Suddenly, … In the end, … · I was taking care of… · 200 hours of experience · a contact person for each experience

HOW AGENCIES CHECK YOU

Cultural Care Au Pair, “How are Au Pairs Screened and Trained?” (2 min, captions checked on 15/10/2026). It is made for host families: ask the student what it means for HER application.

MEMORY

A memory game. The student opens two cards; when they match, the student makes a sentence with the pair (example under Show answer).

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

YOUR BEST STORY

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Homework idea: a table with all childcare experience (where, when, hours, contact). It helps with the real application.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I take care (for the past), didn’t wanted, readed, maked. A2+: one correction per answer is enough.

WHAT DID SHE DO? (V) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the student’s own story, three short babysitting stories (fictional) stop at the problem: a lost shoe on a late morning, a broken plate, and “green food, no way!” at dinner. The student bets what the au pair did and says it in the past simple; then press Check to see the real ending. The bet is not right or wrong: ask what THEY would do. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it). Recast quietly while they talk (took, made, didn’t want); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
