This Time Next Year · The Moment Line

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: A new year, and everybody is making plans.

The grammar: The Rule: A moment in the future, and something already in progress.

Short class (about 43 min): I You and Next Year · III Earth in 2050 · V The Neon Skyline · VI The Rule · VII The Forecast Orbit · XI See You in 2050. Skip if you’re short on time: VIII, IX, X (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 63 min.

Open with: “Did you make any plans for this year? Tell me one.”

A new year, and everybody is making plans. The grammar: will be + -ing for something that will be in progress at a moment in the future (This time next year, I’ll be living in Lisbon. At 8 AM in 2050, robots will be cooking breakfast.), the negative won’t be + -ing, the polite question Will you be using …?, and where it stops: a decision made now is plain will, and state verbs (know, have, be) don’t take -ing. On screen, a moment line: a pin on the future moment and a moving striped bar crossing it, because the action is already happening there. And a neon skyline of eight towers that light up one by one.

THE VIDEO

Scene III: a five-minute video about what the Earth could look like in 2050, sealed until you press play, in four chapters that stop by themselves. The questions are open on purpose. The narrator mostly uses plain will and could; the student turns the ideas into will be + -ing. Recast once: So people will be driving electric cars. After the video (page III, step 3): ONE screen. The video stays on the left, with the neon skyline under it; on the right, six True or Lie lines from the video (tap the time to hear that moment again). Every right answer lights a tower. On a Lie the student says the true line, with will be + -ing when it fits. When all six are done, the student’s future self asks one last question (“What will you be doing right now?”): the student taps an answer and says the sentence out loud.

THE SKYLINE

Scene V, before the rule: the student picks a tower and makes a forecast. Tap exactly what you heard; a wrong version explains the missing piece. Most students produce will working (no be) or will be work (no -ing). A good answer in plain will is not wrong: accept it, then recast.

THE TRAPS

will working · will be work · in this time next year · I will have 55 years · I’ll be helping you for a decision made right now · I’ll be knowing · will be not · Will be you …? · I’ll be probably.

THE ORBIT AND THE CALL

Scene VII: read the moment and the first words in a lively voice, and stop; the student finishes at once, with their own details. Moments 2, 4 and 6 are where it stops: age with be, know without -ing, and a decision now. Scene VIII: you are the student’s future self, calm and friendly. In the last exchange, the student forms the question.

The words (II): emissions, blackouts, droughts, melted and displaced, before the video, in match the columns and mystery meaning. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (IV, extra): every line of the video; tap one to hear it again, and the lines with yellow words open the explanation.

THE ARENA (IX) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

The video says many people will be moving to the cities by 2050. Your student picks a side, smart city or small town, and defends it for one minute with will be + -ing; then they swap and defend the other side. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence before they start, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I You and Next Year, V The Neon Skyline, VII The Forecast Orbit, VIII A Call from 2050, IX The Arena and X Your 2050.
  • Listen: II The Words, III Earth in 2050 and IV Line by Line.
  • Focus: VI The Rule.
  • Write: XI See You in 2050.
