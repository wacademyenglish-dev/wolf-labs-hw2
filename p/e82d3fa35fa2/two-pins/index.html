Two Pins · The Old City and the City Now

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: Almost everybody has two cities: the one they are from and the one they live in.

The grammar: Left Pin, Right Pin: a postcard from Tim, then the rule. One word decides which pin you mean.

Short class (about 52 min): I Your Two Pins · II Four People · III Six Places · IV Left Pin, Right Pin · V Which Question? · VI Home and People · VIII Your Next Home · X Your Line, Again. Skip if you’re short on time: VII The Landing Card and IX Your People (marked EXTRA on your screen). On the speaking pages the student speaks first and taps Check on their own screen. Full lesson: about 66 min.

Open with: “Where are you from?”

Almost everybody has two cities: the one they are from and the one they live in. On a dark cork board, the red pin is the old city, the amber pin is the city now, and the string between them is the sentence. Four real people (Tim, Sian, Buli and Georgie) show the shape: none of them lives where they are from. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

am / is / are + from (the left pin) · live + in (the right pin) · was born in (a third pin, always past). Where are you from? × Where do you live? Home: a house / an apartment, with my family / on my own. The mistakes: I live from London, I am in Oxford meaning origin, I live at London, I born in, with my own, Where you live?

THE AUDIO

The speakers are British: they say flat and flatmates. The screen teaches the American words (apartment, roommates) and names flat as British. Every recording has pause and 0.75×; the words appear only after listening. Checked against the audio: each person also says their home and who they live with.

THE PAGES

I Your Two Pins: three questions (left pin, right pin, the string). The student reads, says an answer about themselves, then taps Check: an example appears and the pins go up on the board. Nobody is corrected here. Ask the follow-up under the example.

II Four People: play each of the four people (Tim, Sian, Buli, Georgie), then match every name to its two pins (Oxford → London…). Each right match fills a small board. When all four are done, the transcripts open and there is one question about the board.

III Six Places: press play on the fast recording. Tap a city, then its box: the old city (I’m from) or the city now (I live in). A city in the wrong box goes back with one tap.

IV Left Pin, Right Pin: a postcard from Tim on a dark paper. The student reads it and taps every from / live in (six; every word can be tapped). Then they finish the rule on the right: from or in, are you from, do you live, lives. The rule stays in the corner of V and IX.

V Which Question?: six answers (I’m from Mexico · Near the station…). The student says the question out loud, then Check shows it. The last one has two questions (from and live).

VI Home and People: the five short lines first (say each one), then play each person and tap their home and who they live with. The note about flat / apartment stays.

VII The Landing Card: you are the officer on the card: the student reads each question and answers in a full sentence, then taps Check. Each Check fills a line; the sixth stamps WELCOME. Then the student asks you the six questions.

VIII Your Next Home: a light ranking of three homes (see YOUR NEXT HOME below).

IX Your People: five questions to build (where · your mother · from…). The student says each one, taps Check, then asks you and you answer.

YOUR NEXT HOME (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Three homes for the student’s next pin: a house with the family, an apartment on their own, an apartment with roommates. They tap them in order and say it with live in / with; then they open your order and you agree on one together. There is no right order. Say your order first, then wait five seconds in silence while they choose, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) for one more small question. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment (one of them brings back I was born in). Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Your Two Pins, VII The Landing Card (you are the officer), VIII Your Next Home (a light ranking), IX Your People (VII and IX are no longer extra).
  • Listen: II Four People, III Six Places (ears only), VI Home and People.
  • Focus: IV Left Pin, Right Pin (the postcard), V Which Question?.
  • Write: X, their own pins, with the red wavy line for live from / live at, I born in, I am born, born from, with my own, Where do you from?, she live. It goes to their My notes.
