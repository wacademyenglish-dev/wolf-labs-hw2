The Keynote · B2 Unit 10 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 10 (Closing Book B2). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: The last review of Book B2. Your student is the closing speaker at a conference on the future of work. The board game is the questions from the floor: on each square you are someone in the audience (the CEO, the skeptic, the joker). Then the student writes the closing words.

About 43 min: I · The Die (8) · II · Questions from the Floor (15) · III · Your Closing Words (7) · IV · Unpopular Opinions (7) · V · I Can (3) · VI · The Next Book (3).

Open with: “Roll the die. The number chooses the challenge.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

hedge, concede, disagree · seem to, appear to, tend to · it could be argued, arguably, to some extent · actually, anyway, mind you, by the way · so do I, neither have I, I hope so, I don’t think so · moving on, what this means is, to sum up

THE DIE

Tap Roll the die: the number chooses one of the six topics (no repeats until all six are done). The student does the challenge from memory.

QUESTIONS FROM THE FLOOR

A board game around the table. Tap Roll the die: the token moves and the square shows a task that mixes the grammar of the unit. The student does it out loud; tap Done and roll again until the end of the board.

YOUR CLOSING WORDS

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. This is the last review of Book B2. After I Can there is one more short scene, VI The Next Book, with what Book C1 brings.

THE NEXT BOOK

The last scene of the book: three cards with what the next book brings. Read them together and ask which one the student wants first.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

actually (meaning currently), people tend to working, I don’t think it (for I don’t think so), so I do (for so do I), neither I have, it could argue that. B2: one correction per answer is enough.

UNPOPULAR OPINIONS (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

The conference goes on: a guest says something strong, and your student taps a number from 1 (totally disagree) to 5 (totally agree), then defends it like a speaker, with hedging, concessions and signposts. There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence, then take the opposite number and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
