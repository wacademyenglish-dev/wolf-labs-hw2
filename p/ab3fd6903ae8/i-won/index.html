The Challenge · Episode 10 · I Won!

The final episode. Who won? Paula, Bob and Fran tell their five days. The language is the past: regular verbs with -ed (worked, wanted, liked) and two that change (go → went, win → won). (Was and were are already in “I Was Not Alone”, so today the target is the past of verbs.) No points, no timer.
