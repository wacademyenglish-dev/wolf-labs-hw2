Fifty to Eighty · Roots and Crown

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: A study followed 724 men for 75 years, from 1938: university students and boys from poor city neighborhoods.

The grammar: Roots and Crown: The root is a closed past. The crown is today. The result decides its own tense.

Short class (about 49 min): I An Old Decision · II Roots and Crown · III The Other Direction · IV Two Men, 1938 · VII The Talk · X Explain It · XII Your Decision, Again. Skip if you’re short on time: V, IX, XI (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 68 min.

Open with: “When was it, and what did you decide?”

A study followed 724 men for 75 years, from 1938: university students and boys from poor city neighborhoods. What predicted health at eighty was not cholesterol or money at fifty; it was how satisfied they were in their relationships. Reporting that needs a mixed conditional: a closed past and a present that is still running. If they hadn’t kept those Sundays, they wouldn’t be healthy now. The other direction: If I were braver, I would have said yes. Students say If I had studied, I would have been rich now. On screen, an oak: the root under the ground line is the closed past, the crown is today, and every right sentence grows a branch with leaves. Two clocks show which half is THEN and which is NOW. II: six sentences, right or wrong; if wrong, the student says the fix, and each right answer grows a branch. III: sort six sentences into “the result is still true today” and “the result is finished”. XI: five sentences with one wrong part each; find it and say the fix.

PRIVACY

Scene I asks about decisions, not private life. Let the student choose how much to share. Write the third answer exactly; it comes back in scene X.

THE RECORDINGS

Four recordings (scene IV), each with pause and 0.75×: two men, a researcher and a wife. The two men are composites, not real people. The researcher’s recording names the university; the screen doesn’t. After listening, the student taps what the voice says (the options mean different things) and then says the sentence.

THE VIDEO

Scene VII: the director of the study, two and a half minutes (6:03 to 8:35), sealed until the student taps a guess (two predictions, no wrong answer yet). While watching: five true-or-lie lines, each with a ▶ at its minute; if it is a lie, the student says the truth. After: clues one by one until “I know it!” shows the finding as a mixed conditional.

TOO FAR

Scene IX: perfect grammar can hide a bad claim. A finding about 724 men isn’t a promise about the student. In scene X you are the friend: the student opens three question cards and answers in one minute (timer on screen); push once after each answer, “Really? How do you know?”

The words (VI): invest, survey, famous, memory and persistence, before the video, in mystery meaning and the word in your life. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (VIII, extra): every line of the video; tap one to hear it again, and the lines with yellow words open the explanation.

AT EIGHTY, YOUR ORDER (XI) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After explaining the study, your student puts six things in order: what they want to have at eighty. There is no right order: wait five seconds in silence while they think, ask them to explain the top one and the last one with a mixed conditional, then show your own order and agree on one top three together. Use the PUSH line (only you see it) to test their list against their real week. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I An Old Decision, V At Fifty, At Eighty, X Explain It and XI At Eighty, Your Order.
  • Listen: IV Two Men, 1938, VI The Words, VII The Talk and VIII Line by Line.
  • Focus: II Roots and Crown, III The Other Direction and IX Too Far.
