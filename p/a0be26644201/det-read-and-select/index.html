Read and Select · DET Prep · Lesson 2 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the Read and Select question: a grid of words, a clock, and the student taps only the real English words. Fake words are built to look real: a real ending in the wrong word, a real prefix in the wrong place, a missing letter, y that should become i.

The story: No story: the student is the candidate. The lesson moves from noticing how fakes are made to three timed rounds and a fast word-family drill.

About 39 min: I · How Fake Words Are Made (10) · II · Three Rounds (12) · III · Unpopular Opinions (7) · IV · Speed Round (6) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “Is ‘happyness’ a word?” Most students say yes. That is exactly the trap of today.

This question rewards vocabulary depth AND caution. In the test, tapping a fake word costs more than missing a real one, so teach the habit: when in doubt, leave it.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

y → i (happiness, beautiful) · un- / dis- / im- · -ment / -ness / -ful / -tion · missing letters · word families · when in doubt, leave it

HOW FAKE WORDS ARE MADE

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

THREE ROUNDS

Read and Select: the words stay blurred until you tap Start the clock. The student taps only the REAL English words. At the end: green = found, dashed amber = missed, red = a fake word tapped (that costs points in the test).

SPEED ROUND

A 60-second speed round. Tap Start the clock, then Right or Pass for each item; the answer is on your screen. At the end of class, play again and beat the score.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. If the student taps fakes, slow down the next round: accuracy first, speed second.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

happyness, beautyful, unpossible, decidement, knowlege, childs. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

UNPOPULAR OPINIONS (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After three rounds of tapping, the student stops and gives opinions about words: spelling, word lists, guessing and English spelling itself. For each statement, the student taps a number from 1 to 5 and defends it; you take the opposite number and argue back. Wait five seconds in silence before they start. Use the PUSH line when the answer is too safe. Recast quietly while they talk, and push the word families of the lesson (impossible, decision, carefully, useless); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
