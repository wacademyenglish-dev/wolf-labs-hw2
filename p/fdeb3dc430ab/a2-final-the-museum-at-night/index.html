The Museum at Night · A2 Unit 10 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 10 (Closing Book A2). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: The last review of Book A2. Your student is the night guard at a small museum, and the round happens on a board game. Something strange is happening… it’s the museum cat.

About 51 min: I · Memory (8) · II · The Night Round (15) · III · The Crown Jury (7) · IV · Find the Mistakes (7) · V · The Hot Seat (8) · VI · I Can (3) · VII · The Next Book (3).

Open with: “Open two cards. Find the pair, then make the sentence.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

phrasal verbs (turn it on, look for it) · who / that / which / where · the passive in the present · must / might / can’t be

MEMORY

A memory game. The student opens two cards; when they match, the student makes a sentence with the pair (example under Show answer).

THE NIGHT ROUND

A board game around the table. Tap Roll the die: the token moves and the square shows a task that mixes the grammar of the unit. The student does it out loud; tap Done and roll again until the end of the board.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. This is the last review of Book A2. After I Can there is one more short scene, VI The Next Book, with what Book B1 brings.

THE NEXT BOOK

The last scene of the book: three cards with what the next book brings. Read them together and ask which one the student wants first.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

turn on it, look it for, the room where … there, it must not be (for can’t be), it made of gold, the cat what lives here. A2: one correction per answer is enough.

THE CROWN JURY (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the night round, the morning: the crown is not in its glass box, it’s in the café (nothing was stolen, it only moved). Your student reads the evidence on Rosa, Mr. Hale and Max the cat and gives a verdict with must, might or can’t be. There is no single right answer: let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast quietly while they talk (it was found, the man who…); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. The last word is one minute for the final verdict, then you defend that person. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.

  • Focus: IV · Find the Mistakes and VI · I Can.
