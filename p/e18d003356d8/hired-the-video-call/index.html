The Video Call · WOLF HIRED · Lesson 9 of 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: online interviews: preparing the space (camera at eye level, light in front, look at the camera), solving technical problems (You’re on mute. Sorry about that. Can you hear me now? You’re breaking up. I lost you for a second. Can you see my screen?) and opening and closing the call.

The story: A former CEO’s video interview tips, five recorded interviewer lines about technical problems, then a fictional call with five mistakes.

About 34 min: I · Set It Up Right (6) · II · Five Things That Go Wrong (12) · III · Fix the Call (12) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “Have you ever had a technical problem in an important video call? What happened?”

Use your real call: at some point, mute the student on purpose (or pretend the video froze) and see if they use the phrases. Also check their real camera, light and background together for one minute.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Can you hear me okay? · Sorry about that. Can you hear me now? · You’re breaking up a little. · I lost you for a second. · Can you see my screen? · Excuse me for one second.

SET IT UP RIGHT

The Companies Expert, video interview tips (0:00–2:00).

FIVE THINGS THAT GO WRONG

Listen or read, then choose one option. The colors show right and wrong, and one line says why. Ask the student WHY before you move on.

FIX THE CALL

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to test their setup before the next class: camera height, light, sound, background.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Are you listening me? (Can you hear me?), My internet fell (My internet dropped / My connection went down), Can you see my screen? (correct!), Sorry, sorry, sorry (one short apology is enough). B1+: one correction per answer is enough.
