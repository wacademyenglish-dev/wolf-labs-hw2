Who Signs the Contract? · Selling Services · Lesson 20

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 20 (has / have · who, what, where questions · job titles). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: The “Our team” page of Cobalt Home Goods (fictional): who signs, who has the budget, who uses SAP, who compares prices. The student separates people who DECIDE from people who INFLUENCE, finds the Brazilian traps, guesses jobs from six short audios, and then meets the Mystery Client: you are Sam Porter, of Fenwick Packaging (fictional), who loves the proposal… but is only the controller. He closes with the client’s team in sentences.

About 60 min: I · Your Company (5) · II · “Our Team” (7) · III · Decides or Influences? (6) · IV · “Who Signs?”, Not “Who Does Sign?” (9) · V · Who Is It? (6) · VI · The Mystery Client (10) · VII · The Account Board (6) · VIII · The Client’s Team (8) · IX · I Can (3).

Open with: “Who signs the contracts at your company? Who is your boss? Does he or she have a big team?”

Finding the decision maker is a core B2B skill: a friendly contact is not always the buyer. Grammar: has / have; questions with who as subject (Who signs? Who has the budget?, no do/does), and Where / What + does + subject + verb. Job titles: CEO, CFO, controller, procurement, IT manager, board. Brazilian traps: “Who does sign?”, “she have”, “Where she works?”, “Does she has…?”. In VI you are Sam: very enthusiastic, talk like the boss, never say your job unless he asks “What do you do?”.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Who signs the contract? Who has the budget? · She has … · They have … · What does he do? Where does she work? · Who else is in the decision?

YOUR COMPANY

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

“OUR TEAM”

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

DECIDES OR INFLUENCES?

One situation at a time. The student says which side it goes on and WHY, then taps. The color confirms and one line explains. The two columns build a board the student can photograph at the end.

“WHO SIGNS?”, NOT “WHO DOES SIGN?”

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

WHO IS IT?

Listen or read, then choose one option. The colors show right and wrong, and one line says why. Ask the student WHY before you move on.

THE MYSTERY CLIENT

Arena do Cliente: YOU are the client. Read the client card on your screen (only you see the answers). The student asks; you answer ONLY what was asked, short and a little busy. Tap each fact when the student finds it. At the end, Show answer has model questions. (Soon the AI can play the client; the card stays the same.)

THE ACCOUNT BOARD

Project P-02: the account board. The student fills the fields (real client with no secret data, or invented) and then presents the account in 5–6 sentences.

THE CLIENT’S TEAM

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask him to map the team of one real client (who decides, who influences) before the next class.

THE TECH WORDS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno vende serviços B2B. Você não precisa ser especialista; precisa saber do que ele está falando. Não ensine estes termos: deixe que ele explique o trabalho dele (é conversa real).

  • BPO (Business Process Outsourcing): Terceirização de processos: a empresa contratada EXECUTA uma rotina do cliente (folha de pagamento, contabilidade, fiscal, contas a pagar). O cliente para de fazer aquilo dentro de casa.
  • BTO (Business Transformation Outsourcing): Terceirização com transformação: além de executar, a contratada MUDA o processo (tecnologia, automação, integração de sistemas). Vende-se como “melhorar”, não só “fazer por você”.
  • ERP: Sistema de gestão que integra finanças, compras, estoque, RH etc. Exemplos (marcas): SAP, Oracle, TOTVS. Um BPO/BTO não vende o ERP: trabalha COM ele ou o integra.
  • Decision maker: Quem decide a compra (assina ou aprova o orçamento). Diferente de “influencer”, que opina mas não decide.
  • Procurement: Compras / suprimentos: área que cota fornecedores, compara preços e negocia. Influencia muito, raramente assina sozinha.
  • Controller: Controller: cuida da controladoria/contabilidade e do fechamento mensal; costuma reportar ao CFO.
  • Board: Conselho de administração ou diretoria colegiada: aprova projetos grandes.
  • Sign off · approve: Dar o aval / aprovar formalmente (“The CFO signs off on the budget”).
  • Account map: Mapa da conta: quem é quem no cliente (decide, influencia, usa). Ferramenta comum em vendas B2B.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

Who does sign the contract? (Who signs…?), She have the budget (She has), Where she works? (Where does she work?), Does she has…? (Does she have…?). A1+: one correction per answer is enough.
