The Café Where We Met · A Little Guide to Portland

An easy reader in six short pages. Rosa and Ben met in Portland ten years ago, when Ben spilled his coffee on Rosa’s book. Now Ben’s sister is moving to the city, and they write her a little guidebook: the places where their story happened. Rosa tells everybody they met at the Little Fern. The guide says something else: the Blue Door is the café where they met; the Little Fern is the café which they loved.

THE TEST

Say the second half alone. We met there → where. We loved it → which (or that). No extra there or it at the end. Before a new person, which / that can go, and the little word stays at the end: the song we danced to, the bench we sat on.

THE PORTUGUESE TRAP

One word covers which, that and who, and the word for where spreads to any place, so students say the café where we loved. Spoken Portuguese repeats the place or the thing at the end (where we met there, which we crossed it), and puts the preposition before, so it disappears in English (the song we danced).

BEN’S CLUES

Scene IV is the pressure: you read the two facts and the first words; the student, as Ben, says the clue at once. Clue 7 is the turn: they did NOT meet there.

THE JURY (VI) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After Lucy’s call, your student is the jury for three small cases from Rosa and Ben’s story: the coffee on the book, Rosa’s little lie, and a sign for the Little Fern. They read the evidence and give a verdict. There is no right answer: let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. The last case is the rule itself: the café where they met × the café which they loved. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I, II The Guidebook (read the pages aloud), IV Ben’s Five Clues, V Lucy Calls, VI The Jury, VII Your City Guide.
  • Focus: III The Rule. A sister lesson to The Book You Keep, in another format.
  • Write: VIII, three pages of their own guide. It goes to their My notes.
