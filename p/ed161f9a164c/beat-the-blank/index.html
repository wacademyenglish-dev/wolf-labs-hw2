Beat the Blank · The Detour

Your student stops when a word doesn’t come, or switches language. This hour trains the reflex fluent speakers use all the time: go around the word. There are only four shapes: It’s a kind of…, It’s the thing you use for …-ing, It’s when you…, It’s like…, but… Inside each one is a small joint: that, who, where, whose, or nothing. The screen is a road with the bridge closed: the four shapes are the detour signs.

NOBODY NAMES THE FIVE

Scene I shows four objects and one feeling your student probably can’t name. Nobody names them until scene XI: not the screen, not you. Count the blanks with I GOT STUCK.

DON’T SUPPLY THE WORD

When your student is stuck, ask one question: What do you DO with it?

STRICT ON THE BAN, GENEROUS ON GRAMMAR

Scene V: a forbidden word costs a point; broken grammar costs nothing today. Tap + DETOUR each time your student uses a shape.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

How do you say…?, long silence, the thing for open, a person who his job…, a place which you…

THE JURY (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the forbidden cards, your student judges three people who lost a word in real life: Lucas in an interview, Mia with a translator app, Tom at a dinner party. There is no right verdict. Let them read the evidence and choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it personal. Push them to show the better detour (“He could say: it’s when you…”). Recast quietly while they talk (the thing you use for opening, a person who…); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I The First Blank, V The Forbidden Cards, VI Which One Am I?, VIII The Jury, IX The Side Door, X No Take-Backs.
  • Listen: II What You Do Now, IV Buying Time, VII The Joint (both versions).
  • Focus: III The Four Detours, VII The Joint.
  • Write: XI, three things from their home with a detour each. It goes to their My notes.
