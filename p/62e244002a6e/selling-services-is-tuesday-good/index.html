Is Tuesday at 10 Good for You? · Selling Services · Lesson 9

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 9 (on · at · in (time) · a.m. / p.m.). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Leo and Karen Wells (Lumen Logistics, fictional) find a time for a demo on Teams. Then Rachel Kim (Atlas Motors) calls to move a meeting, twice. The student fixes a message with five mistakes and writes his own message to move a real meeting.

About 56 min: I · Your Week (6) · II · A Chat about a Demo (7) · III · The Meeting Moves (8) · IV · On Monday, at 10, in the Morning (10) · V · Fix the Message (8) · VI · You Choose (5) · VII · Your Meeting Message (9) · VIII · I Can (3).

Open with: “What time do you start work? What is your busy day?”

Portuguese uses “na segunda / de manhã / às 10h / em março”, so Brazilians say “in Monday”, “at the morning”, “on 10 a.m.” Teach three boxes: ON + day/date, AT + clock time, IN + part of the day / month / year (exception: at night). Americans use the 12-hour clock: “15h” in an e-mail confuses them; write 3 p.m. Also practice saying dates: March 12 = “March twelfth”.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

on Monday · on March 12 · at 10 a.m. · at 3 p.m. · in the morning · in March · Is … at … good for you? · Sorry, I can’t …

YOUR WEEK

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

A CHAT ABOUT A DEMO

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

THE MEETING MOVES

A short audio dialogue (American voices). BEFORE: one question. LISTEN: play it once without stopping (0.75× is fine for A1), then the questions; Show answer after the student tries. The transcript opens only after the audio ends: read it together and point to the highlighted words.

ON MONDAY, AT 10, IN THE MORNING

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

FIX THE MESSAGE

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

YOU CHOOSE

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

YOUR MEETING MESSAGE

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Next real meeting: write the invite or the reschedule message in English.

THE TECH WORDS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno vende serviços B2B. Você não precisa ser especialista; precisa saber do que ele está falando. Não ensine estes termos: deixe que ele explique o trabalho dele (é conversa real).

  • BPO (Business Process Outsourcing): Terceirização de processos: a empresa contratada EXECUTA uma rotina do cliente (folha de pagamento, contabilidade, fiscal, contas a pagar). O cliente para de fazer aquilo dentro de casa.
  • BTO (Business Transformation Outsourcing): Terceirização com transformação: além de executar, a contratada MUDA o processo (tecnologia, automação, integração de sistemas). Vende-se como “melhorar”, não só “fazer por você”.
  • ERP: Sistema de gestão que integra finanças, compras, estoque, RH etc. Exemplos (marcas): SAP, Oracle, TOTVS. Um BPO/BTO não vende o ERP: trabalha COM ele ou o integra.
  • Demo: Demonstração (do serviço ou da plataforma/integração) para o cliente. Normalmente a 2ª ou 3ª reunião.
  • Invite (calendar invite): Convite de agenda (Outlook, Google Calendar, Teams). “I can send the invite” = eu mando o convite.
  • Reschedule · move a meeting: Remarcar reunião. Na aula usamos “move” e “Can we do Monday?”, mais simples para A1.
  • a.m. / p.m.: Relógio de 12 horas dos EUA: a.m. = da meia-noite ao meio-dia; p.m. = do meio-dia à meia-noite. 15h = 3 p.m. Escrever “15h” para americano confunde.
  • Video call · client visit: Chamada de vídeo · visita presencial ao cliente.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

in Monday (on Monday), at the morning (in the morning), on 10 a.m. (at 10 a.m.), at 15h (at 3 p.m.). A1+: one correction per answer is enough.
