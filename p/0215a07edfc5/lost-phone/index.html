The Lost Phone · The Lock Screen

Someone left a phone on the 8:15 bus. No name, just nine apps and 451 photos. The phone is drawn on screen with a dark lock screen; next to it, the owner’s profile card fills in with every sentence the student says. In the end the owner walks in and takes one line away: she walks seven dogs every day because it’s her job, and she doesn’t like it. Doing is not liking. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

like / love / hate + -ing (I like cooking; I like to cook is also correct, but today we train the -ing shape). The answer says what: Yes, I like it / them, never Yes, I like. She: doesn’t like, one S only. The mistakes: I like watch, I like of…, Yes, I like., she don’t like, doesn’t likes.

WOULD YOU RATHER (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Before the owner comes in, three light “would you rather” choices, two options each: lose your phone or your keys, a Saturday job walking dogs or washing cars, photos of people or places. There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) for a yes/no follow-up. The starters keep love / like / hate + -ing and don’t like in their mouth. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: II Nine Apps, VI Three Cards, IX The Owner (pause and 0.75× on every recording; transcripts after listening, checked against the audio).
  • Out loud: I Your Phone First, V The Photo Roll, VIII Would You Rather (three choices), X Is It Really Yours? (you are a stranger, then the owner).
  • Focus: III After the Verb, IV Yes, I Like It, VII What She Doesn’t Like.
  • Write: XI, their own profile card, with the red wavy line for like watch, like of, Yes, I like., she don’t like, doesn’t likes and spellings like runing. It goes to their My notes.

THE OWNER

Scene IX: let the student read the card first, then play the owner. The wrong line is She loves dogs. Ask: does she walk dogs? Does she like it?
