Have You Ever Worked with SAP? · Selling Services · Lesson 27

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 27 (Have you ever…? · experience). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: The lesson opens with six doors of “Have you ever…?” about the student’s own life and work. Then Nora Bell, controller of Oakridge Paper (fictional), asks Leo about Bluepeak’s experience, and Leo answers honestly, even about a lost client. The student writes three experience lines and uses them in the Objection Gym against Victor Hale, the doubtful CFO (you).

About 59 min: I · Six Doors (9) · II · “Have You Worked with a Company Like Us?” (8) · III · Have You Ever…? (8) · IV · Yes, We Have or Yes, We Did? (6) · V · Your 3 Experience Lines (7) · VI · The Tough Client (12) · VII · You Choose (6) · VIII · I Can (3).

Open with: Door 1 or any door: “Have you ever traveled for work? Have you ever presented in English?”

Present perfect ONLY for experience, light: Have you ever…? / Yes, we have / No, I haven’t / We’ve never… Brazilians translate “Você já trabalhou…?” as “You already worked…?”, forget the -ed (“Have you ever work”), answer “Yes, I do” and use have + -ed with a year (“We have worked with them in 2024”). Rule of thumb for A1+: a time (in 2024, last year) → simple past. In I, don’t correct during the answer: listen for the trap on your screen and fix it after. In VI you are Victor: polite but doubtful; after each answer, say “Hmm, OK…” and give the next doubt only when he asks.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Have you ever worked with…? · Yes, we have. / No, I haven’t. · We’ve never had a late payroll. · In 2025, we worked with … (a time → past)

SIX DOORS

Six doors, one per topic of the unit. The student picks any door and does the task from memory. Tap Got it or Review; amber doors come back as a short list “to review”.

“HAVE YOU WORKED WITH A COMPANY LIKE US?”

A short audio dialogue (American voices). BEFORE: one question. LISTEN: play it once without stopping (0.75× is fine for A1), then the questions; Show answer after the student tries. The transcript opens only after the audio ends: read it together and point to the highlighted words.

HAVE YOU EVER…?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

YES, WE HAVE OR YES, WE DID?

Listen or read, then choose one option. The colors show right and wrong, and one line says why. Ask the student WHY before you move on.

YOUR 3 EXPERIENCE LINES

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE TOUGH CLIENT

Arena do Cliente: YOU are the client. Read the client card on your screen (only you see the answers). The student asks; you answer ONLY what was asked, short and a little busy. Tap each fact when the student finds it. At the end, Show answer has model questions. (Soon the AI can play the client; the card stays the same.)

YOU CHOOSE

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask for his real three experience lines in his company’s words and save them for the final project (lesson 30).

THE TECH WORDS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno vende serviços B2B. Você não precisa ser especialista; precisa saber do que ele está falando. Não ensine estes termos: deixe que ele explique o trabalho dele (é conversa real).

  • BPO (Business Process Outsourcing): Terceirização de processos: a empresa contratada EXECUTA uma rotina do cliente (folha de pagamento, contabilidade, fiscal, contas a pagar). O cliente para de fazer aquilo dentro de casa.
  • BTO (Business Transformation Outsourcing): Terceirização com transformação: além de executar, a contratada MUDA o processo (tecnologia, automação, integração de sistemas). Vende-se como “melhorar”, não só “fazer por você”.
  • ERP: Sistema de gestão que integra finanças, compras, estoque, RH etc. Exemplos (marcas): SAP, Oracle, TOTVS. Um BPO/BTO não vende o ERP: trabalha COM ele ou o integra.
  • Track record: Histórico comprovado da empresa (clientes, projetos, resultados). Cliente pergunta “Have you ever…?” para checar isso.
  • Reference (client reference): Cliente atual que aceita conversar com um possível cliente e contar a experiência. Muito pedido por CFOs antes de assinar.
  • Objection: Objeção: dúvida ou resistência do cliente (“Have you ever worked with a company like us?”, “It’s too expensive”).
  • Controller: Controller: responsável pela contabilidade e pelos controles financeiros; costuma responder ao CFO.
  • Late go-live: Go-live atrasado: quando o novo serviço/sistema entra no ar depois da data combinada; é o maior medo do cliente.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

You already worked with SAP? (Have you ever worked with SAP?), Have you ever work…? (Have you ever worked…?), Have you…? Yes, I do. (Yes, I have.), We have worked with them in 2024 (We worked with them in 2024). A1+: one correction per answer is enough.
