It Can’t Be the Butler · The Lanterns

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: Tonight is riddle night.

The grammar: How Sure Are You?: The lantern says how sure you are.

Short class (about 43 min): I You and Mysteries · III The Bridge Riddle · V The Pause · VIII How Sure Are You? · IX The Ashford House Case · XIII Case Closed. Skip if you’re short on time: VI, VII, X, XI, XII (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 77 min.

Open with: “Do you like riddles, puzzles or escape rooms? Which ones?”

Tonight is riddle night. Your student watches a famous riddle: four people, one old rope bridge, one lantern, 17 minutes. The video is sealed and stops before the answer; your student solves it on the drawn bridge, thinking out loud with must be / might be / can’t be. Then six small mysteries, the rule, and a case at an old house where everybody says it’s the butler. It isn’t. Every lantern shows certainty: full flame = must be, half flame = might / may / could be, out = can’t be.

THE VIDEO AND THE PAUSE

Scene II plays three chapters; each stops by itself. At 1:58 the narrator says “Pause the video now…”. Scene III is the pause: plan every trip out loud before you tap. Scene IV plays the answer. After the answer chapter, the last step is ONE screen: the video stays on the left, with the bridge under it; on the right, six True or Lie lines about how the four people cross (tap a time to hear that moment again). Every right answer moves the team across the bridge and the clock runs. On a Lie the student says the true line. When all six are done, the narrator asks one last question (“Who is the real villain?”): the student taps an answer and says the sentence out loud, with must be, might be or can’t be.

SIX MYSTERIES, BEFORE THE RULE

Scene VII: let your student guess; tap what you heard. Listen for it can be (a guess about now: might / could) and it should be (evidence: must).

THE CASE

Scene IX: read each clue with drama. Your student deduces at once with the modal on the card; each suspect’s lantern changes. After a mistake, they say it again.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

It can be the butler (for a guess about now), he should be sick, must to be, might is, it doesn’t can be, Maybe is, must be walk.

The words (II): regrets, bridge, lantern, escape and slowest, before the video, in hear it in the video and mystery meaning. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (IV, extra): every line of the video; tap one to hear it again, and the lines with yellow words open the explanation.

THE BEST DETECTIVE (XII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After Riley is locked out, your student ranks five ordinary people from the best detective to the worst, and explains the top one and the last one with must, might and can’t. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it). Then tap “Show my teacher’s order” and agree on one top 3 together. Recast quietly while they talk (“it can be the waiter” → “it might be”); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I You and Mysteries, V The Pause, VII Four Small Mysteries, IX The Ashford House Case, X Your Own Mysteries, XI Locked Out and XII The Best Detective.
  • Listen: II The Words, III The Bridge Riddle, IV Line by Line and VI The Answer.
  • Focus: VIII How Sure Are You?
  • Write: XIII Case Closed.
