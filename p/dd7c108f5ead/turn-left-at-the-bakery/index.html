Turn Left at the Bakery · The Map of Gull Harbor

Kenji arrives in Gull Harbor with the map his grandmother drew in 1974: a red line from the bus stop to an X. Her note says the box is across the street from the lighthouse. Yesterday he dug at the lighthouse door and found nothing. The student is the local who helps him: first in the round town square, then step by step along the red line, where a pin walks every time the step is right. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

Directions start with the verb: Go straight. Turn left at the bakery. Go past the bank. Cross the street. Take the first right. Places: on your left, next to, between A and B, opposite. Questions: Excuse me, where is…? How do I get to…?

THE TRAP

Many learners use one expression for two places: at the door of a building, and across the street from it. In English, in front of is at its door, on the same side; across the street is opposite. That is Kenji’s mistake, and the whole story. Also expect: turn right in the church, go right for straight, turn to right, traverse, pass for, get the first right, in your left, next of / near of, in the half of the square.

OK OR NOT OK? (VI) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

In the middle of the lesson, Kenji makes two choices in the street: he crosses on a red light, then he digs in the square. Answer first, then let them choose green or red; wait five seconds in silence and let them tell Kenji what to do, verb first: Don’t cross. Go to …. The PUSH line (only you see it) is a quick yes or no. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one light personal question. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Your Street, II The Square (Kenji stands at the bus stop looking at the fountain: his left and right), IV The Red Line (you are Kenji; the arrow on the pin is where he looks), V Mrs. Brennan (you are the baker), VI OK or Not OK? (Kenji’s choices), VII Your Town, VIII Paper or Phone?
  • Focus: III The Rule (four traps, the student fixes each one out loud before tapping).
  • Write: IX, the way from their home to a place they love, with the red wavy line for go to straight, turn in, turn to right, in your left, next of, opposite of, traverse, How I go to…? It goes to their My notes.

No audio in this lesson: your voice is Kenji’s and Mrs. Brennan’s.
