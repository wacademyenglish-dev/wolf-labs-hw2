The Copy Editor · C1 Unit 1 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 1 (Precision: the right word). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student is the copy editor at a magazine (invented). You are three writers with weak drafts: a travel writer, a business writer and a food writer. Then the student writes an editor’s note.

About 49 min: I · Memory (8) · II · The Writers (15) · III · The Editorial Board (7) · IV · Find the Mistakes (7) · V · The Editor’s Note (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Open two cards. Find the pair, then make the sentence.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

adverb + adjective (eerily quiet) · adjective + noun collocations · formal verb + noun · idioms in context · business idioms · vague language: kind of, and stuff

MEMORY

A memory game. The student opens two cards; when they match, the student makes a sentence with the pair (example under Show answer).

THE WRITERS

You are each writer. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then the student gives editing advice: change … to … Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

THE EDITOR’S NOTE

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

strong rain, took conclusions, did a review (formal: carried out), very very cold, and stuff (in writing), I’m very thankful (deeply grateful). C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE EDITORIAL BOARD (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the three writers, your student joins the Monday meeting and votes on three proposals for the magazine (yes, no, or propose a change), then has one minute of last word on AI editing. You are the editor-in-chief. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the vote harder. Listen for the Unit 1 precision (raise a concern, draw a conclusion, strongly / deeply / highly, move the needle), recast quietly while they talk, and at the end ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · Memory.
  • Out loud: II · The Writers and III · The Editorial Board.
  • Focus: IV · Find the Mistakes and VI · I Can.
  • Write: V · The Editor’s Note.
