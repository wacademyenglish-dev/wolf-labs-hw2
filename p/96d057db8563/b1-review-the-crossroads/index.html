The Crossroads · B1 Unit 5 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 5 (If, when, unless). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student hosts a podcast called The Crossroads. You are three guests with a big decision: Julia (a job in Canada), Rafa (leaving the bank to open a restaurant) and Bia (a gap year). Then the student gives advice on the air.

About 40 min: I · Speed Round (6) · II · The Crossroads (15) · III · The Arena (7) · IV · The Hot Seat (8) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “Sixty seconds. Ready? Start the clock.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

zero conditional · first conditional · unless · second conditional and If I were you · third conditional

SPEED ROUND

A 60-second speed round. Tap Start the clock, then Right or Pass for each item; the answer is on your screen. At the end of class, play again and beat the score.

THE CROSSROADS

You are each guest. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you ask: “What would you do if you were me?” The student answers with If I were you, I’d… Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

THE STORY IN ORDER

The events are mixed. The student taps them in order; wrong order shows in red. When it’s right, the student tells the whole story with first, then, after that, finally.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

if I will go, if I would have known, unless it doesn’t rain, if I was you (accept it, but model were), if I would have money, I would called (for would have called). B1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE ARENA (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the three guests, your student takes a side: at a crossroads, the safe plan or the big risk? One minute for their side, then they swap and defend the other one. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence before they start, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Recast quietly (if you try, you’ll; unless; if I had known, I would have); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Speed Round and V · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Crossroads and III · The Arena and IV · The Hot Seat.
