All I Want Is Time · C1 Unit 3 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on focusing clefts: All I want is…, The only thing I miss is…, The reason why I left was…, The last thing I expected was…, The one person who…

The story: Rita (invented), a chef with two stars and a TV show, left it all to run a tiny café by the sea. A weekend magazine interviews her.

About 49 min: I · The Interview (10) · II · Information or Spotlight? (9) · III · Turn On the Spotlight (7) · IV · The TV Offer (8) · V · All You Need (7) · VI · Your Confession (8).

Open with: “Imagine you had your dream job and were famous. Why would you ever leave?”

These structures are emotional: they say “this, and nothing else”. Read the interview with real feeling (you can be the magazine). In the comparison, ask about the effect, not the grammar. Watch for “All what” and “The reason is because”: they are the classic Brazilian traps.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

All I want / need is… · The only thing … is… · The reason (why) … is / was… · The last / first thing … was… · The one person who… · All you have to do is + verb

THE INTERVIEW

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

INFORMATION OR SPOTLIGHT?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

TURN ON THE SPOTLIGHT

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

ALL YOU NEED

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

YOUR CONFESSION

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

all what I want, the reason is because, all I want are, all you have to do is to ask (stiff), the only thing what, the reason of I left. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE TV OFFER (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the transformations, your student is Rita and you are the TV producer who wants her back. Your card shows your secret limit (only you see it): six episodes, filmed at her café. Ask the three questions in character, wait five seconds in silence, and let the student protect her limit with clefts (All I want is…, The only thing I’d accept is…). Use the PUSH line to raise the pressure, then close the deal on the screen. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · The Interview.
  • Focus: II · Information or Spotlight? and III · Turn On the Spotlight.
  • Out loud: IV · The TV Offer and V · All You Need.
  • Write: VI · Your Confession.
