What’s the Weather Like? · The 7 O’Clock Weather

Tonight is the student’s first night as the new weather presenter on Channel 9, live at 7 PM. On the studio wall, five live pictures around a globe: Toronto, London, Sydney, Chicago and Seattle. The magnets only stick to a whole sentence with It’s. Then the season dial: twelve months, north and south, eight cities. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

It’s + the weather: It’s sunny, cloudy, windy, hot, cold, 31 degrees. The verb is be (never make or have). Nouns get -y (sun → sunny); heat → hot. Right now: It’s raining, It’s snowing. Every year: It snows in winter. Seasons and months with in. What’s the weather like?

THE TRAP

Many learners leave out the subject (Is raining), use make for the weather (It makes cold), use the noun (It’s sun, very heat) and mix time and weather (How is the time?). Also: How is the weather like?, on winter, in the January, It’s rain now.

THE VIDEO

A one-year time-lapse of one garden, no words, in five chapters: one per season. The student is the presenter.

THE BEST SUNDAY (VII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After Walt’s call, the student puts three kinds of weather in order for a free Sunday. Say your order first (the button shows it), then let them tap 1, 2, 3; wait five seconds in silence and let them explain number one with It’s … and When it’s …, I …. The PUSH line (only you see it) is a quick yes or no. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one light personal question. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I You and the Weather, III The Weather Wall (before the rule: tap the version you heard), V The Season Dial (south turns around: July is winter in Rio, but warm and sunny), VI A Viewer Calls (you are Walt; the big question closes it), VII The Best Sunday (a ranking: your order first), VIII Your Weather.
  • Listen: II One Year, Forty Seconds.
  • Focus: IV The Rule.
  • Write: IX, tonight’s weather for their city and every season, with the red wavy line for Is raining, makes cold, It has 31 degrees, It’s sun / heat, It’s rain now, on winter, in the July, How is the weather like? It goes to their My notes.

We use American English (fall) and degrees Celsius. No audio in this lesson: your voice is Dana’s and Walt’s.
