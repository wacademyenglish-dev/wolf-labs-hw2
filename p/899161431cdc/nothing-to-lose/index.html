Better Speaking · Episode 6 · Nothing to Lose · B1

Episode 6 of Better Speaking, B1 version. Conchita Martínez practiced against a wall at ten; years later she played her hero at Wimbledon. “I don’t have anything to lose.” The language is something, anything, nothing. The sister lesson for B2 is “Like, You Know”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — Your dream, What’s in the bag. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — Nothing, anything, Say it another way, Fix the traps, Choose the right word.
  • Write — Nothing to lose. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

The Portuguese double negative: I don’t have nothing. Something in every question. And “nada a perder” becomes nothing for lose.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, matches and writes. “Only you see this” is yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. The bar on top shows the season: eight episodes, today’s one lit. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
