Plan B · The Radar

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: The first conditional is for a real possibility: If it rains, we’ll move inside.

The grammar: if + present, will + verb · when (sure) × if (maybe) · unless · as soon as · until · in case.

Short class (about 46 min): I Your Weekend · II Sam’s Message · III The Radar · IV Maybe or Sure? · V The Same Sentence, Twice · VI Ask Me · X Back to Your Weekend. Skip if you’re short on time: VII, VIII The Concert in the Park and IX The Chain (marked EXTRA on your screen). On every practice page the student speaks first and taps Check on their own screen. Full lesson: about 66 min.

The first conditional is for a real possibility: If it rains, we’ll move inside. If + present, will + verb, never If it will rain. The same machine runs with other doors: when (sure), unless (if not), as soon as, until, and in case (a precaution you take now). The screen is a weather radar at night: every checked plan locks a blip in green.

SCENE I IS NOT CORRECTED

Your student will say If it will rain. Write it down. It comes back in scene X.

THE PAGES

Sam’s Message (II): Sam, the manager of Saturday’s concert in the park, writes to the crew. The student reads it out loud and taps the IF half of each plan (If it rains, When the band arrives, unless the field is dry…; every word can be tapped, wrong taps go red). Then they finish the rule by choosing inside each line. A small copy of the rule stays in the corner on the next pages.

The Radar (III): six places. The student reads the forecast and the prompt, says the plan B out loud, then taps Check: the right sentence appears and the blip locks. Under it, one question to ask you.

Maybe or Sure? (IV): six situations. The student says the sentence with IF or WHEN, then taps Check.

The Same Sentence, Twice (V): four recordings. The student plays each line and taps the small word they hear (if / when, unless / until, in case / if). Then they tell you which line is sure, and when the coat leaves the house.

Ask Me (VI): the student builds each question out loud (what · you · do · if · it rains…), taps Check, then asks you. Answer for real, and compare.

If It Will Rain (VII, extra): six sentences, one wrong part in each. The student says the whole sentence right, then taps Check.

The Concert in the Park (VIII): you are Sam, the nervous manager. Four risks: the student says the plan B, taps Check, then you ask the “And what if…?” on the screen, and they answer with a new first conditional. Under pressure, WILL sneaks into the IF half.

The Chain (IX): five links, shuffled. The student taps them in order and taps Check; then says the whole chain and makes one of their own.

THE RECORDINGS

Four short lines: IF and WHEN in the same sentence, UNLESS, and IN CASE. Lines 1 and 2 sound almost the same; that’s the point. Each plays with pause and 0.75×.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

If it will rain, when I will arrive, unless it doesn’t rain, if she call, we’ll still to have.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Your Weekend, III The Radar, VI Ask Me, VIII The Concert in the Park, IX The Chain.
  • Listen: V The Same Sentence, Twice (four recordings).
  • Focus: II Sam’s Message, IV Maybe or Sure?, VII If It Will Rain.
  • Write: X, their own plan B. It goes to their My notes.
