Nice to Meet You · The Welcome Desk

A first-hour lesson. Your student is at the welcome desk of an evening event. Four strangers are in the queue: Tim, Sian (said “Shan”), Georgie and Buli. The student’s badge fills in letter by letter when they spell their name, and every guest who hears hello, a name and nice to meet you gets a badge and goes in. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

Hello four ways (Hello · Hi · Hey · Hiya). A name: I’m … · My name’s … Two questions: What’s your name? · How do you spell it? Other people: His name’s … · Her name’s … The mistakes to listen for: My name’s is …, answering Fine to Nice to meet you, his for a woman, and How you spell?

THE BADGE

In scene I, tap exactly the letters you hear. If they say E for I, tap E and let them see it on the badge. Print it when the name is complete: it comes back in the party and at the end.

YOUR PICK (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Three light choices at the welcome party, two options each: who says hello first, a name or a nickname on the badge, and a famous guest at the door (a singer or a football player, so his and her come out). There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) for one more small question. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: II Six Sounds at the Door (listen, say it three times, then the words appear), III the video, VI His or Her. Every audio has pause and 0.75×; the transcript opens only after listening, and every transcript was checked against the audio.
  • Out loud: I Your Badge, IV The Queue, VII Five Moments, VIII Your Pick, IX The Welcome Party, X Your People.
  • Focus: V Spell It (the letters live in seven sound families: A H J K · B C D E G P T V Z · F L M N S X · I Y · O · Q U W · R; G and J are the classic swap).
  • Write: XI Goodbye, a whole first conversation, with the red wavy line for name’s is, What’s you name, How you spell, I call Ana, she name. It goes to their My notes.

THE PARTY

Scene IX: you are the guest on the card. Don’t say hello first: wait for the student to start. Tap each of the five things as you hear them, and spell your name when they ask. The spelling is on your side only.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The badge, the queue, the letter families and the circle move on both screens. G opens this guide.
