Better Speaking · Episode 1 · Why Didn’t You Start in Greece? · B1

Episode 1 of the BBC Learning English podcast Better Speaking, in its B1 version (shorter clips, simpler tasks). Stelios Haji-Ioannou is Greek, but he started his airline in London; everybody asks him why. The language is the negative question in the past (Why didn’t you…? Didn’t you…?) and his trick: ask the question first, then answer it. The sister lesson for B2 is “Organize Your Speaking”. No points, no timer.

THIS SCENE

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — Big decisions, Ask it, then answer it. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — Why didn’t you…, Find the answer, Fix the traps, Choose the right words.
  • Write — Your question, your answer. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

The -ed stays after didn’t: Why didn’t you started? The word order follows Portuguese: Why you didn’t come? And “por que você não…” becomes Why not you called me?

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, matches and writes. “Only you see this” is yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. The bar on top shows the season: eight episodes, today’s one lit. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
