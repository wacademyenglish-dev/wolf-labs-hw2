I Grew Up in Smallville · Superman & Lois · The Barn Album

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: A baby from the sky, a reporter, and the road back home.

The grammar: The Past: Moved, came, grew up. Didn’t tell. Did he…?

Short class (about 50 min): I · III · V Martha’s Letter · VI The Barn Album · VII Ask Me · VIII. Skip if you’re short on time: IV Line by Line. On every practice page the student speaks first and taps Check on their own screen.

Open with: “You know Clark’s past now. In the trailer, why did the family go back to the farm? Guess.”

Superman & Lois (The CW, 2021–2024) stars Tyler Hoechlin as Clark Kent and Elizabeth Tulloch as Lois Lane. A baby came to Earth from another planet; a farmer and his wife found him in Kansas, and he grew up on their farm in Smallville. Clark moved to Metropolis, met Lois at the Daily Planet, and they had twin boys. Years later, the family goes back to the farm, and one of the boys finds out his father’s secret. The grammar: past simple, regular (moved, loved, lied) and irregular (came, grew up, met, had, went, told), didn’t + base verb, and Did he…? / Yes, he did.

A note for the teacher: this is the second lesson with this series (the A1 one is the Kent fridge); this one is the barn. The trailer is family drama with a little action; nothing violent is on the lesson screens. The times follow its captions (23/09), and each moment is told in our own words.

Warm-up and Put It in Order (I): two parts. 1 · Warm-up: three questions about the student’s life, one at a time (big on the screen, with “Ask more” under each and one short line about the show under the photo). Let them talk; A2: short answers are fine. 2 · Put It in Order: four photos of Clark’s life, shuffled. The student taps them in order (“First… then…”); then the cards show the real order, and the student tells his story in the past.

The words (II): dangerous, lie, together, abandon and needs, before the video, in mixed letters and the word in your life. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (IV) lists the lines with the yellow words: tap a line, hear it again, and the student says it like the character.

The Trailer (III): after “Watch the trailer now”, two short tasks sit next to the video. Which word did you hear? four sentences with a pair of words that sound alike; the student taps one. One word is wrong: the student says the right word, then taps Check. Every line has a ▶ time button: it plays the trailer from that moment.

Martha’s Letter (VI): a letter from Martha Kent, 1979, the night the ship fell. The student reads it out loud and taps the verbs in the past (every word can be tapped; wrong taps go red). Then they finish the rule on the right by choosing inside each line (-ed, found, didn’t cry, Did). When the rule is complete, a small copy of it stays in the corner on VII and VIII.

The Barn Album (VII): six photos. The clue shows the year and the verb. The student says the sentence first, then taps Check: the right sentence appears and the photo hangs on the wall. Under it, one question about the student’s life. You listen and talk; there is nothing for you to tap.

Ask Me (VIII): the student is the reporter. They build each question out loud from the prompt (where · grow up), tap Check, then ask you. Answer for real, and they find one thing you have in common.

The ending (IX): you pick A or B. A: the student is Clark and you are a reporter from the Daily Planet. B: the student’s own album (the place they grew up, the day they moved, a secret, a hero). Three minutes. Then one minute: three prompts and a short note that goes to the student’s My notes.

ON SCREEN

The cover is a Kansas farm at dusk with a red barn. I: Warm-up and Put It in Order. III: the trailer in three steps. IV: five frames on the film strip. V: Who Was Right?, three choices from the trailer. VI: Martha’s letter on dark old paper, and the rule plaque. VII: The Barn Album, a string across the wooden barn wall with six empty frames from 1979 to 2021; every sentence the student checks hangs that year’s photo. VIII: Ask Me. IX: two posters, A and B, and the note for My notes.

THE COMMON ERRORS

comed, growed, meeted, haved, goed / wented, moveed, did grew / did met, didn’t told, no told, Did he told…?, He told Lois…? (Did he tell…?), in 1997 he move.

WHO WAS RIGHT? (V) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after Line by Line, three choices from the trailer: was it right to keep the Superman secret from the boys, was it a good idea to move to the farm, and was Lois right (“this family needs you more”)? There is no right answer: let the student choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to bring it to their own life. The starters are in the past (-ed, irregular verbs, didn’t): recast mistakes quietly (comed, didn’t told), and at the end ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About five minutes: the hot seat stays at the end (IX).

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Warm-up and Put It in Order, V Who Was Right?, VII The Barn Album, VIII Ask Me and IX The Hot Seat.
  • Listen: II The Words, III The Trailer and IV Line by Line.
  • Focus: VI Martha’s Letter.
  • Write: the note at the end of IX, and the homework.
