Mind Matters · The Certainty Dial

Portuguese uses one verb for deduction and another for obligation, and both turn into have to in the student’s head. English splits them: must be is you reading the proof; have to is somebody forcing. Patient 07 is in the waiting room; the student is the doctor’s assistant. The screen is a dial from 0 to 100%: can’t be · might be · must be.

NEEDLE FIRST

In scene IV the student sets the needle before saying a word. A wrong zone shakes; then they say the sentence, and you open the chart line.

THE REAL RULE

Scene III, round 2: there HAVE TO is correct (a clinic rule). Don’t let the student learn “have to is wrong”.

ONLY WHAT HE SAID · THE PAST IS ELSEWHERE

Scene II: you read Patient 07’s six lines; nothing invented goes on a chart. must have been is not this hour: keep everything about now.

THE MIND READER (V) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

In the middle of the lesson, your student leaves the clinic and goes to a party: they are the mind reader, you are the host. Ask the five questions on the screen; your student answers in the role with must be, might be, can’t be. Wait five seconds in silence before you help. After every answer, use the PUSH line (only you see it): “Are you sure? Prove it.” Recast quietly (can’t be, not must not be); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.
