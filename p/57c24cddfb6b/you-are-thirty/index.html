You Are Thirty · Blow Out the Candles

In English you don’t have your age: you are your age. The same is true for a whole family of states: I’m hungry, thirsty, cold, hot, afraid, sleepy. Many languages use have for these; English uses be. The hour is a birthday cake: every sentence with I am lights a candle. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

I’m thirty. She’s seventy. I’m hungry. Are you cold? Things use have: I have a phone. How old are you? How old is she? The mistakes: I have 30 years, I have hungry, Do you have cold?, How many years do you have?, How old you are?

THE BEAT

For thirteen × thirty, clap the beat: DA-da for thirty, da-DA for thirteen. The ending disappears in fast speech; the beat stays.

THE AUDIO

Pause and 0.75× on every recording; the words appear only after listening. Checked against the audio: the six party guests introduce themselves (“Hi, I am Ana. I am eighteen.”); the student tells you She’s eighteen.

WOULD YOU RATHER? (VI) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

In the middle of the lesson, three choices of two: a birthday party at the beach or in the snow, pizza or bed at midnight, one day at eighteen or at fifty. There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it). The starters keep I’m + a state or an age in their mouth. Recast quietly while they talk (I have cold → I’m cold); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I On You or in the Bag (first, ask How old are you? and tap how they said it; the bag sends thirty back).
  • Out loud: II The Family of Am (mime one at the end), VI Would You Rather? (choose, then say it with I’m), VII Guess How I Am, VIII The Family Party (real people; ends with questions about them).
  • Listen: III Listen, IV Where the Beat Falls, V How Old Is She?.
  • Write: IX, a birthday card, with the red wavy line for have + age, have + a state, Do you have cold?, How many years, How old you are? It goes to their My notes.
