The Challenge · Episode 1 · Three People

The first episode of the BBC Learning English series The London to Edinburgh Challenge: three people, one hundred pounds, five days. Today the contestants meet, get their phones, money and maps, and the race starts. The language is the first thing anyone says to a stranger: I’m + name, I’m + age, I’m from + city, and the numbers 10 to 100. No points, no timer.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — Say hello, Meet the contestants, Count the money. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Episode 1, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the two Before questions.
  • Focus — I’m, Ten to one hundred, Three traps, Practice.
  • Write — Your contestant badge. It goes to the student’s My notes.

THE TRAP

Portuguese says “eu tenho 25 anos”, so students say I have 25. “Sou de Curitiba” becomes I’m of Curitiba. And the d of “I’m” disappears in writing: I from York.

IN THE LIVE ROOM

The student taps, votes and writes. “Only you see this” is yours: GOT IT when the answer works, ONE MORE when you want another question. The map on top shows the season; today the first stop lights up. Arrows move the scenes; G opens this guide.
