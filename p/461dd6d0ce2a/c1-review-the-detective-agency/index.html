The Detective Agency · C1 Unit 2 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 2 (Modals with nuance). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: A rainy night at a detective agency (invented). Mrs. Vane’s pearl necklace has gone from the safe. The case is a board game of twelve clues; each clue needs a modal with nuance. Suggested answer: the nephew knew the code.

About 43 min: I · The Die (8) · II · The Case Board (16) · III · The Fee (7) · IV · The Hot Seat (8) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “Roll the die. The number chooses the challenge.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

needn’t have × didn’t need to · softened requests: could we possibly… · bound to / set to / due to · will / would for typical behaviour · will / would for deduction · needn’t × mustn’t

THE DIE

Tap Roll the die: the number chooses one of the six topics (no repeats until all six are done). The student does the challenge from memory.

THE CASE BOARD

A board game around the table. Tap Roll the die: the token moves and the square shows a task that mixes the grammar of the unit. The student does it out loud; tap Done and roll again until the end of the board.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I needn’t have to, he will left (will have left), could we possible, prices must rise (for bound to), would have a car (state: used to), you mustn’t pay, it’s free. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE FEE (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Case closed: now your student, the detective, negotiates the fee with Mrs. Vane (you). They want £3,000 and will not go below £2,000. Your secret (only you see it): you will go up to £2,500 if they promise the press never hears about the nephew. Ask the four questions in role, wait five seconds in silence after each, and use the PUSH line to make it harder. Listen for Unit 2 (Could we possibly…?, Would you be willing to…?, needn’t have, bound to, needn’t × mustn’t); recast quietly while they talk, close the deal on the screen, and at the end ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: II · The Case Board and III · The Fee and IV · The Hot Seat.
