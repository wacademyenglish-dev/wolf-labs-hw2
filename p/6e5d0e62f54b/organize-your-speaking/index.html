Better Speaking · Episode 1 · Organize Your Speaking · B2

Episode 1 of the BBC Learning English podcast Better Speaking. Stelios Haji-Ioannou, the Greek founder of a British airline, explains why he started in London; teacher Richard Hallows shows why he is so easy to follow. The language is signposting: phrases that tell the listener what is coming (An interesting question is… Let me tell you about… on top of that, and another thing is, but in fact, on the other hand), and the Stelios move: ask the listener’s question, then answer it. The sister lesson for B1 is “Why Didn’t You Start in Greece?”. No points, no timer.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud — Are you easy to follow, Ask it, then answer it. After each answer a post-it comes up in the middle with starters and a Help.
  • Listen — Only the clips of the episode, in three steps. The video unlocks when the student answers the Before questions; in Watch, play the clips in order.
  • Focus — Tell them what’s coming, Match the signal, Fix the traps, Choose the marker.
  • Write — Your one-minute answer. It goes to the student’s My notes; then the student says it without reading.

THE TRAP

Portuguese markers translated word by word: “na verdade” becomes in the truth, “além disso” becomes beyond that, “por outro lado” becomes for other side. And Brazilian learners love idioms; Stelios’s advice is to keep it simple with non-native speakers.
