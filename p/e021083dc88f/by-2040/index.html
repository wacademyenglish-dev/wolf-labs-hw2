By 2040 We Will Have … · The Blueprint

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: The city is making a time capsule to open in 2040.

The grammar: The Rule: Stand in 2040. Look back, and look around.

Short class (about 50 min): I Your Future · III The Future of Cities · V The Rule · VI The 2040 Blueprint · VII The Capsule · X The Arena · XI Open in 2040. Skip if you’re short on time: VIII, IX (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 64 min.

Open with: “How old will you be in 2040? How do you imagine yourself?”

The city is making a time capsule to open in 2040. The student watches a short video on the future of cities, predicts the 2040 skyline on a blueprint, and fills the capsule with messages to their future self. The grammar is two ways of standing in the future and looking around: will have + participle (finished before that moment: by 2040 we will have built …) and will be + -ing (in progress at that moment: in 2040 we will be growing food on rooftops). On screen, a 2040 city plan: eight plots, and a building rises in blueprint lines when the prediction comes out in the right form. A ruler with 2040 in the middle shows the two arrows: look back, look around.

THE VIDEO

Scene III: a four-minute video, sealed until you press play, in five chapters that stop by themselves. The last chapter is the future: listen for will and might. The narrator says what WILL happen; the student then stands in 2040 and says what will HAVE happened. After the video (page III, step 3): ONE screen. The video stays on the left, with the 2040 city plan under it; on the right, six True or Lie lines from the video (tap the time to hear that moment again). Every right answer raises a building on the plan. On a Lie the student says the true line. When all six are done, Noa asks one last question (“Which idea will your city have by 2040?”): the student taps an answer and says the sentence out loud, with will have or will be.

THE TRAPS

By 2040 we will build … (finished before: will HAVE built) · In 2040 I will work at home for a scene in progress (will BE working) · will have finish · until June for a deadline (by June) · by the time you will read (by the time you read).

THE BLUEPRINT

Scene IV is the pressure: read each plot; the student predicts; tap the form you heard; the building goes up. A present-simple or simple-future answer does not build anything.

THE CAPSULE

Scene VII: six slips. Every slip wants one sentence of each: what they will have done, and what they will be doing. Tap each form you hear; the slip drops into the capsule when both are there.

The words (II): urban, increase, settlements, surplus and overcrowded, before the video, in the missing word and the word in your life. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (IV, extra): every line of the video; tap one to hear it again, and the lines with yellow words open the explanation.

THE ARENA (X) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the capsule, one argument from the video’s idea of homes built up, not out: the city wants ten housing towers in the big park by 2040. The student picks a side and defends it for one minute with will have and will be; then they swap and defend the other side. Wait five seconds in silence before they start, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: II The Words, III The Future of Cities and IV Line by Line.

  • Write: XI Open in 2040.
