The Final Talk · C1 Unit 10 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the final project of Book C1: the student plans and gives a five-minute talk using the tools of the book (hook, story arc, nuance, contrast, rephrasing, close), then answers questions and marks what they can do.

The story: Graduation night (invented): the student is the last speaker of the evening. The model talk is by a speaker who froze in a meeting in 2022 and decided to change.

About 51 min: I · The Die (8) · II · A Model Talk (10) · III · Unpopular Opinions (7) · IV · Your Talk Plan (12) · V · Questions from the Floor (10) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “If you had five minutes on a stage tonight, what topic would you choose?”

This is a celebration, not a test. The die warms up the six tools. The model talk shows them all in five minutes (each part is labelled). Give the student real time to write the plan, then let them speak for five minutes WITHOUT interrupting: take notes for later. In the Q&A, ask the questions on your screen about THEIR talk. Finish with I can, and congratulate: Book C1 is complete.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

the hook · the story arc · nuance: hedging and modals · contrast and concession · rephrasing · the close: clefts and a call to action

THE DIE

Tap Roll the die: the number chooses one of the six topics (no repeats until all six are done). The student does the challenge from memory.

A MODEL TALK

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

YOUR TALK PLAN

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

QUESTIONS FROM THE FLOOR

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. This is the last lesson of Book C1. After the student stamps it, congratulate them: the whole book is complete.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

starting with my name / an apology, telling the story in the present by mistake, on the contrary (for on the other hand), I don’t know. (dry, in the Q&A), in conclusion, to conclude, no call to action at the end. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

UNPOPULAR OPINIONS (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the model talk and before your student writes their own, four unpopular opinions about speaking in public (slides, nerves, reading from paper, accents). They tap a number from 1 to 5 and defend it with the tools of the book (It could be argued that…, Granted… On the other hand…, The way I see it…, In other words…). There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then take the opposite number and use the PUSH line (only you see it). Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. A strong opinion here is often a good topic for their talk in IV.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Die and VI · I Can.
  • Listen: II · A Model Talk.
  • Write: IV · Your Talk Plan.
  • Out loud: III · Unpopular Opinions and V · Questions from the Floor.
