The Hidden Present · A Birthday at Midnight

It is midnight before Leo’s birthday, and his present is hidden somewhere in the house. Leo is awake on the stairs and keeps pointing at things. The center of the screen is the wrapped present: the gold ribbon cuts the box into four corners, one or more against near or far, and each word lives in one corner. This lesson uses the body: your student holds, points at and shows real things from their own home on the camera. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

this (one, near) · that (one, far) · these (more, near) · those (more, far). One takes is, more takes are: What’s that? What are those? The mistakes: this are, these is, What is those?, that shoes. The sound: this /ðɪs/ is short, these /ðiːz/ is long. Smile on these.

YOUR PICK (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Leo is asleep: three choices with this, that, these and those: his present, the party cake, and a present for your student. Answer first yourself, as a model, and point at the screen while you say it. Then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and ask the PUSH line (only you see it): a yes / no question. Recast quietly while they talk (this sneakers → these sneakers); at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves. About 5 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. There are three; use one when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Show Me (hold it, point at it, on camera), IV Leo Is Awake (you are Leo), V Hot and Cold, VI Point and Ask, VII The Birthday Plan.
  • Focus: II The Ribbon Box (tap the corner), III Near or Far?, VIII Your Pick (out loud).
  • Write: IX Back to Bed, their own room, with the red wavy line for this are, these is, What is those and that shoes. It goes to their My notes.

HOT AND COLD

The student picks a place and asks the question; you answer. The present is in the drawer (look closely at the photo). The bed is warmer; the books and the wardrobe are colder.
