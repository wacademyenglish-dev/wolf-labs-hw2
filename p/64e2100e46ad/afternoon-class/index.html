What Are They Doing? · Afternoon Class

A lesson about right now. It is two o’clock, the school is warm, and everybody is falling asleep. On screen, the school building at dusk: eight lit windows, something happening in each. The heart of the hour is Afternoon Class, an animated short film with no dialogue: the student is the voice of the film. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

am / is / are + verb-ing: The cat is sleeping. The spelling: +ing (eating), drop the e (writing), double the short ones (running). No: isn’t / aren’t / I’m not (never I amn’t). Question: Is it raining? — Yes, it is. The mistakes: The cat sleeping, I am sleep, writeing, runing, You are studying?

THE FILM

Seven short chapters, each one stops by itself. After each chapter, the student says what is happening; open Show answer only if they need it. If the video doesn’t play in your call, open it on YouTube and share that tab.

WOULD YOU RATHER? (VII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the film, the student is in the sleepy two o’clock class: coffee or the desk, go home or wake the teacher up. Answer first, then let them choose; wait five seconds in silence and let them say the whole sentence with I’m …ing. The PUSH line (only you see it) is a quick yes or no. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one light personal question. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Two O’Clock, II The School Windows (tap the version you heard), VI Zoom In (the student asks Is it …ing?; every no opens the photo), VIII The Phone Call (you are Maya), VII Would You Rather? (the student chooses, with I’m …ing), and the big question: is a nap OK?
  • Listen: V The Film (no words: the student narrates).
  • Focus: III The Rule, IV Spell the -ing.
  • Write: IX, what is happening in their home right now, with the red wavy line for a missing is, a missing -ing, writeing, runing, amn’t and the question without the swap. It goes to their My notes.

No audio in this lesson: your voice is Maya’s.
