Under the Sofa · A1 Unit 4 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new A1 lesson on saying where things are: in, on, under, next to, behind, between; Where is…? / Where are…? It’s… / They’re…

The story: Lia, the neighbor in 4B (invented), is on a trip. The student has her keys, her plants and her cat, Mochi. Mochi, the keys and the student’s phone are lost in the apartment.

About 46 min: I · The Note (9) · II · In, On or Under? (9) · III · Where Is Mochi? (15) · IV · Mochi’s Bed (6) · V · Your Home (7).

Open with: “Where are your keys right now? Point and say.”

A1 students only need short answers here: “Under the sofa.” is a perfect answer. Point at real things around you and the student during the lesson. In the mission, answer only yes / no and short words, so the student does the asking.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

in (inside, rooms) · on (surfaces) · under · next to · behind · between

THE NOTE

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

IN, ON OR UNDER?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

WHERE IS MOCHI?

Each card is a lost thing, and only you know where it is. The student asks; tap each question when it is asked, and answer short (yes, no, a word). If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. At the end Lia calls and the student says where each thing is. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

YOUR HOME (V)

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

on the kitchen (in the kitchen), in the table (on the table), next the door (next to the door), between of (between), the keys is (the keys are). A1: one correction per answer is enough.

MOCHI’S BED (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Mochi is found, and now the student decides where Mochi sleeps: three places, 1 to 3. Say your order first, short (“Number 1: next to the window”), then let them tap theirs, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it): a yes / no question. Then tap Show my teacher’s order and agree on one top 3. Recast quietly (“in the kitchen” for “on the kitchen”); at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one light personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · The Note.
  • Focus: II · In, On or Under?.
  • Out loud: III · Where Is Mochi?, IV · Mochi’s Bed and V · Your Home.
