Six Eggs · The Notebook on the 7:40

An easy reader in five chapters. Sofia finds an old notebook on the 7:40 train: no name, no phone number, a shopping list with six eggs, a bad drawing of a cat, and half an address. She works out the other half with because. The grammar of the hour is the four words that join two facts: and, but, so, because. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

One sentence, a comma before and / but / so, a small letter after it. So puts the result second; because puts the reason second: A, so B is B because A. The trap: because where the student means so, and but for any second idea. Scene VII turns every SO sentence into a BECAUSE sentence.

LISTEN FIRST

Every chapter now starts with the recording, text closed (pause and 0.75× on the player). The text opens only after listening; then you read it together, then the questions. The five recordings were checked against the text.

THE SURPRISE QUESTION

This lesson already has its conversation moments: the bet in III, the dilemma in VIII and the personal scenes in I and XII. So it only gets the Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it): one short personal question with and, but, so or because, at any moment. Answer first yourself, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (a quick yes or no). Use it when the lesson has been only listening and exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: the five chapters (II, IV, VI, IX, XI).
  • Out loud: I Your Morning, III What Is Inside?, VIII What Would You Do? (often the first time an A1 student argues in English: give it time), X Tell It Back, XII Your Week.
  • Focus: V Four Words (pairs 6 and 7 are the same two facts in the other order), VII Turn It Around (the trap piece is so).
  • Write: XIII, a true story, with the red wavy line for a full stop before and / but / so, a capital after the comma, “Because.” alone and because of + a sentence. It goes to their My notes.

THE DEDUCTION AND THE LAST LINE

In chapter four the student picks the street (only Canal Street goes past 40) and says the sentence with because. In chapter two the list has one line nobody can read; in chapter five the student reveals it: a big box. Don’t say it for them.

IF YOU ARE LATE

Cut two pairs in V and two sentences in VII. Don’t cut I, IX or XII.
