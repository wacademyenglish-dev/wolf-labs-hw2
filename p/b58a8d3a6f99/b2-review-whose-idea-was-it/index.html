Whose Idea Was It? · B2 Unit 7 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 7 (Reporting and describing). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: The launch of the Glow Mug (an invented product) was a disaster: half the mugs broke. Your student works in HR. You are three colleagues: Priya (marketing), Otto (production) and Mr. Lane (the boss, who finally admits it was his idea).

About 41 min: I · Bingo (8) · II · The Meeting (15) · III · The Jury (7) · IV · Would You Rather? (7) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “Nine squares. Pick one, answer, and make a line.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

reported speech at work · reporting verbs + -ing: deny, admit, suggest, insist on · reporting verbs + to: promise, refuse, agree · extreme adjectives · compound adjectives · double comparatives

BINGO

A 3×3 card of quick tasks. The student picks a square and does it out loud; tap Done. Three in a line (across, down or diagonal) is a BINGO. Keep going for a second line if there is time.

THE MEETING

You are each colleague. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the director: the student reports with denied / admitted / insisted on / promised. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

WOULD YOU RATHER?

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

she denied to choose, he suggested us to launch, very exhausted, a ten-hours flight, the more we rushed, more mugs broke, he insisted to launch. B2: one correction per answer is enough.

THE JURY (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the HR interviews, the student is the jury: for Priya, Otto and Mr. Lane they read the evidence on the screen, choose a verdict (or say their own) and report what each person said with denied, admitted, insisted on and promised. There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the verdict harder. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Bingo and V · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Meeting, III · The Jury and IV · Would You Rather?.
