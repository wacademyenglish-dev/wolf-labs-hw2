The Pet Hotel · A1 Unit 3 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 3 (Whose is it? What have you got?). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student works at the desk of a hotel for pets. You are the three owners: Maria, Ken and the twins.

About 48 min: I · The Die (8) · II · The Pet Hotel (15) · III · Your Favorite Guest (5) · IV · Memory (8) · V · The Hot Seat (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Pick a door.” There is no order. One minute per door, no notes.

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

his / her / their / its · the possessive ’s · whose, mine / yours / hers · have got + short answers · have / has · has got to describe

THE DIE

Tap Roll the die: the number chooses one of the six topics (no repeats until all six are done). The student does the challenge from memory.

THE PET HOTEL

You are each owner. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the room card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then the owners come back: the student says whose pet it is (Maria’s dog, the twins’ rabbit) and describes it with has got. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

MEMORY

A memory game. The student opens two cards; when they match, the student makes a sentence with the pair (example under Show answer).

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

the dog of Maria, his dog (for a woman), it have big ears, she have, Yes, I have got, it’s my (for mine). A1: one correction per answer is enough.

YOUR FAVORITE GUEST (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the pets go home, a light ranking: Bolt, Miso and Pip from 1 to 3. Tap “Show my teacher’s order” to give yours; the student taps theirs and says why (Ken’s cat, it has got…). Answer first yourself, as a model. Then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and ask the PUSH line (only you see it): a yes / no question. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves. About 5 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. There are three; use one when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Die and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Pet Hotel, III · Your Favorite Guest and V · The Hot Seat.
  • Listen: IV · Memory.
