When the Sun Goes Down · From · The Town Rules

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: A town you can’t leave, and a night you can’t go out in.

The grammar: When the Sun Goes Down: When the sun goes down, the creatures come out.

The class (about 50 min): I Warm-up and The Town Rules · II The Words · III The Trailer · V Line by Line · VI The Sheriff’s Notice · VII The Town Rules · VIII Your Rules · IX One Minute. No extra scenes: every scene is part of the class.

Open with: “You know the rules now. In the trailer, what happens when someone breaks a rule? Guess.”

From (MGM+, 2022), a horror series with Harold Perrineau as Boyd, the sheriff. A family leaves the highway, finds a tree on the road, and ends up in a small town that every road brings you back to. In the day it is quiet; when the sun goes down, creatures with human faces come out of the forest and smile at the windows. So the town lives by rules. The grammar: the zero conditional: if / when + present, present for rules that are always true (when the sun goes down, the creatures come out), with the -s of he / she / it, no will, and What happens if…?

A note for the teacher: real horror. There are creepy smiling faces, a voice at a window, screams, and one quick “God damn it”. Nothing bloody stays on screen. The times come from the trailer’s automatic captions (07/10), told in our own words.

Warm-up and The Town Rules (I): two parts. 1 · Warm-up: three questions about the student’s life, one at a time (big on the screen, with “Ask more” under each and one short line about the film under the photo). Let them talk; don’t correct the grammar yet. 2 · The Town Rules: Four rules of the town appear one at a time (the road brings you back, everyone is inside when the sun goes down, the stone in the window, never open the door at night). For each, the student taps Yes / No way / Only if… and says why, with when or if. At the end, they invent a fifth rule for the town. Horror without scares: nothing is described, only the rules.

The words (II): storm, highway, detour, stuck and rules, before the video, in hear it in the video and mystery meaning. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (V) lists the lines with the yellow words: tap a line, hear it again, and the student says it like the character.

The ending (IX, One Minute): the student picks a topic (the rules of your house, what always happens when you are stressed, how to survive one night in From) and talks for one minute with if / when + present. Then the teacher. Speaking only.

ON SCREEN

The cover is the town’s main street at dusk. I: Warm-up and The Town Rules. III: the trailer in three steps. Before: a slider (do strict rules make people safer?), and the student says why. Watch: True or lie? and Which word did you hear?, each line with a ▶ time: tap it and the video jumps to that moment. After: two taps (why do they nail the windows? what happens when they drive away?), then the student says the rule. V: five frames on the film strip. VI, the sheriff’s notice: a notice nailed on the door. The student reads it, taps the verbs in the present, and finishes the rule (goes / will go, gets / get, open / opened, don’t nail, happens). The rule stays in the corner on the next pages. VII, the town rules: on top, the forest horizon and the sun; below, the town’s wooden board with six rules. The student reads the clue, says the rule out loud, then taps Check: the rule is written on the board and the sun goes one step down, until night. VIII, Your Rules: three people ask (a new family, a friend, the sheriff). The student picks how to answer and the talk appears: read it out loud, you are the person. IX: one minute.

THE COMMON ERRORS

when the sun go down, everybody stay, when the sun will go down, if you opened the door, they come in, they comes, they don’t can, everybody is staying inside, if you doesn’t, if you not nail, What does happen…?

AGREE OR DISAGREE? (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the trailer, four unpopular opinions about the town, rules and fear. Your student taps a number from 1 to 5 and defends it; you take the opposite side. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Recast if / when + present quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Warm-up and The Town Rules, IV Agree or Disagree?, VII The Town Rules, VIII Your Rules and IX One Minute.
  • Listen: II The Words, III The Trailer and V Line by Line.
  • Focus: VI The Sheriff’s Notice.
  • Write: the homework (the lesson ends speaking).
