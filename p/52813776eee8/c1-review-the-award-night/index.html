The Award Night · C1 Unit 3 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 3 (Emphasis). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Chef Rosa (invented) wins Best New Restaurant and has thirty seconds to thank everyone. Her speech is full of clefts, fronting and emphatic do. Then the student transforms neutral sentences and talks about their own life, with emphasis.

About 42 min: I · Speed Round (6) · II · The Speech (9) · III · The Morning After (7) · IV · Turn On the Spotlight (8) · V · After the Gala (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Sixty seconds. Ready? Start the clock.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

it-clefts: It was … who… · what-clefts: What matters is… · It wasn’t until … that… · fronting: This I will never forget · emphatic do / did · all / the only thing / the reason why

SPEED ROUND

A 60-second speed round. Tap Start the clock, then Right or Pass for each item; the answer is on your screen. At the end of class, play again and beat the score.

THE SPEECH

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

TURN ON THE SPOTLIGHT

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

AFTER THE GALA

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

it was my mother that she taught me, the thing what I need, it wasn’t until 2019 when, all what we had, I do believed, the reason is because. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE MORNING AFTER (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after Rosa’s speech, the award changes everything: three decisions for the student to make for her (a company wants her name, a critic wants the regulars’ table, the prize money). There is no right answer: let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Listen for the Unit 3 emphasis (What matters is…, It was … who…, The reason why…, All she needs is…, I do think…); recast quietly while they talk, and at the end ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Speed Round and IV · Turn On the Spotlight and VI · I Can.
  • Listen: II · The Speech.
  • Out loud: III · The Morning After and V · After the Gala.
