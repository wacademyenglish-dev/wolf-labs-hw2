Mock Test 2 and the Plan · DET Prep · Lesson 16 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the last lesson of the course: a second full mock test with new, original content (including the photo tasks and the speaking sample), then the student writes a plan for the days before the test.

The story: No story: a full practice test (a call to a housing office, a text about multitasking, two photos, a paragraph, two recorded questions, the speaking sample), then the test-day plan.

About 60 min: I · Read and Select (3) · II · Fill in and Complete (7) · III · Listen and Type (5) · IV · Listening and Reading (7) · V · Speaking and Writing (22) · VI · Your Test-Day Plan (6) · VII · The Hot Seat (6) · VIII · Ready (4).

Open with: Bring back the list from Mock Test 1: “Last time, the part to fix was… Let’s see today.”

Compare with Mock Test 1 part by part, using the checklists, not a number: do not promise a score. Before the student books, open the official website together and read the current rules (ID, camera, microphone, room). Formats and rules change.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

every question type · the real clock · answer, reason, example, wrap-up · the speaking sample · the test-day plan · check the official rules

READ AND SELECT

Read and Select: the words stay blurred until you tap Start the clock. The student taps only the REAL English words. At the end: green = found, dashed amber = missed, red = a fake word tapped (that costs points in the test).

FILL IN AND COMPLETE

The gaps: the student types only the missing letters (the box moves to the next gap by itself). With a clock, the text stays blurred until you start it. Check paints right and wrong and shows the full word. Spelling counts.

LISTEN AND TYPE

Listen and Type: the audio plays up to 3 times, like the test. The student types, then Check compares word by word: green is right, red is missing or misspelled. Read the tip out loud.

LISTENING AND READING

Listen or read, then choose one option. The colors show right and wrong, and one line says why. Ask the student WHY before you move on.

SPEAKING AND WRITING

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

YOUR TEST-DAY PLAN

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

READY

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Congratulate the student: the course is complete. Agree on the booking date together.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

the same mistakes as Mock Test 1, panic with the clock, stopping speaking early, booking without reading the rules. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.

THE HOT SEAT (VII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

The mock is never interrupted. After the test-day plan, the student closes the course as themselves, a future student; you play Mr. Hayes from the admissions office of their dream university and ask four questions (why abroad, the hardest part of the test, one thing to change in their English, a low score). Your secret rule is on your screen only: if an answer is shorter than 20 seconds, ask for an example. Wait five seconds in silence before they start. Use the PUSH line when the answer sounds memorized. Recast quietly while they talk, and push the shape of the course (answer, reason, example, wrap-up); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. In a mock test, use it only between parts, never during a task.
