The Press Office · C1 Unit 8 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 8 (Explain and report). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student runs the press office of Northlight Energy (invented) on a crisis day. You are three directors: IT (an attempted data access), Finance (a rumour about profits) and HR (a new Friday policy). The student writes careful statement lines.

About 47 min: I · Speed Round (6) · II · Three Directors (15) · III · The Story in Order (7) · IV · The Coffee Break (7) · V · Off the Record (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Sixty seconds. Ready? Start the clock.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

analogies: it’s a bit like, think of it as · participle clauses: having reviewed · it has been suggested / agreed that · reportedly, allegedly, according to · in other words, what I mean is · if so, if not, not that I know of

SPEED ROUND

A 60-second speed round. Tap Start the clock, then Right or Pass for each item; the answer is on your screen. At the end of class, play again and beat the score.

THREE DIRECTORS

You are each director. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then the student writes one careful line for the press statement. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

THE STORY IN ORDER

The events are mixed. The student taps them in order; wrong order shows in red. When it’s right, the student tells the whole story with first, then, after that, finally.

OFF THE RECORD

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

according to me, data was stolen (not confirmed), if yes, call us, it was suggested to, it’s a bit like as, having review. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE COFFEE BREAK (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the crisis story is in order, your student takes a coffee break with you, off the record: four unpopular opinions about news and the truth. They tap a number from 1 to 5 and defend it with the Unit 8 tools (according to, reportedly / allegedly, it has been suggested that, in other words, it’s a bit like). There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, take the opposite number, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Recast quietly while they talk, and at the end ask for one or two self-corrections.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Speed Round and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · Three Directors and III · The Story in Order, IV · The Coffee Break and V · Off the Record.
