So Strange Was It · C1 Unit 4 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on so / such + inversion: So bright was the light that…; So quickly did it happen that…; Such was the noise that…, compared with the normal forms (so… that, such a… that).

The story: The Valley Gazette (invented local paper): a blue light over Pine Forest, a farmer’s scared dogs, a circle of burnt grass, and a disappointing explanation: a weather balloon.

About 42 min: I · The Gazette (10) · II · Normal or Dramatic? (9) · III · The Interview (8) · IV · The Mystery Board (15).

Open with: “Is there a strange story or legend in your town or family?”

Students know “so… that”. Today the intensity moves to the front. Read the article with a dramatic voice. Three patterns: So + adjective + be + subject; So + adverb + did + subject + verb; Such + be + the noun. The bingo practises the form fast; the board lets the student tell real strange stories.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

So + adjective + be + subject + that · So + adverb + did + subject + verb + that · Such + be + the noun + that · the normal way: so… that / such a… that · result after that · where it lives: stories, news, speeches

THE GAZETTE

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

NORMAL OR DRAMATIC?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

THE MYSTERY BOARD

A board game around the table. Tap Roll the die: the token moves and the square shows a task that mixes the grammar of the unit. The student does it out loud; tap Done and roll again until the end of the board.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

So strange the light was that, So fast it happened that, Such the noise was that, Such was a noise that, So quickly happened it that, So bright the light was. C1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE INTERVIEW (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the rule, your student is Farmer Joe Lane and you are a TV reporter from the city. Ask the four questions in character; the student answers with drama (So bright was the light that…, Such was the noise that…). Your secret plan (only you see it) is to make him say “aliens” on camera. Wait five seconds in silence before each answer, and use the PUSH line for a follow-up he can’t prepare for. At the end, the student chooses Joe’s last line on camera. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · The Gazette.
  • Focus: II · Normal or Dramatic?.
  • Out loud: III · The Interview and IV · The Mystery Board.
