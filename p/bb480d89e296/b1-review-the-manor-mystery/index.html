The Manor Mystery · B1 Unit 6 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 6 (Rules, abilities and guesses). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: A weekend at old Ashworth Manor (invented). At 2 AM the famous blue vase fell and broke. You are three guests: the cook, the nephew and the old aunt. The answer: the aunt opened the window (guests mustn’t), and the wind broke the vase.

About 50 min: I · The Die (8) · II · The Blue Vase (15) · III · The Arena (7) · IV · Memory (8) · V · By the Fire (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Roll the die. The number chooses the challenge.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

must, have to, mustn’t, don’t have to · should for advice · be supposed to, be expected to · could, was able to, managed to · must be, might be, can’t be · must have, might have, can’t have

THE DIE

Tap Roll the die: the number chooses one of the six topics (no repeats until all six are done). The student does the challenge from memory.

THE BLUE VASE

You are each guest. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then the whole house listens: the student says what each guest can’t have / must have done. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

MEMORY

A memory game. The student opens two cards; when they match, the student makes a sentence with the pair (example under Show answer).

BY THE FIRE

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

you mustn’t (for don’t have to), I could finish it last night (one success: managed to), he must broke it, it mustn’t be (for can’t be), you should to sleep, we are suppose to. B1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE ARENA (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the mystery is solved, one argument: the aunt opened the window, but nobody fixed the old windows. Who has to pay for the vase? The student picks a side and defends it for one minute with must have, have to and supposed to; then they swap and defend the other side. Wait five seconds in silence before they start, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it harder. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About 7 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Die and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Blue Vase, III · The Arena and V · By the Fire.
  • Listen: IV · Memory.
