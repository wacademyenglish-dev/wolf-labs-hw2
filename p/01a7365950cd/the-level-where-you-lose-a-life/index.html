The Level Where You Lose a Life · Jumanji: Open World · The Board

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: A book, a board, a video game, and now the real world.

The grammar: Who, Which, Where: A person who, a thing which, a place where.

Short class (about 50 min): I Warm-up and The Game Rules · II The Words · III The Trailer · IV Line by Line · V The Danger Light · VI Who, Which, Where · VII The Board · IX The Last Square. Skip if you’re short on time: VIII (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 57 min.

Open with: “You know the game now. In the trailer, what is the place where the game comes out? Guess.”

Jumanji began as a book (1981) and a film with Robin Williams (1995); in the new films the game is a video game, and Spencer and his friends play as its heroes (Dr. Bravestone is Dwayne Johnson). In Jumanji: Open World (Sony, 2026) the game breaks: the characters and the animals come out into a real town, and the town becomes a map of levels. The grammar: relative clauses: a person who, a thing which / that, a place where (+ a full sentence); never what as a relative.

A note for the teacher: the trailer has game animals running in a real town and some chases, but nothing violent is on the lesson screens. The lesson plays the whole trailer (3:06). The times follow its captions (25/09), and each moment is told in our own words.

Warm-up and The Game Rules (I): two parts. 1 · Warm-up: three questions about the student’s life, one at a time (big on the screen, with “Ask more” under each and one short line about the film under the photo). Let them talk; don’t correct the grammar yet. 2 · The Game Rules: four rules of the game appear one at a time (you go into the game, you become an avatar, you have three lives, the animals come out). For each, the student taps Yes / No way / Only if… and says why. A2: help with who / which / where (“a game which…”, “a place where…”).

The words (II): shoulder, reputation, problem, stuck and uncomfortable, before the video, in match the columns and hot seat. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (IV) lists the lines with the yellow words: tap a line, hear it again, and the student says it like the character.

The ending (IX, The Last Square): you pick A or B for the student. A: the student is the host of Jumanji and explains the game to you, a new player. B: they describe their own game (the hero, the place, the thing you need) and you guess. About three minutes of speaking, then one minute of writing: one new word, one sentence, one thing that was hard. It goes to My notes.

ON SCREEN

The cover is a quiet street at night covered by jungle vines. I: Warm-up and The Game Rules. III: the trailer in three steps. Before: four cards to open and answer out loud. Watch: What happens? (tap the answer) and True or lie?, each line with a ▶ time: tap it and the video jumps to that moment. After: Guess it: four clues with who, which and where, one at a time; the student taps “I know it!”. IV: five frames on the film strip. V, The Danger Light: the game is in the student’s town; for three things from the trailer they choose green, yellow or red and say why with who / which / where. VI: match six things to their clues (the boy who plays, the drum which calls…); when all six are right, the rule appears. VII, The Board: a carved wooden board with a Start, six round squares in a snake and Jumanji! at the end. Two rounds of three sentences, each with one wrong word: the student finds it, says the fix out loud and taps Check. The right words are written on the squares and the green gem jumps ahead. VIII, Your Riddles: spin for a topic, describe it for 45 seconds without the name, and count how many riddles the teacher guesses. IX, The Last Square: the teacher picks A (the student is the host of Jumanji) or B (their own game); three minutes of talking; then a one-minute note that goes to My notes.

THE COMMON ERRORS

the boy which plays, the drum who calls, the stone what saves, the level which you lose a life (where), the place who…, the hero who never give up, where escape the animals, where the animals escapes.

THE DANGER LIGHT (V) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after Line by Line, the game is in the student’s own town. Three things from the trailer: the mall (the level where you lose a life), a game character who says only one line and wants a job, and a jaguar which is sleeping in the garden. For each, the student chooses green, yellow or red (or says their own idea) and says why with who / which / where. There is no right answer: wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to bring it to their own life. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk (which for a person, what for a thing); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About six minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Warm-up and The Game Rules, V The Danger Light, VII The Board, VIII Your Riddles and IX The Last Square.
  • Listen: II The Words, III The Trailer and IV Line by Line.
  • Focus: VI Who, Which, Where.
  • Write: the one-minute note at the end of IX, and the homework.
