It’s Cheaper Than a New Team · Selling Services · Lesson 12

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 12 (comparatives: cheaper, more efficient, better, than). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Page 2 of a Bluepeak proposal compares a new in-house payroll team with outsourcing. Then Rachel Moore, HR director of Harbor Paper (fictional), says “Why not a new team?” and Leo compares. The student writes a three-sentence comparison for a real client.

About 57 min: I · Your Week (6) · II · The Proposal Page (8) · III · “Why Not a New Team?” (8) · IV · Cheaper, Not “More Cheap” (10) · V · Which Word? (7) · VI · You Choose (6) · VII · Your Comparison (9) · VIII · I Can (3).

Open with: “Is this week busier than last week? What is faster for you: e-mail or WhatsApp?”

Portuguese uses “mais” for everything, so Brazilians say “more cheap”, “more better” and “cheaper that” (que → that). The rule fits on one line: short word → -er, long word → more, good → better, bad → worse, and always than. In the comparison, push for honesty: the reading itself says in-house can be easier for a small team. A seller who admits one weak point sounds more credible.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

It’s cheaper / faster / easier than … · It’s more efficient / more expensive than … · It’s better · It’s worse · about 30% less per year

YOUR WEEK

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

THE PROPOSAL PAGE

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

“WHY NOT A NEW TEAM?”

A short audio dialogue (American voices). BEFORE: one question. LISTEN: play it once without stopping (0.75× is fine for A1), then the questions; Show answer after the student tries. The transcript opens only after the audio ends: read it together and point to the highlighted words.

CHEAPER, NOT “MORE CHEAP”

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

WHICH WORD?

Listen or read, then choose one option. The colors show right and wrong, and one line says why. Ask the student WHY before you move on.

YOU CHOOSE

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

YOUR COMPARISON

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. If the student has a real proposal, use his comparison as the text of the proposal page.

THE TECH WORDS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno vende serviços B2B. Você não precisa ser especialista; precisa saber do que ele está falando. Não ensine estes termos: deixe que ele explique o trabalho dele (é conversa real).

  • BPO (Business Process Outsourcing): Terceirização de processos: a empresa contratada EXECUTA uma rotina do cliente (folha de pagamento, contabilidade, fiscal, contas a pagar). O cliente para de fazer aquilo dentro de casa.
  • BTO (Business Transformation Outsourcing): Terceirização com transformação: além de executar, a contratada MUDA o processo (tecnologia, automação, integração de sistemas). Vende-se como “melhorar”, não só “fazer por você”.
  • ERP: Sistema de gestão que integra finanças, compras, estoque, RH etc. Exemplos (marcas): SAP, Oracle, TOTVS. Um BPO/BTO não vende o ERP: trabalha COM ele ou o integra.
  • In-house × outsourcing: In-house = fazer dentro de casa, com equipe própria. Outsourcing = terceirizar para um fornecedor (BPO). É a comparação central desta aula.
  • Headcount: Número de pessoas no quadro. “Five new people” = aumentar o headcount, algo que muitos CFOs não podem aprovar.
  • Automation (RPA): Automação de tarefas repetitivas (robôs de software que digitam, conferem, lançam). Um argumento de eficiência do BTO.
  • Accuracy (more accurate): Precisão / exatidão: relatórios sem erro. “More accurate than spreadsheets” = mais confiáveis que planilhas.
  • Fixed cost × variable cost: Custo fixo (salários da equipe, todo mês) × custo variável (paga pelo volume). Terceirizar costuma transformar custo fixo em variável.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

It’s more cheap (It’s cheaper), It’s more better (It’s better), cheaper that a new team (cheaper than), It’s efficienter (It’s more efficient). A1+: one correction per answer is enough.
