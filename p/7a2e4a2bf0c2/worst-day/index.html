The Worst Day · Domino of Disasters

Marcos had the worst morning of his life: his phone died, the alarm didn’t ring, the bus left, he took a taxi with no wallet, he walked in the rain, the office was closed. Each disaster knocked over the next one. On screen, a row of black dominoes: a domino only falls with the right negative or question. And at the end: it was Sunday. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

In a no or a question, the past moves into did and the verb goes plain: he didn’t take, Did he see?, Where did he go? The verb be doesn’t use did: wasn’t. The glue: so (the result) and because (the reason). The mistakes: didn’t went, did you saw, didn’t was, he not took, where you went.

THE AUDIO

Marcos tells his own story (first person); pause and 0.75× on the player; the transcript opens after listening and was checked against the audio. The punchline plays at the end of scene III.

YOU ARE MARCOS (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Your student becomes Marcos and makes three choices, one of three each, sentence almost ready: what he did when the bus left, what he did on the closed Sunday, and what he bought on Monday. There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it a little harder. The starters keep the past, didn’t, because and so in their mouth. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: II Marcos (tap only what he really said).
  • Out loud: I Your Turn First, IV Where the Past Lives, VI Ask Marcos (you are Marcos), VII My Day Was Worse (you decide who wins), VIII You Are Marcos (three choices), IX All of It, X Your Worst Day (add a domino for every link you hear).
  • Focus: III One Thing After Another, V The Domino Row.
  • Write: XI, their worst day as a chain, with the red wavy line for didn’t went, did you saw, didn’t was, he not took and where you went. It goes to their My notes.
