First Contact · English for Healthcare · Lesson 1 of 16

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the first lesson of English for Healthcare (16 lessons: receiving the patient, examining and explaining, difficult conversations, team and career). Today: the AIDET routine for any first contact (Acknowledge, Introduce, Duration, Explanation, Thank you) and I’m going to… for the next step.

The story: A Lecturio Nursing video explains AIDET with a nurse inserting a catheter. Then a dentist’s first contact in order, actions from five professions, and the student’s own first contact.

About 41 min: I · AIDET (9) · II · At the Dentist (7) · III · I’m Going To… (9) · IV · Your First Contact (12) · V · I Can (4).

Open with: “What is your profession, and who are your foreign patients (tourists, colleagues abroad, conference contacts)?” Adapt every example to the student’s area.

One course for the whole area: the core is the same (receive, ask, explain, difficult conversations), and each lesson has examples from different professions. It is not a licensing exam course (OET, NCLEX). Always let the student choose examples from their own profession.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

Good morning, Mr./Ms. … · I’m …, your … today. · This will take about… · I’m going to… · You may feel… · Thank you for your time.

AIDET

Lecturio Nursing, “AIDET for Patient Communication” (4 min, captions checked on 15/10/2026). American English, clear and slow.

AT THE DENTIST

The events are mixed. The student taps them in order; wrong order shows in red. When it’s right, the student tells the whole story with first, then, after that, finally.

I’M GOING TO…

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

YOUR FIRST CONTACT

The task with the real clock of the test. Read the task together, tap Start: the prep time runs, then the answer time starts by itself. Do not help while the clock runs. When it stops, go through the checklist together (tap what was there), then Show answer for the model. Try again is a good idea: the second try is always better.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to write their AIDET for the most common procedure of their job: it becomes their script.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I will make an exam (I’m going to examine you), It will take ten minutes more or less (about ten minutes), Relax! (Try to relax for me), forgetting to say your role. B1+: one correction per answer is enough.
