There Is · The Front Seat

A street, and then four real towns: Brighton, Petworth, London and Cambridge. The idea of the hour: an English sentence can’t start with an empty front, so English hires a word that means nothing (there) just to sit there. On screen, every sentence is a row of cinema seats, and the front seat is empty until the right word sits in it. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

There is (one) / there are (more): the front never changes, so the number is at the back. There isn’t a… / there aren’t any… To ask, swap the two words: Is there…? Are there any…? The mistakes: In my town has a park, Is a park near here (empty front), there is two, There is a station? as a question, aren’t no.

THE AUDIO

Real answers from four towns (pause and 0.75×; the transcript after listening; checked against the audio). Note: Cambridge says two universities, lots of churches, no water park.

ONE NEW THING (VIII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the four towns, your student’s own town gets one new thing: a beach, a big park or a new cinema. They put the three in order. There is no right answer. Say your own order first, then let them tap 1, 2, 3, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it). The starters keep there isn’t a … in my town in their mouth. Then show your order and agree on one top 3. Recast quietly while they talk (have a beach → there is a beach); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: II Five Sentences, V Six Things in Brighton (ears only), VI Four Towns.
  • Out loud: I Your Street, VIII One New Thing (three things in order, with there isn’t), IX You Decide (both halves: there is and there isn’t), X Your Town (only true sentences count).
  • Focus: III The Front Seat, IV The Back of the Sentence (cover the front), VII Is There…?.
  • Write: XI, their own town, with the red wavy line for has / have for a place, an empty front, there is two, there are a, There is…? and aren’t no. It goes to their My notes.
