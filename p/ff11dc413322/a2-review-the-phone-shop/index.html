The Phone Shop · A2 Unit 5 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 5 (Comparing and describing). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Your student works in a phone shop (the phones are invented: Nova 2, Nova 5, Pebble). You are three customers: a photographer, a grandfather and a student.

About 47 min: I · The Ladder (8) · II · The Phone Shop (14) · III · One Week Later (6) · IV · Find the Mistakes (7) · V · The Hot Seat (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Climb the ladder. Step one is easy.”

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

comparatives · superlatives · as … as, the same as, different from · -ed × -ing adjectives · adverbs of manner · adjective + preposition + -ing

THE LADDER

Six steps from easy to hard. A good answer: tap Go up. Not yet: Try again on the same step.

THE PHONE SHOP

You are each customer. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then the student recommends a phone, comparing. The best match: photographer → Nova 5, grandfather → Pebble, student → Nova 2. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

more fast, the most cheap, as good than, I’m boring (for bored), it works good, good in cook. A2: one correction per answer is enough.

ONE WEEK LATER (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the shop, your student bets on what happened to each customer one week later. Let them choose and say why with comparatives, as … as and -ed / -ing; wait five seconds in silence, use the PUSH line (only you see it), and only then tap “Check what happened”. Being wrong is part of the fun. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Ladder, IV · Find the Mistakes and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Phone Shop, III · One Week Later and V · The Hot Seat.
