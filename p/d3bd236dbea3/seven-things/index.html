Seven Things the House Can Do · The Smart House

The owner is away for a week. The smart house runs on its own and writes down everything it does. On screen: a control panel with seven functions. Every can lights a function; every can’t turns one red. At the end, the night log shows one thing the house can’t do, so somebody was inside. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

can / can’t + the plain verb, one form for everyone: he can, never he cans; can swim, never can to swim. Questions and short answers with no do: Can you…? Yes, I can. / No, I can’t. One word, three jobs: ability, permission, possibility. In speech, can is short and weak (/kən/); can’t is long and strong.

THE AUDIO

Every line has pause and 0.75×, and the transcript opens only after listening. All transcripts were checked against the audio. The four questions in scene XI are the house asking the student about their own life (What can you do that most people cannot? and three more).

YOUR SMART HOUSE (VII) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Three things the smart house can do in your student’s home: open the door for the neighbour, play loud music at seven, talk to the cat. Two choices each, then the sentence with can or can’t. This keeps the permission job of can alive before the night log. There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make it a little harder. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I You First, II Can You?, III The Panel, VII Your Smart House (a choice, then can / can’t), IX She Has a Key, X Sell Me the House.
  • Listen: V No Hands, VI Hey, House (the student wakes the house out loud, asks, then hears the answer), XI Lights Off.
  • Focus: IV It Never Changes, VIII The Night Log. Don’t give the mystery away: let the red list from scene V do the work.
  • Write: XI, their own house, with the red wavy line for cans, can to, don’t can, Do you can and can drives. It goes to their My notes.

THE SALE

Round 1: sell the house with energy; the student asks with Can it…? Round 2: swap. Tap the ideas you use and the parts you hear. Strong students: the customer must find one thing the house can’t do.
