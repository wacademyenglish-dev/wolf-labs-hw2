The Lost Wallet · A1 Unit 10 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 10 (Yesterday and tomorrow). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: The last review of Book A1. Your student is the manager of a café; a black wallet was on a table last night. You are three customers: Ms. Lee, Mr. Costa and Dani. Then you are the owner of the wallet.

About 41 min: I · The Die (8) · II · The Lost Wallet (14) · III · The Reward (7) · IV · Small Talk (6) · V · I Can (3) · VI · The Next Book (3).

Open with: “Pick a door.” There is no order. One minute per door, no notes.

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

was / were · there was / there were · past simple: -ed and irregulars · didn’t + verb, Did…? · going to for plans · still / already / not … yet

THE DIE

Tap Roll the die: the number chooses one of the six topics (no repeats until all six are done). The student does the challenge from memory.

THE LOST WALLET

You are each customer. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Only Dani saw the wallet. Then you are the owner on the phone: the student tells you what each customer said, in the past, and the plans for tomorrow. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

SMALL TALK

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. This is the last review of Book A1. After I Can there is one more short scene, VI The Next Book: three cards with what Book A2 brings. Read them together and ask which one the student wants first.

THE NEXT BOOK

The last scene of the book: three cards with what the next book brings. Read them together and ask which one the student wants first.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

she didn’t saw, they was, I going to, goed / eated, had a garden (for there was), did you went?. A1: one correction per answer is enough.

THE REWARD (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

The wallet is home, and now the student decides: what to do with a reward, what to do with a wallet in the street, and one thing they lost last year. There is no right answer. Answer first, short, so they hear the sentence; then wait five seconds in silence and use the PUSH line (only you see it): a yes / no question. Recast mistakes quietly (“I’m going to” for “I going to”, “didn’t see” for “didn’t saw”); at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one light personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · The Die and V · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · The Lost Wallet, III · The Reward and IV · Small Talk.
  • Listen: VI · The Next Book.
