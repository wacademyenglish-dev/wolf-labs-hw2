Who Doesn’t Like Baseball? · Superman & Lois · The Kent Fridge

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: From a lost planet to a family farm.

The grammar: Love It, Hate It: Like, love, hate + -ing. He likes, she doesn’t like.

The class (about 55 min): I The Family · II The Words · III The Trailer · IV Would You Rather? · V Line by Line · VI Love It, Hate It · VII The Kent Fridge · VIII Love It or Hate It? · IX Your Fridge Note. No extra scenes: every scene is part of the class.

Open with: “You know the twins now. In the trailer, who likes the farm, and who hates it? Guess.”

Superman & Lois (2021–2024) is the family series with Tyler Hoechlin as Clark Kent and Elizabeth Tulloch as Lois Lane. They move from the big city to the Kent farm in Smallville, Kansas, with their twin boys, Jonathan and Jordan, two very different fourteen-year-olds. The grammar: like / love / hate + -ing (I love playing, they hate driving), he / she likes with -s, doesn’t like, and questions with Do you like…? / Does he like…?

A note for the teacher: the trailer has no captions on YouTube now, so the five frames on the film strip are estimated, and each moment is told in our own words. A1: present simple only.

Warm-up and The Interview (I): two parts. 1 · Warm-up: three questions about the student’s life, one at a time (big on the screen, with “Ask more” under each and one short line about the show under the photo). A1: short answers are fine. 2 · The Interview: you are Clark Kent: the card with his facts shows only on your screen (dashed border). The student asks questions (the chips help: Do you like…? What do you love…?) and taps each one they ask; answer in short A1 sentences, in character. Then the student guesses who you are, and the photo shows the answer.

The words (II): stories, family, boys, world and needs, before the video, in match the columns and hot seat. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (V) lists the lines with the yellow words: tap a line, hear it again, and the student says it like the character.

ON SCREEN

The cover is the Kent farm at night. I: the family (the show, the Kents, the farm). III: the trailer in three steps. Before: who is this? Clues one by one (he loves helping people, he loves flying…); the student says it when they know, and “I know it!” shows the answer. Watch: True or lie? and Pick the word you hear, each line with a ▶ time: tap it and the video jumps to that moment. After: match each Kent to what he or she loves or hates (tap one on the left, then its pair on the right), then say the sentences. IV: five frames on the film strip. V, the fridge door: five sentences with one mistake each. The student says the five fixes out loud, taps Check, and the rule appears. The rule stays in the corner on the next pages. VI, The Kent Fridge: a fridge door with six notes, each with a colored magnet. The student reads who and the clue, says the sentence out loud, then taps Check: the sentence is written and the note sticks to the door. VII, Love It or Hate It?: six things (getting up early, cooking, watching sports…). The student taps love it, like it or hate it for each, then says it about themselves and a person in the family. VIII ends with writing this time: the student’s own family fridge note.

THE COMMON ERRORS

Jonathan love playing, loves play, likes to writing, Clark don’t like, doesn’t likes, You like living here?, Does you like…?, the boys likes, the boys are like playing, Who don’t like baseball?

WOULD YOU RATHER? (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the trailer: three quick choices from the show, two options each. Flying or super speed, keep a secret or tell it (the boys find out their father’s secret), watch a baseball game or play it. Each option has an almost-ready sentence with love / like / hate + -ing. There is no right answer. Answer first yourself, then wait five seconds in silence and use the PUSH line (only you see it). Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one short personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I The Family, IV Would You Rather?, VII The Kent Fridge and VIII Love It or Hate It?.
  • Listen: II The Words, III The Trailer and V Line by Line.
  • Focus: VI Love It, Hate It.
  • Write: IX Your Fridge Note.
