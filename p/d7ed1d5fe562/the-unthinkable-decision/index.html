The Unthinkable Decision · Avengers: Doomsday · The War Table

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: Eighteen years of heroes, and the one threat they may not be able to stop.

The grammar: May Not Be Able To: We may not be able to stop it. It is far stronger than us.

The class (about 45 min): I Warm-up and Pick a Side · III The Trailer · V The Unthinkable Decision · VI May Not Be Able To · VII The War Table · IX The Hot Seat. II The Words and IV Line by Line are short listening scenes. VIII Weigh It is EXTRA: skip it if you are short on time.

Open with: “You know the history now. In the trailer, why may the heroes not be able to win? Guess.”

Avengers: Doomsday (2026), Marvel Studios, with Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom. Something is coming that the heroes may not be able to stop; the last warriors who faced something like it were far stronger than all of them, and they died. Everything will be for nothing if they don’t stand together. The grammar: may not be able to + verb, far / much + comparative (far stronger, far less), and the consequence: it will be for nothing if we don’t…, unless…, …otherwise…

A note for the teacher: superhero action, no blood. The times come from the trailer’s captions (07/10), told in our own words.

Warm-up and Pick a Side (I): two parts. 1 · Warm-up: three questions about the student’s life, one at a time (big on the screen, with “Ask more” under each and one short line about the film under the photo). Let them talk; don’t correct the grammar yet. 2 · Pick a Side: the heroes are divided: attack Doom now, or wait and prepare? The student picks a plan and defends it for thirty seconds. Then tap “The teacher pushes back” and let them answer. Twice. Push for may not be able to, far + comparative, unless / otherwise.

The words (II): unthinkable, threats, sacrifice, warriors and miracle, before the video, in hear it in the video and turn the cards. Five minutes: let the student guess first. In the video (with CC on), these words turn yellow; the explanation comes after the video, in Line by Line. Line by Line (IV) lists the lines with the yellow words: tap a line, hear it again, and the student says it like the character.

The ending (IX, The Hot Seat): the student leads the war room. The teacher asks five hard questions (can we win, how strong is the enemy, what if the team splits, what we need, another way). Then swap. Speaking only.

ON SCREEN

The cover is a dark war room with a holographic table. I: Warm-up and Pick a Side. III: the trailer in three steps. Before: three clues, one by one, and the student guesses who is behind it (tap “I know it!”). Watch: four lines from the trailer, each with a ▶ time: tap it and the video jumps to that moment. The student taps True or Lie and, if it is a lie, says the truth. After: three taps (what happened to the old warriors? what must they do? what do they need?), then the student says the answer. IV: five frames on the film strip. V, The Unthinkable Decision: three decisions, each harder (two cities, a friend or a city, Doom’s deal), then one minute to convince the team. VI, May Not Be Able To: a note left on the war table. The student reads it out loud and taps the six key phrases (may not be able to, far stronger, far less, if we don’t, unless, otherwise); then the rule on the right is finished with the student: what each phrase means. VII, The War Table: a world map in points of light on the round table, six regions. The student matches each half sentence (“Doom is far stronger …” to “… than all of us”); every right pair lights a region in alert green with its name. The rule stays in the corner. At the end the whole map is lit. Then the student says each whole sentence. VIII, Weigh It (EXTRA): a ruler (how ready is your team?), and then the plan against Doom: the student tells it out loud and taps each of the six phrases when using it. IX: the hot seat.

THE COMMON ERRORS

may not to be able, may not can, maybe not able, be able come, very stronger, far more strong, more far harder, far fewer scary, if we won’t, unless we will / unless we don’t, will must, must to.

THE UNTHINKABLE DECISION (V) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after the video, the trailer’s “unthinkable decision” becomes the student’s: two cities, then a friend or a city, then Doom’s deal. There is no right answer. Let them choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to make the choice harder. Recast mistakes quietly while they talk (may not be able to, far + comparative, unless / otherwise); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. The last word is one minute to convince the team, then you speak for the team. VIII Weigh It is now EXTRA: skip it if you are short on time.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Warm-up and Pick a Side, V The Unthinkable Decision, VII The War Table, VIII Weigh It (EXTRA) and IX The Hot Seat.
  • Listen: II The Words, III The Trailer and IV Line by Line.
  • Focus: VI May Not Be Able To.
  • Write: the homework (the lesson ends speaking).
