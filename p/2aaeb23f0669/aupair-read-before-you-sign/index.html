Read Before You Sign · Au Pair · Lesson 7 of 24

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: reading the written agreement with the host family (the program requires a signed agreement on childcare duties before departure). Key language: up to, per week, no more than, must / must not, curfew. The student separates lines that are OK from lines to ask about.

The story: The Miller family’s agreement (fictional), then ten lines from different agreements, then the student asks the host mom (you) about the unclear lines.

About 36 min: I · The Agreement (10) · II · OK or Ask First? (12) · III · Ask Before You Sign (10) · IV · I Can (4).

Open with: “Would you sign a contract in English without reading it?” Then: “Today you learn to read it.”

Program rules checked on 15/10/2026 in the federal regulation (22 CFR 62.31) and on j1visa.state.gov: up to 10 hours per day and 45 per week; one and a half days off per week; one complete weekend off per month; two weeks of paid vacation; up to $500 from the family for classes. Extra pay does NOT allow extra hours. The passport belongs to the au pair. When something is unclear, the answer is: ask the coordinator before signing.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

up to 45 hours per week · no more than 10 hours per day · must / must not · curfew · Could you explain…? · I’d like to keep my passport with me.

THE AGREEMENT

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

OK OR ASK FIRST?

One situation at a time. The student says which side it goes on and WHY, then taps. The color confirms and one line explains. The two columns build a board the student can photograph at the end.

ASK BEFORE YOU SIGN

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Tell the student: in a real agreement, any line from the “ask first” column goes to the coordinator before she signs.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

signing without reading, accepting more than 45 hours for extra pay, giving the passport to someone, being afraid to ask. B1: one correction per answer is enough.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · The Agreement.
  • Focus: II · OK or Ask First? and IV · I Can.
  • Out loud: III · Ask Before You Sign.
