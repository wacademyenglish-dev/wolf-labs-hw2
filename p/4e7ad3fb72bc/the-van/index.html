The Van · Let’s, Shall We?

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: A van stops on a narrow street at six in the evening, and two people have to empty it before dark.

The grammar: The First Box: You and me, now.

Short class (about 43 min): I Before It Arrives · II The First Box · III Let’s Not · IV Six More · V Shall We? · VI Two Kinds of Plan · X Let’s. Skip if you’re short on time: VII, VIII, IX (marked EXTRA on your screen). Full lesson: about 64 min.

Open with: “We have one hour and a full van. What do we do first?”

A van stops on a narrow street at six in the evening, and two people have to empty it before dark. On screen, the open van with its load and a floor plan of the house: every right sentence moves one thing into its room. Every decision has to be said out loud to the other person. No timer, no points.

THE GRAMMAR

Let’s + the plain verb: an invitation (you and me, now) that waits for an answer. Let’s not for a no. Shall we…? to ask instead of decide. And the trap: an invitation is not a plan already decided (we’re going to). The mistakes: let’s to go, let’s don’t, we go eat, we will as an invitation, shall us. Short answers: Yes, let’s. / No, let’s not.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Before It Arrives (nobody is corrected), VII On the Sidewalk (swap sides, then cover the lines), VIII Yes, Let’s (say no once), IX Your Room (a real room; if it isn’t true, it doesn’t count).
  • Focus: II and IV The Van (ten things into four rooms), III Let’s Not, V Shall We? (the question mark is the clue), VI Two Kinds of Plan.
  • Write: X, a plan for their own room, with the red wavy line for let’s to, let’s don’t, let’s going, shall us, we go eat and lets. It goes to their My notes.
