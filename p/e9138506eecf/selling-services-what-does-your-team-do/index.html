What Does Your Team Do? · Selling Services · Lesson 6

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 6 (present simple: he works · she doesn’t · does it…?). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Rachel Kim, finance manager at Atlas Motors (fictional), sends Leo an e-mail about her team and then explains on a call who does what: payroll, invoices, the close, tax. The student draws the process map of a real client (or Atlas) and explains it.

About 56 min: I · Your Week (6) · II · The Client’s E-mail (8) · III · Who Does What? (8) · IV · She Works, He Doesn’t (10) · V · I → He, She, It (7) · VI · You Choose (5) · VII · Your Process Map (9) · VIII · I Can (3).

Open with: “What does your manager do on Mondays? Who sends the proposals in your team?”

Brazilians drop the third-person s (“she work”) and use “don’t” for everybody (“he don’t send”, “the team don’t use”). In American English “the team / the company / the client” is IT: doesn’t. Teach the “one s only” rule: Does he pay…? (not “does he pays”). In the process map, ask follow-up questions as a curious seller: “Does she use the ERP? What does he do on Fridays?”

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

… does payroll. She works with … · He doesn’t use … · The team doesn’t … · Does she …? What does he do? · It takes … days.

YOUR WEEK

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

THE CLIENT’S E-MAIL

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

WHO DOES WHAT?

A short audio dialogue (American voices). BEFORE: one question. LISTEN: play it once without stopping (0.75× is fine for A1), then the questions; Show answer after the student tries. The transcript opens only after the audio ends: read it together and point to the highlighted words.

SHE WORKS, HE DOESN’T

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

I → HE, SHE, IT

Use: the student reads a sentence and the clue, and says it the new way. Show the answer after the attempt; accept natural variations. Tap Right for each good one.

YOU CHOOSE

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

YOUR PROCESS MAP

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Ask the student to send the process map (in English) to his manager or keep it for the next real client call.

THE TECH WORDS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno vende serviços B2B. Você não precisa ser especialista; precisa saber do que ele está falando. Não ensine estes termos: deixe que ele explique o trabalho dele (é conversa real).

  • BPO (Business Process Outsourcing): Terceirização de processos: a empresa contratada EXECUTA uma rotina do cliente (folha de pagamento, contabilidade, fiscal, contas a pagar). O cliente para de fazer aquilo dentro de casa.
  • BTO (Business Transformation Outsourcing): Terceirização com transformação: além de executar, a contratada MUDA o processo (tecnologia, automação, integração de sistemas). Vende-se como “melhorar”, não só “fazer por você”.
  • ERP: Sistema de gestão que integra finanças, compras, estoque, RH etc. Exemplos (marcas): SAP, Oracle, TOTVS. Um BPO/BTO não vende o ERP: trabalha COM ele ou o integra.
  • Process map: Mapa do processo: quem faz o quê, com qual sistema e em quanto tempo. É a base do diagnóstico antes da proposta de BPO.
  • Invoice · supplier: Fatura / nota fiscal · fornecedor. “He receives the invoices and pays the suppliers” = rotina de contas a pagar.
  • Accountant: Contador(a). Quem fecha a contabilidade do mês.
  • Close the books (month-end close): Fechar a contabilidade do mês. “It takes ten days” = o fechamento demora dez dias (lento; o ideal costuma ser 3–5).
  • Payroll module: Módulo de folha de pagamento dentro do ERP. Sem ele, a folha vai para planilha.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

She work in tax (She works in tax), He don’t send the reports (He doesn’t send the reports), The team don’t use the ERP (The team doesn’t use the ERP), Does he pays the suppliers? (Does he pay the suppliers?). A1+: one correction per answer is enough.
