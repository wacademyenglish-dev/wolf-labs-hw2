The Week Fills Up · The Planner Wall

The present continuous is the form of now, and English lends it to the future: I’m working on Saturday. The day at the end turns now into an arrangement. Your student fills a week with four slots and six invitations, turns two people down without the word no, and finds the one free hour in your calendar and theirs. Decisions made this second take I’ll; guesses take will.

WHO AGREED?

When your student is stuck, ask: Is it already in the calendar? Who agreed to it?

SPENT IS SPENT

Scene III: an accepted invitation can’t be undone (a teacher-only button clears the week). The two invitations that lose their slot come back in scene IV.

THE INFORMATION GAP

Scene VII: your calendar is hidden. Your student asks about a slot; you tap it and answer with the continuous: On Monday at one I’m teaching. Only Thursday at 1 PM is free in both calendars.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I meet my doctor tomorrow, I will see him on Friday for something booked, What do you do tomorrow?, and It’s raining tomorrow.

WHAT GOES FIRST? (VI) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

A ranking. Saturday is full, and a friend calls with two free concert tickets for tonight: your student puts five plans in order, from the first thing they cancel to the last. There is no right order. Wait five seconds in silence while they think, let them explain the top and the bottom, then use the PUSH line (only you see it). Open your own order and agree on one top 3 together. Listen for the arrangement (I’m having lunch with…) and for decisions made right now (OK, I’ll call…); recast quietly, and at the end ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I Your Week Already, III Six Invitations, IV The Refusals, VI What Goes First?, VII Two Calendars, VIII Six About Your Week.
  • Focus: II Now or Already?
  • Listen: V The Day Comes Last (the transcript opens after they listen).
  • Write: IX, their week. It goes to their My notes.
