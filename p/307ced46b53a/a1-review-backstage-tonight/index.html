Backstage Tonight · A1 Unit 8 Review

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 8 (I can, right now). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: It’s the night of the talent show and your student runs the backstage. You are three nervous performers: Leo, Priya and Tom. Then you are the director on the radio.

About 44 min: I · Speed Round (6) · II · Backstage (15) · III · Backstage Choices (5) · IV · Your Act (6) · V · The Hot Seat (8) · VI · I Can (4).

Open with: “Pick a door.” There is no order. One minute per door, no notes.

A review is not a new lesson. Do not explain the grammar again: let the student try first. Mistakes here are useful: they show you what to review. Every review of the course uses a different mix of games, so the student never does the same thing twice.

THE TOPICS OF THIS UNIT

can / can’t · Can you…? + short answers · polite requests (Could I have…? I’d like…) · present continuous · the -ing spelling · what you do × what you are doing

SPEED ROUND

A 60-second speed round. Tap Start the clock, then Right or Pass for each item; the answer is on your screen. At the end of class, play again and beat the score.

BACKSTAGE

You are each performer. Your card (only you see it) has the answers. When the student asks a question, tap it: the card fills in. If the student freezes, tap Say it easier. Then you are the director on the radio: the student tells you about one performer with can / can’t, right now + -ing and usually. Listen for the three slips on your screen and tap the one you heard.

YOUR ACT

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

he can to sing, he can sings, do you can…?, he practicing, runing / makeing, I want / give me (too direct). A1: one correction per answer is enough.

BACKSTAGE CHOICES (III) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

After the radio call, three quick backstage cases: Leo sings or dances, a phone rings, Tom plays or waits. Choose one, with can / can’t and is + -ing. Answer first yourself, as a model. Then let your student choose, wait five seconds in silence, and ask the PUSH line (only you see it): a yes / no question. Recast quietly while they talk; at the end, ask them to fix one sentence themselves. About 5 minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. There are three; use one when the review has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Focus: I · Speed Round and VI · I Can.
  • Out loud: II · Backstage, III · Backstage Choices and V · The Hot Seat.
  • Write: IV · Your Act.
