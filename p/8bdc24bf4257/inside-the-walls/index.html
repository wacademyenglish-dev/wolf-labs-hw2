Inside the Walls · Attack on Titan · The Three Walls

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

The story: From one hundred years of peace to a dream of the sea.

The grammar: The Rules of the Wall: Can, can’t, couldn’t. Be allowed to.

The class (about 56 min): I The World · II The Trailer · III Scene by Scene · IV Inside the Walls · V The Rules of the Wall · VI The Three Walls · VII Your Walls · VIII The Interview. No extra scenes: every scene is part of the class.

Open with: “Would you like to live in a safe place that you can never leave? Why or why not?”

Attack on Titan (2013–2023) is the Japanese anime from Hajime Isayama’s manga. For one hundred years, people have lived inside three giant walls, Maria, Rose and Sina; outside there are titans, and nobody is allowed to go out. When a titan breaks the wall, Eren Yeager loses his home and joins the army with Mikasa and Armin. The grammar: can / can’t and could / couldn’t (ability now and before), and be allowed to / not allowed to for rules (soldiers are allowed to go outside), with questions Can we…? Are we allowed to…?

A note for the teacher: the trailer has titans and people in danger; nothing violent is on the lesson screens, but watch it once before class. It is in Japanese with English subtitles burned into the picture; the lesson plays the whole trailer (2:00). The five frames follow the times read from the picture (24/09), and each moment is told in our own words.

The world (I, The World): the map of the three walls. Tap the stops in order: the walls, the titans outside, Shiganshina in year 845, the Training Corps and Armin’s book. The student reads each page out loud, then answers one question about their own life (with can, could and allowed to). Show answer gives a model. No need to know the anime: the map tells the story.

The ending (VIII, The Interview): the teacher is a soldier of the Survey Corps; the student interviews them with can, could and allowed to. Then swap: the student talks about the rules of their own life. Speaking only.

ON SCREEN

The cover is an enormous stone wall around a town at dusk. I: the world (the map of the walls). II: the trailer in three steps. Before: a slider (safe inside or free outside?) and a quick pick (Eren, Mikasa or Armin?), then the student says why. Watch: What happens? and True or lie?, each with a ▶ time: tap it and the video jumps to that moment. (The subtitles are burned into the picture, so the times follow the five moments of the trailer.) After: a guard stops you at the gate and the student picks how to answer (the guard’s answer changes); then match who is who. III: five frames on the film strip. Tap one: the clip plays and stops by itself, then three True or Lie lines (no ▶: the trailer’s subtitles are in the picture); on a Lie the student says the true line. When all three are answered, the moment and its grammar open. IV: Inside the Walls, three choices about the rules of the walls. V, The Rules of the Wall: a soldier’s notebook: the student reads it out loud, taps can, could and allowed to, then finishes the rule. The rule stays in the corner on the next pages. VI, The Three Walls: the map of the walls with six gates. For each sentence the student says it out loud, then taps the card and the right box: now (can / can’t), before (could / couldn’t) or a rule (allowed to). Every right box opens a gate; a wrong card goes back with one tap. VII, Your Walls: four cards (work or school, as a child, on a plane, in your city); the student opens one, answers out loud and taps the counter for each sentence. VIII: the interview.

THE COMMON ERRORS

People don’t can, can’t to go, are allow to, children don’t allowed, aren’t allowed climb, as a child, he can’t (couldn’t), couldn’t fought, cans, can fights, We are allowed to…?, Do we allowed…?, didn’t allowed.

INSIDE THE WALLS (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

Right after Scene by Scene, three hard choices about life inside the walls: what Armin should do with his forbidden book, who should lead the three friends, and which rule the student changes as king for one year. Nothing about titans or fighting: only the rules and the choices. There is no right answer: let the student choose, wait five seconds in silence, then use the PUSH line (only you see it) to bring it to their own life. The starters use can / could / allowed to: recast mistakes quietly (don’t can, are allow to), and at the end ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves. About six minutes.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Out loud: I The World (the map of the walls: five stops, each with one question about their life), IV Inside the Walls, VI The Three Walls, VII Your Walls and VIII The Interview.
  • Listen: II the trailer and III Scene by Scene.
  • Focus: V The Rules of the Wall.
  • Write: the homework (the lesson ends speaking).
