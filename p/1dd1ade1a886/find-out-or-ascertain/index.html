Find Out or Ascertain? · C1 Unit 9 Lesson

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: a new C1 lesson on choosing between phrasal verbs (find out, look into, set up, carry on, put up with, come up with, go up, point out, deal with, turn down) and their formal one-word partners (ascertain, investigate, establish, continue, tolerate, devise, increase, indicate, address, reject).

The story: Hotel Alma (invented) has noise complaints. The manager talks to the team (spoken) and then writes the incident report (formal).

About 49 min: I · The Meeting and the Report (10) · II · Spoken or Written? (9) · III · Bingo (8) · IV · Unpopular Opinions (7) · V · Find the Mistakes (7) · VI · The Hot Seat (8).

Open with: “Have you ever complained in a hotel or a restaurant? What happened?”

Brazilians often have the opposite problem from native learners: formal Latin verbs feel easy (they look like Portuguese), phrasal verbs feel hard. So they sound robotic in conversation. Today: phrasal verbs for people, one-word verbs for documents. The report with mistakes trains the formal direction; the hot seat trains the spoken one.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

find out / ascertain · look into / investigate · set up / establish · put up with / tolerate · come up with / devise · go up / increase, turn down / reject

THE MEETING AND THE REPORT

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

SPOKEN OR WRITTEN?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

BINGO

A 3×3 card of quick tasks. The student picks a square and does it out loud; tap Done. Three in a line (across, down or diagonal) is a BINGO. Keep going for a second line if there is time.

FIND THE MISTAKES

A short note with mistakes. The student finds a mistake and SAYS the right form first; then tap it to show the correction and the reason. At the end, the student reads the whole note correctly.

THE HOT SEAT

The hot seat: now the STUDENT asks and you answer, as yourself, short. Each card says what to ask about; Help me ask shows a model question. Encourage reactions and one follow-up.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

I will ascertain (to a friend), the team will look into the stuff (in a report), put up the noise (missing with), came up a great idea (missing with), I will investigate my keys (overformal), look on it (for look into it). C1: one correction per answer is enough.

UNPOPULAR OPINIONS (IV) AND THE SURPRISE QUESTION

In the middle of the lesson, your student gives a number from 1 to 5 to four unpopular opinions (noisy guests, people who talk like documents, turning down a job, bad reviews) and defends it in spoken English, with phrasal verbs. There is no right answer. Wait five seconds in silence, then take the opposite number and use the PUSH line (only you see it). Recast quietly when they use a stiff verb in conversation (“investigate” → “look into”); at the end, ask them to fix one or two sentences themselves.

The Surprise question button (bottom right, only you see it) opens one personal question at any moment. Use it when the lesson has been only exercises for a while.

WOLF IN THIS LESSON

  • Listen: I · The Meeting and the Report.
  • Focus: II · Spoken or Written? and III · Bingo and V · Find the Mistakes.
  • Out loud: IV · Unpopular Opinions and VI · The Hot Seat.
