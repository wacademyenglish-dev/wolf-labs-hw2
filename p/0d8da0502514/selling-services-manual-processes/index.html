There Are a Lot of Manual Processes · Selling Services · Lesson 2

PREPARE IN 1 MINUTE

What this is: the review of Unit 2 (there is · there are). No new grammar. The student pulls everything from memory.

The story: Leo reads the CRM notes about Brava Foods (fictional), prepares the call with his manager, and the student writes an x-ray of a real client (no secret names needed) or of Brava.

About 57 min: I · Your Week (6) · II · The Client Notes (8) · III · Prepare with Your Manager (8) · IV · There Is or There Are? (10) · V · Is or Are? (7) · VI · You Choose (6) · VII · Your Client X-Ray (9) · VIII · I Can (3).

Open with: “How many meetings are there in your week?”

Brazilians say “tem” for existence and translate it as “have” (“have two ERPs”), and they say “there is three systems”. Look at the word AFTER: one → is, two or more → are. Accept “there’s” in speech. If the student uses a real client, numbers can be approximate.

THE LANGUAGE OF THIS LESSON

There is a … · There’s a … · There are some / many / a lot of … · There isn’t a … · There aren’t any … · Is there …? Are there …? How many … are there?

YOUR WEEK

Small talk: personal questions with the easier version a tap away. After each answer, wait: the student asks “And you?”.

THE CLIENT NOTES

Read: the student reads the text out loud. First one question before reading, then questions about the MEANING. Only then Look closer: let the student find the pattern, and tap Show the pattern to highlight it.

PREPARE WITH YOUR MANAGER

A short audio dialogue (American voices). BEFORE: one question. LISTEN: play it once without stopping (0.75× is fine for A1), then the questions; Show answer after the student tries. The transcript opens only after the audio ends: read it together and point to the highlighted words.

THERE IS OR THERE ARE?

Notice: two sentences side by side. The student explains the difference in their own words (Portuguese is fine here). Then Show answer. After the pairs come the rule cards and the traps: the student says the right form before you tap.

IS OR ARE?

Listen or read, then choose one option. The colors show right and wrong, and one line says why. Ask the student WHY before you move on.

YOU CHOOSE

Would you rather: the student taps A or B and says why, with because. Then you answer too, and agree or disagree.

YOUR CLIENT X-RAY

The writing card: short lines with the model on the side. One correction per line is enough.

I CAN

The student reads each “I can” line out loud and taps the ones they can do. You confirm and stamp the unit. Lines left open go to the homework, and to your next class. Next class: bring one real client x-ray (no confidential numbers).

THE TECH WORDS · SÓ PARA O PROFESSOR

O aluno vende serviços B2B. Você não precisa ser especialista; precisa saber do que ele está falando. Não ensine estes termos: deixe que ele explique o trabalho dele (é conversa real).

  • BPO (Business Process Outsourcing): Terceirização de processos: a empresa contratada EXECUTA uma rotina do cliente (folha de pagamento, contabilidade, fiscal, contas a pagar). O cliente para de fazer aquilo dentro de casa.
  • BTO (Business Transformation Outsourcing): Terceirização com transformação: além de executar, a contratada MUDA o processo (tecnologia, automação, integração de sistemas). Vende-se como “melhorar”, não só “fazer por você”.
  • ERP: Sistema de gestão que integra finanças, compras, estoque, RH etc. Exemplos (marcas): SAP, Oracle, TOTVS. Um BPO/BTO não vende o ERP: trabalha COM ele ou o integra.
  • Head office · factory: Matriz (sede) · fábrica/planta.
  • Accounts payable · accounts receivable: Contas a pagar · contas a receber.
  • Manual processes: Processos manuais (planilhas, digitação, conferência à mão): a “dor” que o BPO resolve.
  • Spreadsheet: Planilha (Excel).
  • CRM: Sistema onde o vendedor registra clientes, contatos e notas (ex.: as “client notes” da cena II).
  • Not connected / integrated: Sistemas que não “conversam”: dados passam de um para outro à mão.

THE MISTAKES TO LISTEN FOR

There is three systems (There are three systems), Have two ERPs (There are two ERPs), There don’t have a system (There isn’t a system), There is many problems (There are many problems). A1+: one correction per answer is enough.
